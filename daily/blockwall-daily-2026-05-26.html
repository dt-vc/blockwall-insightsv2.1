UK sanctions Huobi and ruble stablecoin issuer in crackdown on Russia crypto networks

CoinDesk • Regulation • Score: 8/10

The United Kingdom has introduced its first banking‑style sanctions aimed at cryptocurrency exchanges. Eighteen entities are targeted, among them the exchange Huobi (also known as HTX) and a stablecoin issuer linked to Kyrgyzstan. Regulators say the sanctions are intended to disrupt financial channels that support Russia’s war financing. Under Regulation 17A UK financial institutions must freeze the assets of the listed firms and monitor related transactions to prevent use of Russia’s illicit crypto infrastructure.

Michael Saylor’s Strategy repurchases $1.5 billion in convertible debt

CoinDesk • Enterprise • Score: 8/10

MicroStrategy, led by CEO Michael Saylor, repurchased $1.5 billion of its 0% convertible senior notes. The company paid $1.38 billion in cash, reducing its total outstanding debt to $6.7 billion. The repurchase lowered its cash balance to $871 million. The market responded positively, with MSTR shares rising about 2% in pre‑market trading, signaling investor approval of the debt reduction strategy.

Coinbase (COIN) news: Base launches AI tool that lets ChatGPT manage crypto wallets and DeFi apps

CoinDesk • Layer2 • Score: 7/10

Coinbase’s Ethereum Layer‑2 network Base has launched Base MCP, an AI integration that allows users to manage crypto wallets and DeFi applications through conversational prompts. The system links Base accounts to AI agents such as ChatGPT, Claude and Cursor via the Model Context Protocol. Users can initiate fund transfers, execute token swaps and interact with DeFi protocols without using a traditional graphical user interface. This development could streamline on‑chain operations for institutional investors by reducing friction and enabling programmatic control through familiar AI interfaces.

Tom Lee's Bitmine (BMNR) bought $237 million worth of ether (ETH) last week

CoinDesk • Investment • Score: 7/10

Bitmine Immersion, chaired by Tom Lee, purchased 111,942 ether for roughly $237 million, bringing its total holdings to about 5.4 million ether. This stake represents approximately 4.5 percent of the total ether supply. The firm has stated a target of controlling 5 percent of Ethereum’s circulating supply by 2026. With its current holdings, Bitmine Immersion is projected to generate roughly $276 million in annualized staking revenue, a metric that may be of interest to institutional investors monitoring on‑chain yield opportunities.

Spain joins growing list of countries shutting out Polymarket and Kalshi

CoinDesk • Regulation • Score: 7/10

Spain's gambling regulator has ordered internet service providers to block access to the prediction‑market platforms Polymarket and Kalshi because they lack the required gambling licences. This action places Spain alongside other jurisdictions that have recently targeted the same platforms, reflecting a broader regulatory push against unlicensed prediction markets. Institutional investors should monitor the evolving legal landscape as further restrictions could affect the availability and compliance requirements of these platforms in European markets.

Circle Internet Group (CRCL): The Start of Full Vertical Integration

StablR freezes USDR and EURR after attacker mints $13.5 million in unbacked tokens

CoinDesk • Security • Score: 6/10

StablR, a European stablecoin issuer, froze its USDR and EURR tokens after a cyberattack. The breach exploited a one‑of‑three multisig wallet and allowed attackers to mint $13.5 million of unbacked stablecoins while extracting $2.8 million in profit. The unauthorized minting left the tokens under‑collateralised and forced StablR to halt both minting and redemption operations. Regulators and security firms have launched investigations, underscoring the systemic risk such incidents pose to stablecoin ecosystems.
