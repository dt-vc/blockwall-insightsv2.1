Charles Schwab begins rollout of spot BTC, ETH trading for U.S. retail customers

Charles Schwab has started rolling out its Schwab Crypto platform that enables U.S. retail customers to trade spot Bitcoin and Ether directly. The service gives Schwab’s roughly 35 million clients direct access to the two largest crypto assets without the need for a separate exchange. This rollout could increase retail participation in crypto markets and support broader mainstream adoption, which may be relevant for institutional investors monitoring demand and liquidity trends.

Telecom giant KDDI to acquire 14.9% stake in Coincheck Group in $65 million deal

CoinDesk • Investment • Score: 7/10

Japanese telecom giant KDDI is buying a 14.9% equity stake in crypto exchange operator Coincheck Group for $65 million, acquiring 28.5 million newly issued shares. The transaction includes a business alliance that will allow revenue sharing and customer referrals, enabling KDDI to expand crypto access through its consumer channels. In addition, KDDI will receive the right to nominate a member to Coincheck’s board, giving it influence over the exchange’s strategic direction.

RWA news: Animoca-backed NUVA brings Figure's $19 billion of tokenized assets to Ethereum

CoinDesk • Tokenization • Score: 7/10

Animoca Brands and Nuva Labs have launched NUVA, an Ethereum‑based marketplace for tokenized real‑world assets. The platform will bring roughly $19 billion of assets from Figure Technologies onto decentralized finance. It debuts with a Treasury‑linked yield vault and a token backed by an $18.4 billion home‑equity loan portfolio. The offering expands collateral and investment options for retail participants and signals growing institutional interest in real‑world asset tokenization.

Bitcoin's available supply is shrinking as long-term holding hits record 4 million BTC

CoinDesk • Bitcoin • Score: 6/10

Bitcoin's available supply is decreasing as long‑term holders now control nearly 4 million BTC. This represents a 300 % increase in conviction‑buyer balances since late 2025. The concentration of coins off‑exchange is tightening liquid supply on major trading venues. UK parliament to probe Nigel Farage’s $6.8 million donation from crypto billionaire

CoinDesk • Regulation • Score: 6/10

The UK parliamentary standards watchdog has launched a formal investigation into Reform UK leader Nigel Farage for failing to declare a donation of £5 million (approximately $6.8 million) from crypto billionaire Christopher Harborne. Harborne is known for his stake in the stablecoin issuer Tether, linking the case to broader scrutiny of crypto‑related political financing. A finding of a breach of the parliamentary code of conduct could result in Farage’s suspension from the House of Commons, underscoring regulatory risk for political actors receiving crypto‑industry funds.

Joe Lubin's Consensys has delayed its potential IPO until fall

CoinDesk • Enterprise • Score: 6/10

Consensys, the Ethereum infrastructure firm founded by Joe Lubin, has postponed its planned U.S. initial public offering until the fall, citing weak market conditions. The company had been targeting a draft S‑1 filing with the SEC by the end of February 2026. Consensys previously raised $450 million in a 2022 Series D financing round. The delay aligns with recent postponements by other crypto companies such as Kraken and Ledger, underscoring the sector’s sensitivity to market volatility.

Coinbase (COIN) backs Hyperliquid (HYPE) stablecoin push as DeFi trading volumes climb

CoinDesk • Stablecoins • Score: 6/10

Coinbase will serve as the official treasury deployer of USDC on Hyperliquid’s DeFi trading platform, giving it the right to acquire assets tied to Hyperliquid’s native stablecoin USDH. The agreement also includes Circle expanding USDC usage on Hyperliquid and committing to stake 500,000 HYPE tokens. By linking a major custodial exchange’s treasury to a decentralized stablecoin, the partnership signals increased institutional confidence in on‑chain liquidity provision. The move comes as DeFi trading volumes are climbing, highlighting the strategic importance of stablecoin integration for large‑scale investors.

Unpacking Aave: Quantifying the Leverage in DeFi

Galaxy • DeFi • Score: 6/10

Galaxy Research has examined Aave’s loan book following the recent $290 million KelpDAO exploit. The exploit created $123 million of bad debt on Aave and drove utilization on its core lending pools close to 100 percent. The analysis underscores a systemic leverage risk that could affect broader DeFi lending stability. It also references the DeFi United initiative’s intention to raise more than $300 million to address the shortfall.
