Step Finance shuts down following $40 million security breach

Brian Danga • Security • Score: 7/10

Step Finance, a Solana-based DeFi platform, announced its immediate shutdown of core operations, SolanaFloor, and Remora Markets after a January 31, 2026, hack compromised executive devices and drained nearly $40 million from its treasury wallets. The breach exploited weak endpoint security rather than smart contract vulnerabilities, enabling attackers to unstake and transfer assets like 261,854 SOL, with only about $4.7 million recovered via Solana's Token22 protections and partners. This incident triggered a 97% collapse in the STEP token value, eroded investor confidence, and blocked funding or acquisition paths despite exhaustive efforts. Institutional investors should note the heightened risks of centralized treasury controls and human-operator weaknesses in crypto protocols, alongside planned STEP token buybacks and 1:1 Remora redemptions.
