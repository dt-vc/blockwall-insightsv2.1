Trump Iran Deal Lifts Crypto Markets By $75 Billion

A newly announced Iran deal involving former President Donald Trump has lifted cryptocurrency market valuations by roughly $75 billion. The surge has sparked broad bullish sentiment across the sector as investors react to the perceived reduction in geopolitical risk. The valuation jump highlights how macro‑political developments can materially affect crypto asset pricing, a factor institutional investors monitor closely.

Crypto and the Fed: State of Crypto

CoinDesk • Regulation • Score: 6/10

President Donald Trump signed executive orders aimed at integrating digital assets with existing payment networks and tightening Bank Secrecy Act rules. The Federal Reserve concurrently released an updated proposal for a “skinny” master account that would allow crypto firms direct access to payment rails. Together these actions suggest a shift toward reducing regulatory obstacles for digital‑asset participation in the U.S. payments infrastructure. Institutional investors should monitor the evolving policy landscape as it could affect liquidity, compliance costs and market access for crypto‑related services.

The Vast Majority of Crypto Wrench Attacks Happen in France: Report

Cointelegraph • Security • Score: 6/10

A new report finds that the vast majority of crypto wrench physical attacks in 2026 are concentrated in France. The incidents have generated losses exceeding $101 million and a data breach that exposed personal information of more than 270,000 customers worldwide. The concentration of attacks in a single jurisdiction raises operational risk for institutions with exposure to French crypto assets or service providers. Experts recommend custodial security, decoy wallets and a low profile approach to mitigate the threat.

AI is speeding up the quantum threat to crypto, security experts warn

CoinDesk • Security • Score: 5/10

Security experts warn that advances in artificial intelligence are accelerating quantum computing research, which could enable attacks on the cryptographic primitives that underpin blockchain networks. The convergence of AI and quantum capabilities shortens the timeline for quantum‑capable adversaries to threaten the confidentiality and integrity of crypto assets. As a result, industry stakeholders are being urged to adopt post‑quantum cryptographic schemes to protect decentralized finance and other blockchain applications.

Why is Bitcoin Down Despite Pro-Crypto Kevin Warsh Becoming Fed Chair?

Cointelegraph • Bitcoin • Score: 5/10

Bitcoin fell sharply as the U.S. two‑year Treasury yield moved above the Federal Reserve’s target range. The decline occurred despite the appointment of Kevin Warsh, a candidate known for his pro‑crypto stance, as Fed chair. Analysts attribute the price pressure to higher yields and market expectations of a more hawkish monetary policy that outweigh Warsh’s crypto‑friendly rhetoric. Historical patterns show that transitions in Fed leadership have often been negative for Bitcoin, reinforcing the current downside risk for institutional investors.

Blockchain Researcher Defends Ethereum Foundation, Says It’s ‘Exactly’ Doing Its Job

Cointelegraph • Ethereum • Score: 5/10

Blockchain researcher William Mougayar defended the Ethereum Foundation, stating that its emphasis on protocol hardening and funding research aligns with its mandate. The foundation recently sold roughly $47 million of ETH in OTC transactions and unstaked about $90 million of ETH, moves that have attracted criticism from parts of the community. Despite a modest price increase on the day, ETH remains well below its all‑time high, underscoring the market’s sensitivity to the foundation’s asset‑management decisions.

Ruble-pegged stablecoin A7A5 says it has a business even if sanctions end

CoinDesk • Stablecoins • Score: 4/10

A7A5 is a stablecoin pegged to the Russian ruble and linked to the sanctioned bank Promsvyazbank. The token is being positioned as a long‑term settlement instrument that could remain viable even if Western sanctions on the bank are lifted. According to executive Oleg Ogienko, A7A5 aims to compete by providing fast cross‑border payments that do not rely on the US dollar and by delivering yields tied to Russian interest rates. The project acknowledges ongoing regulatory and infrastructure challenges but sees a niche for institutions seeking non‑dollar settlement options in the Russian market.

CFTC Officials Who Questioned Prediction Markets Were Suspended: NYT

Cointelegraph • Regulation • Score: 5/10

The U.S. Commodity Futures Trading Commission has suspended staff members who publicly questioned the legitimacy of crypto prediction markets, as reported by the New York Times. The officials were responsible for overseeing derivatives and related market activities, including emerging digital‑asset platforms. The suspension reflects a broader increase in regulatory scrutiny of prediction‑market operators that could affect how these platforms are structured and offered. Institutional investors should monitor the CFTC’s actions because they may signal future compliance requirements and impact the risk profile of crypto‑related strategies.
