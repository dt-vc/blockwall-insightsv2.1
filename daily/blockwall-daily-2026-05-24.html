Crypto and the Fed: State of Crypto

CoinDesk • Regulation • Score: 6/10

President Donald Trump signed executive orders aimed at integrating digital assets with existing payment networks and tightening Bank Secrecy Act rules. The Federal Reserve released an updated proposal for a “skinny” master account that could give crypto firms direct access to payment rails. Together these actions suggest a potential easing of regulatory barriers, which could expand crypto participation in the U.S. payments system and attract institutional investors seeking new liquidity channels.

AI is speeding up the quantum threat to crypto, security experts warn

CoinDesk • Security • Score: 5/10

Security experts warn that AI is accelerating quantum computing research, which could enable quantum‑capable attacks on blockchain encryption sooner than anticipated. The convergence of AI and quantum advances creates an emerging arms race that threatens the cryptographic foundations of crypto networks. Institutional investors are being urged to consider post‑quantum cryptographic solutions to protect assets as the risk of quantum attacks becomes more immediate.

Coinbase does not fear competition from Wall Street, says exchange executive

CoinDesk • Regulation • Score: 4/10

Coinbase executive Katie Harries said the exchange does not view Wall Street firms as a competitive threat. She called on regulators to implement sensible, coordinated crypto regulation. Harries also pointed to the upcoming Stand With Crypto events, scheduled in more than 500 locations worldwide and backed by a community of 3.7 million members advocating for crypto‑friendly policies.

Ruble-pegged stablecoin A7A5 says it has a business even if sanctions end

CoinDesk • Stablecoins • Score: 4/10

A7A5 is a stablecoin pegged to the Russian ruble and tied to the sanctioned bank Promsvyazbank. The token is being positioned as a long‑term settlement instrument that could remain useful even if Western sanctions on the bank are lifted. According to executive Oleg Ogienko, its competitiveness relies on fast non‑dollar cross‑border payments and yields linked to Russian interest rates, although it still faces regulatory and infrastructure challenges.
