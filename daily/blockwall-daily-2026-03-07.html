Florida Senate Approves First Stablecoin Bill, Awaits DeSantis’ Signature

News • Stablecoins • Score: 6/10

The Florida Senate unanimously approved a bill with a 37-0 vote, establishing a licensing framework for payment stablecoin issuers, extending anti-money laundering rules to their transactions, and prohibiting unlicensed operations within the state. This legislation clarifies that certain fully backed payment stablecoins are not securities under Florida law, providing regulatory clarity that contrasts with fragmented federal oversight and fosters a compliant environment for institutional stablecoin activities. For institutional investors, the framework enhances consumer protections and oversight by the Florida Office of Financial Regulation, potentially reducing compliance risks and enabling smoother integration of stablecoins into payment systems once Governor Ron DeSantis signs the bill into law. Implementation will follow phased rulemaking, allowing time for issuers to adapt while supporting stablecoin innovation in a major U.S. financial hub.
