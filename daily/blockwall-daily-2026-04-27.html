Ethereum Backers Commit 30,000 ETH to rsETH Recovery After Exploit

Cointelegraph • DeFi • Score: 9/10

An exploit on April 18 drained roughly 116,500 rsETH, valued at about $290 million, from a LayerZero‑based bridge operated by Kelp DAO, creating up to $200 million of bad debt on Aave. In response, a coalition of DeFi projects known as DeFi United, including Lido, EtherFi, Ethena, Mantle, Frax and others, pledged 30,000 ETH to support the recovery of the compromised rsETH. Circle’s venture arm also purchased AAVE tokens to help stabilize the broader ecosystem after the incident. The coordinated capital injection aims to protect Ethereum‑based liquidity providers and mitigate systemic risk across interconnected DeFi protocols.

Justin Sun announces $20m boost for Aave as he calls on lender to expand to his blockchain

DL News • DeFi • Score: 7/10

Justin Sun has pledged a $20 million USDT liquidity injection to Aave’s Core V3 market. He simultaneously urged the lender to launch on the Tron blockchain. Tron, the fifth‑largest DeFi blockchain, holds about $5.3 billion in deposits and is actively courting high‑value protocols. The move follows a large hack of Aave earlier this year and underscores the importance of bolstering liquidity and expanding cross‑chain access for institutional participants.

Israeli Regulators Approve Shekel-Pegged Stablecoin

Cointelegraph • Stablecoins • Score: 6/10

Israeli financial regulators have approved a stablecoin that is pegged to the Israeli shekel. The approval marks the first official endorsement of a fiat‑backed crypto token in Israel and signals regulatory clarity for digital asset projects. Institutional investors can view the development as a catalyst for increased crypto adoption and the launch of related financial services within the Israeli market.

French police record 135 crypto kidnapping cases since 2023 as crime wave swells

DL News • Security • Score: 6/10

French police have recorded 135 crypto‑related kidnapping cases since 2023, indicating a rapid increase in this type of crime. The incidents typically involve the extortion of Bitcoin and other altcoins, with perpetrators often coordinating the attacks through the Telegram messaging platform. Law‑enforcement actions have resulted in a series of arrests and the seizure of assets valued at several hundred thousand dollars. The trend underscores heightened operational risk for institutional investors with exposure to digital assets and highlights the need for robust security and compliance measures.

Tennessee Crypto Kiosk Ban Set to Go into Effect July 1

Cointelegraph • Regulation • Score: 5/10

Tennessee will prohibit the operation of crypto kiosks and ATMs beginning July 1. The statewide ban covers more than 570 devices, including those run by Bitcoin Depot and CoinFlip. Regulators cite a surge in scam activity as a primary driver of the restriction. Federal data from the FBI indicate that crypto‑ATM fraud has generated $389 million in losses, underscoring the risk profile for institutional exposure.

Bernstein Says IREN Pivot to AI Cloud Could Drive $3.7B Revenue

Cointelegraph • Infrastructure • Score: 5/10

Banks are fighting a ‘sideshow’ as Clarity Act stalls. Will it matter?

DL News • Regulation • Score: 5/10

The Senate Banking Committee has postponed the markup of the Clarity Act, a proposal intended to close a loophole that currently permits yield generation on stablecoins. The delay pushes formal debate on the measure into May, extending the period of regulatory uncertainty for banks and crypto firms. Pro‑stablecoin advocates characterize the banks’ resistance as a peripheral “sideshow” that will not alter the broader market trajectory. A White House analysis, however, estimates that any changes to bank lending resulting from the bill would be modest, suggesting limited immediate impact on institutional credit flows.

Kbank Tests Ripple Wallet For Remittances In South Korea

Cointelegraph • Payments • Score: 5/10

South Korean internet‑only bank Kbank has formed a strategic partnership with Ripple to pilot blockchain‑based overseas remittances. The trial uses Ripple’s global network to test faster cheaper more transparent cross‑border payments to destinations such as the UAE and Thailand. The initiative comes as South Korea is considering stablecoin regulations, highlighting the potential for digital‑asset infrastructure in the region.
