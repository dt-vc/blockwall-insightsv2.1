Justin Sun announces $20m boost for Aave as he calls on lender to expand to his blockchain

DL News • DeFi • Score: 7/10

Justin Sun has pledged a $20 million USDT liquidity injection to Aave’s Core V3 market. He also urged Aave to launch on the Tron blockchain. Tron is the fifth‑largest DeFi blockchain and currently holds about $5.3 billion in deposits. The funding and outreach aim to attract high‑value protocols to Tron after Aave experienced a large hack earlier this year.

Why Wall Street is moving onchain - a16z crypto

a16z • Tokenization • Score: 7/10

Wall Street institutions are transitioning to on‑chain infrastructure, highlighted by the DTCC receiving SEC clearance to tokenize assets and the NYSE launching a 24/7 on‑chain trading platform for equities and ETFs. This development is described as the most significant upgrade to capital‑market infrastructure since the advent of electronic trading. The on‑chain shift is expected to deliver faster settlement, enable fractional ownership, and expand market participation for institutional investors.

Israeli Regulators Approve Shekel-Pegged Stablecoin

Cointelegraph • Stablecoins • Score: 6/10

Israeli financial regulators have approved a stablecoin that is pegged to the Israeli shekel. This marks the first official endorsement of a fiat‑backed crypto token in Israel, providing regulatory clarity for the sector. The approval is expected to accelerate domestic crypto adoption and enable the development of related financial services, which may be of interest to institutional investors seeking exposure to regulated digital assets.

French police record 135 crypto kidnapping cases since 2023 as crime wave swells

DL News • Security • Score: 6/10

French police have documented 135 kidnapping cases linked to cryptocurrency since 2023. The incidents have accelerated, prompting a noticeable increase in arrests. Perpetrators typically demand Bitcoin and other altcoins and use Telegram to coordinate the extortion. Authorities have seized assets worth hundreds of thousands of dollars, underscoring the growing regulatory and security challenges for institutional crypto exposure.

Curve founder pitches market-based fix for $700K bad debt in contrast to Aave bailout

CoinDesk • DeFi • Score: 5/10

Curve founder Michael Egorov has proposed a market‑based remedy to recover roughly $700,000 of bad debt on the LlamaLend platform. His plan would tokenise the distressed claims and launch a dedicated Curve pool, giving trapped lenders an exit route and allowing external traders to price the claims. The approach stands in contrast to the industry‑wide bailout that rescued Aave’s $230 million shortfall, illustrating a divergent strategy for handling protocol losses.

Banks are fighting a ‘sideshow’ as Clarity Act stalls. Will it matter?

DL News • Regulation • Score: 5/10

The Senate Banking Committee has postponed the markup of the Clarity Act, a legislative proposal intended to close a loophole that currently permits yield generation on stablecoins. The delay pushes the committee’s consideration of the bill into May, extending the period of regulatory uncertainty for the stablecoin market. Pro‑stablecoin advocates characterize the banks’ resistance as a peripheral issue that will not shape the ultimate outcome of the legislation. A White House analysis, however, indicates that any changes to the act are likely to have only a modest effect on overall bank lending activity.

결제 시장 3.0: AI 에이전트 결제 시장 분석

Tiger Research • Payments • Score: 5/10

Tiger Research’s report identifies a shift toward AI‑agent driven commerce, which it labels “Payment Market 3.0.” The analysis highlights a competitive race among technology firms to define payment standards for transactions initiated by autonomous agents. Google is actively shaping the ecosystem by developing the UCP and AP2 protocols that combine product discovery with transaction authorization. The firm’s strategy is aimed at securing a share of future revenue that will be generated from AI‑mediated purchases.

Payments 3.0 The AI Agent Payments Market

Tiger Research • Payments • Score: 5/10

The Tiger Research report identifies a new Payments 3.0 market built around AI‑driven payment agents. In 2025 eight agent‑payment protocols were introduced by major technology companies, card networks and cryptocurrency exchanges, signaling broad industry participation. The analysis separates two primary use cases: General Agentic Commerce, where consumers delegate purchasing decisions to AI agents, and a longer‑term Pay‑per‑call model that enables direct agent‑to‑agent transactions, a development that could reshape transaction flows for institutional investors.
