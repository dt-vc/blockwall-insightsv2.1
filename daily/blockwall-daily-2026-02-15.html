South Korea’s biggest securities firm snaps up crypto exchange as stock market soars

Tim Alper • Investment • Score: 7/10

Mirae Asset Consulting, a subsidiary of South Korea's largest securities firm, is acquiring a 92.06% stake in Korbit, a licensed cryptocurrency exchange, for 133.5 billion won (approximately $93.82 million) through the purchase of 26.91 million shares from NXC and SK Planet. The acquisition, announced on February 13, 2026, grants Mirae Asset effective control of Korbit's regulated infrastructure, which the buyer intends to leverage for developing institutional-grade services such as security token offerings and custody solutions as regulatory frameworks evolve. This transaction represents a significant shift in South Korea's crypto market, embedding a major traditional financial institution inside the regulated virtual-asset sector and positioning Korbit to benefit from Mirae Asset's extensive network and capital reserves. The deal is structured through a non-financial affiliate to comply with Korean regulations separating financial institutions from virtual asset service providers, though regulatory approvals from the Fair Trade Commission and Financial Supervisory Service remain pending.
