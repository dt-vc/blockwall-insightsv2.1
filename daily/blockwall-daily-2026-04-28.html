🔥 Key Developments (9)

Aave-led 'DeFi United' campaign raises $300m to address Kelp DAO breach

DL News • DeFi • Score: 9/10

Aave is leading the DeFi United campaign that has raised over $300 million to reimburse victims of the Kelp DAO and LayerZero hack. The hack resulted in the loss of approximately $293 million from the Kelp DAO. Major decentralized autonomous organizations, crypto enterprises and prominent individuals have contributed large amounts of Ether and stablecoins to the recovery fund. The broad participation underscores strong community solidarity and provides a coordinated response that may be of interest to institutional investors monitoring systemic risk in the DeFi sector.

A digital shekel is here: Israel approves its first-ever regulated stablecoin

CoinDesk • Stablecoins • Score: 7/10

Israel's Capital Market Authority has approved BILS, the country's first regulated stablecoin that is pegged to the Israeli shekel. BILS is issued by Bits of Gold in partnership with the Solana blockchain, with custody services provided by Fireblocks and audit oversight from EY. The launch introduces a locally denominated digital asset and diversifies the market away from stablecoins that are tied to the U.S. dollar, offering institutional investors a regulated alternative for settlement and liquidity in the region.

Aave publishes plan to plug $246m hole left by Kelp DAO attackers

DL News • DeFi • Score: 7/10

Aave has released a detailed remediation plan to address the $246 million shortfall created by the Kelp DAO attack. The plan is coordinated with Compound and the DeFi United coalition to fully back rsETH and eliminate the outstanding bad debt. Execution will involve converting pledged ETH into rsETH and leveraging governance votes to adjust oracle pricing for a series of controlled liquidations. Successful implementation is intended to recover the stolen assets and restore confidence for users and institutional participants in the protocol’s risk management framework.

Bitmine buys $294m more Ethereum ahead of Clarity Act ‘markup in May’

DL News • Ethereum • Score: 6/10

Bitmine increased its Ethereum position by $294 million, raising its total exposure to about $12 billion, which represents roughly 4.2 % of the total Ether supply. The timing coincides with the U.S. Senate’s upcoming markup of the Clarity Act, a legislative step toward establishing a federal digital‑asset framework. Institutional backers including ARK, Founders Fund, Pantera, Kraken, Galaxy Digital and Bill Miller support Bitmine’s strategy. The accumulation signals confidence among large investors that regulatory clarity could unlock further upside for Ether.

Canaan Lands New Tether Order as Mining Shifts to Modular Infrastructure

Cointelegraph • Infrastructure • Score: 5/10

Canaan has secured a new order from Tether for Bitcoin mining hardware. The order comes as Tether moves its mining operations to a modular infrastructure model. This shift reflects a broader trend of stablecoin issuers seeking dedicated mining capacity to support their token ecosystems. The deal underscores institutional confidence in Canaan’s modular solutions and the growing demand for stablecoin‑backed mining resources.

Bitcoin mining meltdown? Why IREN’s real future is in AI, according to Bernstein

IREN, previously known for its Bitcoin mining operations, is redirecting its core business toward AI cloud infrastructure. Bernstein continues to assign an outperform rating to IREN even though it has lowered its price target. Solana Clients Introduce Post-Quantum Solution Falcon

Cointelegraph • Infrastructure • Score: 5/10

Solana validator clients Anza and Firedancer have released test implementations of the Falcon post‑quantum signature scheme. Falcon‑512 is the smallest NIST‑approved post‑quantum signature and can be verified off‑chain, which helps preserve Solana’s high‑throughput performance. By integrating this scheme, the Solana network is preparing for potential future quantum computing threats. The initiative signals a proactive security hardening effort within the Solana ecosystem, which is relevant for institutional investors monitoring protocol resilience.

Acting US AG Says Devs Will No Longer Be Charged Unless they Knowingly Help Third Parties Commit Crimes

Cointelegraph • Regulation • Score: 5/10

The Acting United States Attorney General stated that cryptocurrency developers will be prosecuted only when they knowingly aid third parties in illegal activity. This clarification narrows the Department of Justice’s focus to intentional assistance rather than passive involvement in blockchain projects. Institutional investors can interpret the guidance as a reduction in liability risk for development teams, potentially encouraging broader participation in regulated digital‑asset ecosystems.

These Three Bitcoin Charts Say BTC Price Set for Recovery to $82,000

Cointelegraph • Bitcoin • Score: 5/10

Bitcoin has rebounded more than 28% from its February low and is currently trading near a historically supportive price zone. Technical analysis suggests that this zone could catalyze an additional 8‑10% rally, which would move the cryptocurrency toward the $82,000 level referenced in the charts. The combination of technical support and increased liquidity is being watched by institutional investors as a potential catalyst for reallocating exposure to Bitcoin in diversified portfolios.
