🔥 Key Developments (11)

Aave restores ether borrowing limits after $230 million exploit

CoinDesk • DeFi • Score: 9/10

Aave, a major decentralized finance lending platform, has lifted emergency borrowing limits on wrapped ether (WETH) across six of its V3 deployments. The change restores the pre‑incident loan‑to‑value ratios that were reduced after a large rsETH exploit that siphoned roughly $230 million in ETH. The move follows the recovery of more than 95 percent of the unbacked rsETH, with the remaining shortfall to be covered by the DeFi United coalition.

Crypto security is turning into an AI arms race, Elliptic CEO warns

CoinDesk • Security • Score: 9/10

Elliptic CEO Simone Maini says the rapid growth of AI‑driven financial activity is straining traditional crypto compliance teams. She characterizes the situation as an emerging arms race in which automated tools are required to keep pace with sophisticated threats. To meet this challenge Elliptic has secured a $120 million funding round that includes strategic investors such as Nasdaq and Deutsche Bank. The capital will be used to build AI‑powered monitoring solutions that aim to protect institutional investors from illicit activity across blockchain networks.

클래리티 법안, 무슨 비즈니스를 해야 하는가?

Tiger Research • Regulation • Score: 9/10

The U.S. Senate Banking Committee approved the CLARITY Act, which prohibits interest on passive stablecoin holdings but permits rewards tied to user activity. The legislation was crafted with bipartisan support and the endorsement of the Trump administration, and it is slated for enactment by July. Institutional investors should anticipate that crypto platforms will need to redesign yield products and may see the emergence of token‑banking services that comply with the new rules.

SEC to propose tokenized stock framework as Wall Street efforts deepen: Bloomberg

CoinDesk • Regulation • Score: 7/10

The U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission is drafting an “innovation exemption” that would let trading platforms list tokenized versions of publicly traded equities under a lighter regulatory regime. The exemption is intended to provide a clearer compliance pathway for digital securities while preserving investor protections. Major market participants including the Depository Trust & Clearing Corporation, Nasdaq and the New York Stock Exchange are already constructing the technology and settlement infrastructure needed for tokenized stocks and exchange‑traded funds. The SEC’s proposal would position these platforms for a potential rollout later this year, giving institutional investors a new, potentially more efficient channel for equity exposure and liquidity management.

Hyperliquid's USDC deal could supercharge HYPE, pressure Circle, Coinbase margins, analysts say

CoinDesk • DeFi • Score: 7/10

Hyperliquid has entered a revenue‑share agreement with Circle and Coinbase that allows the exchange to retain up to 90 percent of the income generated from USDC reserves. The arrangement could channel between $135 million and $160 million each year into Hyperliquid’s ecosystem. By designating USDC as its official Aligned Quote Asset, Hyperliquid aims to create sustained buying pressure for its native HYPE token. The structure also reduces the profit margins available to Circle and Coinbase on USDC‑related activities, a dynamic that analysts say could reshape competitive economics in the stablecoin market.

Solana is shedding its memecoin reputation as big banks move billions into its ecosystem

CoinDesk • Infrastructure • Score: 7/10

Solana is shedding its memecoin reputation as major banks and payment firms move billions of dollars of tokenized funds, stocks and ETFs onto its network. The platform’s low fees and fast settlement are attracting real‑world asset and stablecoin activity. This surge positions Solana as core infrastructure for tokenized finance and global payments, a development that matters to institutional investors.

Strategy (MSTR) news: Company purchased more than $2 billion of BTC last week

CoinDesk • Bitcoin • Score: 7/10

MicroStrategy announced it purchased 24,869 Bitcoin for approximately $2.01 billion, raising its total holdings to 843,738 coins. The transaction pushes the company’s average Bitcoin cost basis to about $75,700 per coin. Funding for the purchase came primarily from sales of the firm’s STRC preferred stock, with a smaller portion raised through common stock issuances. The buy occurred as Bitcoin price dipped modestly and MSTR shares fell roughly 2.5 % in pre‑market trading, underscoring the firm’s continued commitment to a Bitcoin‑centric treasury strategy.

Ethereum news: Bitmine (BMNR) buys the dip as Tom Lee ties ether's pullback to rising oil prices

CoinDesk • Ethereum • Score: 7/10

Bitmine Immersion, an Ethereum treasury firm, bought 71,672 ETH for roughly $157 million, raising its stake to about 4.37 % of the token’s circulating supply. The purchase represents a sharp increase in buying activity compared with the previous week. Chairman Tom Lee attributed the recent ETH price pullback to higher oil prices and described the dip as an attractive buying opportunity. The combined signal of a sizable institutional accumulation and a bullish outlook from a market commentator may indicate potential upside for Ethereum’s price, which is relevant for large‑scale investors monitoring crypto exposure.

Revolut wants to take Dogecoin (DOGE) mainstream with its new physical payment card

CoinDesk • Payments • Score: 6/10

Revolut has introduced a physical debit card branded with Dogecoin that enables holders to spend the cryptocurrency directly. The card operates on Visa and Mastercard networks, allowing transactions wherever those schemes are accepted. Users are not charged additional exchange fees when converting DOGE for fiat at the point of sale. The rollout begins in the United Kingdom and the European Union, reflecting Revolut’s broader strategy to embed crypto assets into mainstream payment infrastructure, which may be of interest to institutional investors monitoring fintech‑crypto convergence.

Ex-OpenAI whistleblower bets big on crypto miners for his $13.6 billion AI play

CoinDesk • Investment • Score: 6/10

Former OpenAI researcher Leopold Aschenbrenner has increased his disclosed equity exposure to $13.6 billion. The bulk of the portfolio is allocated to Bitcoin mining companies and to firms that provide power and data‑center capacity for AI workloads. He is simultaneously shorting major semiconductor stocks and chip‑focused exchange‑traded funds. This positioning reflects a view that AI infrastructure will rely more on mining‑related assets than on traditional chip manufacturers, a trend that could reshape exposure for institutional investors.

UK’s financial payments network is ready for tokenization, regulators say

CoinDesk • Tokenization • Score: 6/10

The Bank of England and the Financial Conduct Authority have released a roadmap that sets out how assets can be tokenised in UK wholesale markets. The consultation invites comments from market participants and other stakeholders. The same plan proposes a phased migration of the CHAPS and RTGS settlement systems to near‑continuous, 24‑hour operation by the early 2030s. Regulators say the changes aim to modernise the payments infrastructure, increase market efficiency and keep the UK competitive in the evolving digital asset ecosystem.
