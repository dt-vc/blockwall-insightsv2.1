SafeMoon CEO Given 8-Year Jail Time Over Crypto Scam

News • Security • Score: 7/10

Braden John Karony, former CEO of SafeMoon, was sentenced to 100 months in federal prison after a jury convicted him in May 2025 on charges including conspiracy to commit securities fraud, wire fraud, and money laundering. Karony misappropriated over $9 million in digital assets from SafeMoon's liquidity pool in 2021, diverting investor funds to purchase luxury properties and vehicles while publicly denying any risk of a rug pull. The SafeMoon token had reached an $8 billion market capitalization in 2021 before collapsing 98% by 2023, leaving investors including military veterans and everyday Americans with substantial losses. This conviction reflects an expanding enforcement trend against crypto executives who manipulate tokens and misappropriate funds, with comparable sentences recently imposed on FTX CEO Sam Bankman-Fried (25 years) and Celsius CEO Alex Mashinsky (12 years).
