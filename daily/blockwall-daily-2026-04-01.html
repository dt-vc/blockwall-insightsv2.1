Solana-based Drift Protocol confirms it’s under attack after $285m leaves DeFi platform

DL News • Security • Score: 9/10

Drift Protocol, a Solana‑based perpetual futures exchange, reported that it is currently under an active attack. The breach resulted in the disappearance of more than $285 million in USDC and other assets from the platform. In response, Drift suspended all deposits and withdrawals to contain further loss. The incident triggered a sharp decline in the value of its native DRIFT token, underscoring the operational and liquidity risks that institutional investors face when allocating capital to emerging DeFi protocols.

Stablecoins endanger US economy, warns Fed governor. ‘Long and painful history of private money’

DL News • Stablecoins • Score: 8/10

Federal Reserve Governor Michael Barr warned that the rapid expansion of stablecoins now exceeding $315 billion creates a systemic risk to the U.S. economy. He likened the potential fallout to the bank runs of the 19th century, emphasizing the historical vulnerability of private money. Barr noted that issuers may be tempted to pursue higher yields, a behavior that could erode confidence in stressed market conditions. Analysts anticipate that the stablecoin market could grow to between $2 trillion and $3 trillion in the coming years, underscoring the scale of the exposure for institutional investors.

U.S. BTC ETFs post first monthly inflows since October

CoinDesk • Bitcoin • Score: 6/10

U.S. spot Bitcoin exchange‑traded funds attracted $1.32 billion of net new capital in March. This represents the first positive monthly flow since October and ends a four‑month period of net outflows. Although the total assets in the ETFs have only partially recovered from a 7 percent decline, the new inflow indicates a resurgence of interest among investors. The development is being watched by institutional investors as a potential signal of renewed demand for Bitcoin exposure through regulated vehicles.

Fed’s Barr Calls for Balanced US Stablecoin Rules Under GENIUS Act

Cointelegraph • Regulation • Score: 6/10

U.S. Treasury Assistant Secretary Michael Barr is urging Congress to enact balanced regulations for stablecoins under the GENIUS Act. The GENIUS Act is designed to establish a clear regulatory framework that protects consumers. At the same time the bill seeks to preserve innovation and competitiveness in the U.S. stablecoin market. Institutional investors are watching the proposal because consistent rules could reduce legal uncertainty and support broader adoption of digital assets.

Crypto exchange Bithumb postpones IPO bid amid economic uncertainty

DL News • Enterprise • Score: 5/10

Bithumb, South Korea’s second‑largest cryptocurrency exchange, announced it will delay its planned Nasdaq initial public offering. The IPO, originally slated for 2026, has been pushed to a date after early 2028. The postponement follows recent compliance fines and operational setbacks that have heightened regulatory scrutiny. Management says the extra time will allow the firm to strengthen internal controls and target a higher valuation when market conditions improve.

S&P Tokenizes Treasury Index On Canton Network

Cointelegraph • Tokenization • Score: 6/10

S&P Global has introduced a tokenized version of its Treasury Index on the Canton Network blockchain. The token represents the same fixed‑income benchmark that tracks U.S. Treasury securities but is issued as a digital asset on a permissioned distributed ledger. By moving the index onto Canton, S&P aims to provide institutional investors with faster settlement, greater transparency and programmable access to a core market reference. The launch highlights the increasing overlap between traditional finance and crypto infrastructure as firms seek to modernize fixed‑income trading.

Meta 2026 Stablecoin Plan : Why It Partnering , Not Issuing

Cointelegraph • Stablecoins • Score: 5/10

Meta has released a roadmap indicating that its stablecoin activities will target the year 2026. Rather than creating a proprietary token, the company plans to collaborate with established stablecoin issuers. This partnership model allows Meta to use existing blockchain infrastructure and liquidity networks. By avoiding direct issuance, Meta seeks to reduce regulatory exposure while still participating in the digital payments market, a factor that may be relevant to institutional investors evaluating exposure to fintech and crypto assets.
