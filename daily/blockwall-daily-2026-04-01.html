Solana-based Drift Protocol confirms it’s under attack after $285m leaves DeFi platform

DL News • Security • Score: 9/10

Drift Protocol, a Solana‑based perpetual futures exchange, reported an active attack that resulted in the loss of more than $285 million in USDC and other assets. In response, the platform halted all deposits and withdrawals to contain further exposure. The breach triggered a sharp decline in the value of its native DRIFT token, highlighting heightened security risk for participants in Solana DeFi infrastructure.

Harvey & Legora in a Land-Grab Race for Dominance of Legal AI

Stablecoins endanger US economy, warns Fed governor. ‘Long and painful history of private money’

DL News • Stablecoins • Score: 8/10

Federal Reserve Governor Michael S. Barr warned that the rapid growth of stablecoins, which now exceeds $315 billion, creates a systemic risk to the U.S. economy. He compared the threat to the bank runs that occurred in the 19th century when private money faced runs. Barr said that issuers’ pursuit of higher yields could erode confidence during market stress. Analysts project the stablecoin market could grow to between $2 and $3 trillion in the coming years, a development that institutional investors should monitor closely.

CFTC Chair Says Agency is Ready to Oversee Entire Crypto Market

Cointelegraph • Regulation • Score: 6/10

This statement indicates that the agency may extend its regulatory authority beyond the derivatives products it currently supervises. Behnam’s remarks also point to a push for clearer regulatory guidance and closer coordination with other U.S. financial regulators. Institutional investors should monitor these developments as they could affect compliance requirements and market practices across the crypto ecosystem.

Fed’s Barr Calls for Balanced US Stablecoin Rules Under GENIUS Act

Cointelegraph • Regulation • Score: 6/10

U.S. Treasury Assistant Secretary Michael Barr is urging Congress to adopt balanced regulations for stablecoins under the proposed GENIUS Act. The GENIUS Act is designed to establish a clear, consumer‑protective regulatory framework for stablecoins operating in the United States. By combining consumer safeguards with provisions that preserve innovation, the legislation seeks to support the growth of the U.S. stablecoin market and provide certainty for institutional investors.

Meta 2026 Stablecoin Plan : Why It Partnering , Not Issuing

Cointelegraph • Stablecoins • Score: 5/10

Meta has announced a stablecoin strategy that targets implementation by 2026. Rather than creating its own token, the company will partner with established stablecoin issuers. This partnership model allows Meta to use the existing blockchain and custody infrastructure of its partners. By avoiding direct issuance, Meta seeks to sidestep the regulatory burdens that typically accompany new stablecoin launches, a factor that is relevant for institutional investors evaluating compliance risk.
