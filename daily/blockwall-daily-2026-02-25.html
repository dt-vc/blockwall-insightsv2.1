North Carolina prosecutors seize $61 million in USDT tied to 'pig butchering' scam

Timmy Shen • Security • Score: 7/10

The U.S. Attorney's Office for the Eastern District of North Carolina announced the seizure of over $61 million in USDT cryptocurrency linked to "pig butchering" investment scams, where criminals establish fake romantic relationships to gain victims' trust before directing them to fraudulent trading platforms. Law enforcement, led by Homeland Security Investigations, traced the funds to multiple cryptocurrency wallet addresses used to launder money stolen from victims who were shown fictitious high-return portfolios and then denied withdrawals or charged fake "taxes" and "fees." This seizure represents a record single recovery in crypto fraud enforcement and demonstrates law enforcement's advancing capability to track illicit cryptocurrency flows in real time through on-chain analysis. The case underscores the broader challenge facing regulators, as the $61 million recovery remains a fraction of the estimated $17 billion in global cryptocurrency fraud losses recorded in 2025.
