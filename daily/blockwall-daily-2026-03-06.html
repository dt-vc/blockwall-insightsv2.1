SEC ends fraud case against Justin Sun after $10M settlement

Benjamin Njiri • Regulation • Score: 7/10

The U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission has settled its 2023 civil fraud case against Justin Sun, founder of Tron, and his companies including Tron Foundation and BitTorrent Foundation, with Rainberry Inc. paying a $10 million penalty. The allegations involved unregistered sales of TRX and BTT crypto assets, wash trading to inflate volumes by $31 million, and undisclosed payments to celebrities like Lindsay Lohan for promotions. Sun and his firms neither admitted nor denied wrongdoing, and all charges were dismissed pending court approval. For institutional investors, this resolution under the Trump administration signals a regulatory shift toward crypto innovation, potentially reducing enforcement risks and boosting TRX ecosystem participation.
