Ethereum news (ETH): The $293 million KelpDAO hack shows why DeFi is finally being forced to grow up

CoinDesk • Security • Score: 9/10

The $293 million exploit of KelpDAO, traced to a vulnerability in LayerZero’s bridge, has underscored a shift in the primary sources of risk within decentralized finance. The incident demonstrates that infrastructure, governance and operational security concerns now outweigh traditional smart‑contract bugs for large‑scale protocols. Leaders such as Lido and Spark say the breach will accelerate a move toward low‑risk, “boring” DeFi strategies as institutional investors prioritize reliability.

Solana news (SOL): Jump Crypto’s ‘Firedancer’ is taking a slow and steady approach to its long-awaited Solana infrastructure rollout

CoinDesk • Infrastructure • Score: 6/10

Jump Crypto has launched its Firedancer validator client on the Solana mainnet. The deployment is being executed in a measured, incremental fashion. This approach enables comprehensive security audits and reduces operational risk. The added client diversity and performance improvements are intended to support Solana’s ambition to attract institutional grade trading and real world financial applications.

Why is bitcoin down? BTC slides to $78,000, SOL, DOGE, XRP down 5%

CoinDesk • Bitcoin • Score: 6/10

Bitcoin fell roughly 3% to $78,000, erasing a week of gains. The decline coincided with a roughly $581 million wave of liquidations in the past 24 hours, of which about 95% were long positions. The broader market impact was evident as SOL, DOGE and XRP each slipped around 5%. Market participants cited higher inflation data, rising bond yields and elevated oil prices as macro pressures driving the sell‑off.

Bhutan ‘doesn’t recall’ selling any bitcoin, disputing widely-tracked $1 billion BTC drawdown

CoinDesk • Bitcoin • Score: 6/10

Arkham Intelligence data indicates Bhutan’s sovereign wealth fund moved and likely sold roughly $1 billion worth of Bitcoin, cutting its holdings from about 13,000 BTC to around 3,100 BTC. The fund’s investment unit says it does not recall any Bitcoin sales, directly disputing the data. The disagreement creates uncertainty for investors about the reliability of Bhutan’s pledged Bitcoin reserve that underpins a planned economic zone.

Hackers are draining billions from DeFi but almost none of your crypto is insured

CoinDesk • DeFi • Score: 5/10

Hackers have drained billions from decentralized finance, with $7.7 billion lost to exploits over the past six years and $600 million lost in April 2026 alone. Less than 2 percent of DeFi’s total value locked is covered by insurance, leaving the overwhelming majority of assets exposed to further attacks. Nexus Mutual currently holds the bulk of the sector’s insured capital, making it the primary source of protection for the limited insured portion.

"Crypto Without Privacy Isn't Crypto" - The Zcash Bull Case

Bankless • Investment • Score: 4/10

Multicoin Capital co‑founder Tushar Jain disclosed on a Bankless podcast that the firm has taken a sizable, undisclosed position in Zcash. He cited renewed narrative momentum around privacy and recent price action as the primary drivers of the investment decision. Guest Mert Mumtaz reinforced the view that privacy is a fundamental attribute that differentiates Zcash within the cryptocurrency ecosystem. Both analysts argue that this privacy focus could catalyze a broader market resurgence for Zcash, making it a strategic consideration for institutional portfolios.
