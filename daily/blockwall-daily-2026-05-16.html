🔥 Key Developments (8)

Ethereum news (ETH): The $293 million KelpDAO hack shows why DeFi is finally being forced to grow up

CoinDesk • Security • Score: 9/10

The recent $293 million exploit of KelpDAO, traced to a vulnerability in LayerZero’s bridge, underscores a growing threat vector in decentralized finance. The loss highlights that the sector’s most significant risks now stem from infrastructure, governance and operational security rather than traditional smart‑contract bugs. Leaders such as Lido and Spark say the event will accelerate a shift toward low‑risk, “boring” DeFi products as institutional capital prioritizes reliability.

Bitcoin Treasury Co Strategy Announces $1.5B Convertible Note Buyback

Cointelegraph • Enterprise • Score: 6/10

Bitcoin Treasury Co Strategy has disclosed a plan to repurchase up to $1.5 billion of its zero‑percent convertible notes that mature in 2029. The company intends to retire roughly half of that tranche through the buyback. Funding for the repurchase will come from cash reserves, proceeds from its at‑the‑market offering, and, if needed, sales of Bitcoin. The move occurs while the firm still holds approximately 819 k BTC valued at about $64 billion, underscoring its large crypto asset base.

US CLARITY Act Brings ‘Major Spike of Euphoria’ to Bitcoin: Santiment

Cointelegraph • Regulation • Score: 7/10

The US CLARITY Act, billed as the largest regulatory proposal for the cryptocurrency sector, has triggered a sharp rally in Bitcoin. Bitcoin’s price moved above $79,000 following the announcement. Analysts and the Santiment data platform see the pending legislation as a possible catalyst for increased institutional capital, but note that the bill remains unfinished.

Solana news (SOL): Jump Crypto’s ‘Firedancer’ is taking a slow and steady approach to its long-awaited Solana infrastructure rollout

CoinDesk • Infrastructure • Score: 6/10

Jump Crypto’s Firedancer validator client has quietly gone live on the Solana mainnet, representing the first phase of its long‑awaited infrastructure rollout. The incremental deployment is designed to enable comprehensive security audits and to ensure the client’s stability before broader adoption. By adding a high‑performance, resilient validator, Solana aims to broaden client diversity and strengthen its platform for institutional‑grade trading and real‑world financial applications.

Why is bitcoin down? BTC slides to $78,000, SOL, DOGE, XRP down 5%

CoinDesk • Bitcoin • Score: 6/10

Bitcoin fell about 3% to $78,000, erasing a week of gains and pulling down other major tokens such as Solana, Dogecoin and XRP, each off roughly 5%. In the preceding 24‑hour period about $581 million of crypto positions were liquidated, with long positions accounting for roughly 95% of the total. The sell‑off was driven by macroeconomic stress from higher inflation data, rising bond yields and elevated oil prices, factors that institutional investors monitor for risk exposure.

Bhutan ‘doesn’t recall’ selling any bitcoin, disputing widely-tracked $1 billion BTC drawdown

CoinDesk • Bitcoin • Score: 6/10

Arkham Intelligence data indicates that Bhutan’s sovereign wealth fund has moved and likely sold approximately $1 billion worth of Bitcoin since mid‑2025, reducing its holdings from roughly 13,000 BTC to about 3,100 BTC. Bhutan’s investment unit publicly disputes the alleged sales, stating it does not recall any Bitcoin transactions. The disagreement creates uncertainty for institutional investors regarding the reliability of the country’s pledged BTC reserve that underpins a planned economic zone.

House Committee Leaders Urge Trump to Nominate CFTC Members, Citing CLARITY Act

Cointelegraph • Regulation • Score: 6/10

House Agriculture Committee leaders are pressing President Trump to submit nominations for additional commissioners to the Commodity Futures Trading Commission. Their request coincides with the Senate Banking Committee’s progress on the CLARITY Act, legislation that would broaden the CFTC’s regulatory reach into digital asset markets. The CLARITY Act contains an amendment that makes the law contingent on having at least four confirmed commissioners in place. This staffing requirement reflects concerns that the agency’s current personnel shortfall could impede effective oversight of emerging crypto trading activities.

Hackers are draining billions from DeFi but almost none of your crypto is insured

CoinDesk • DeFi • Score: 5/10

Hackers continue to drain billions from decentralized finance (DeFi) protocols, exposing the sector to significant financial risk. Less than 2 percent of DeFi’s total value locked is covered by insurance, leaving the majority of assets unprotected despite $7.7 billion lost to exploits over the past six years and $600 million lost in April 2026 alone. Nexus Mutual currently holds the largest share of the insured capital in the market, underscoring the limited availability of coverage for institutional participants.
