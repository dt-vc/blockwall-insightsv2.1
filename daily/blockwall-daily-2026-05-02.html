Crypto industry backs CLARITY Act yield compromise, pushes Senate Banking for markup

CoinDesk • Regulation • Score: 6/10

U.S. Senators Thom Tillis and Angela Alsobrooks have issued a compromise amendment to the CLARITY Act that prohibits stablecoin yield products that replicate traditional bank deposits while allowing rewards linked to legitimate usage activities. The proposal has been quickly endorsed by major crypto trade groups such as Coinbase and Circle, which are urging the Senate Banking Committee to advance the legislation to a markup. Under the compromise, firms must redesign reward programs from a “buy‑and‑hold” structure to a “buy‑and‑use” model that ties earnings to actual token utility. The Crypto Council for Innovation cautioned that the language of the amendment may be overly broad, a point that institutional investors will watch closely as it could affect the design of compliant stablecoin offerings.

Ethereum Foundation Offloads $23M in ETH to BitMine for Third Time in Two Months

Cointelegraph • Ethereum • Score: 6/10

The Ethereum Foundation transferred 10,000 ETH, valued at approximately $23 million, to the mining pool BitMine, marking its third ETH sale within a two‑month window. This transaction follows an earlier unstaking event in which the foundation sold 17,035 ETH for roughly $40 million. The repeated off‑loading has attracted criticism from the Ethereum community and prompted questions about the foundation’s liquidity requirements. BitMine’s holdings now approach 5 million ETH and the firm is steadily raising the share of its portfolio that is actively staked, a trend that may affect staking yields and network security.
