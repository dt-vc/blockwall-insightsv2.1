Crypto industry backs CLARITY Act yield compromise, pushes Senate Banking for markup

CoinDesk • Regulation • Score: 6/10

U.S. Senators Thom Tillis and Angela Alsobrooks have issued a compromise amendment to the CLARITY Act that would prohibit stablecoin yield products that replicate traditional bank deposit interest while allowing rewards linked to legitimate usage activities. The proposal has been quickly endorsed by leading crypto trade groups such as Coinbase and Circle, which are urging the Senate Banking Committee to advance the legislation to a markup stage. Under the compromise, firms would need to redesign reward structures from a “buy‑and‑hold” approach to a “buy‑and‑use” model that ties earnings to actual token utilization. The Crypto Council for Innovation, however, cautioned that the language of the amendment may be overly broad and could create compliance uncertainty for the industry.

Crypto companies raised $600m in April despite despite market downturn, VCs say
