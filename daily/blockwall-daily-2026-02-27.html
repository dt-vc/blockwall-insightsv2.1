AI Tool Helps Avert Critical XRP Ledger Security Flaw

News • Security • Score: 7/10

The XRP Ledger Foundation patched a critical vulnerability in the unactivated Batch amendment, detected on February 19 by Cantina engineer Pranamya Keshkamat and the AI tool Apex through static code analysis of the rippled codebase. This flaw in signature verification logic could have enabled attackers to execute unauthorized transactions from victim accounts without private keys, potentially leading to fund theft on a scale rivaling XRP's $80 billion market capitalization. Validators rejected the amendment, and rippled version 3.1.1 was released on February 23 as an emergency measure, ensuring no mainnet impact. For institutional investors, this underscores XRPL's robust validator coordination and AI-driven security, bolstering network trust and resilience against large-scale exploits.
