Why DeFi is not dead after the KelpDAO exploit

CoinDesk • DeFi • Score: 7/10

The KelpDAO exploit led to a theft of $292 million and a $13 billion decline in total value locked across decentralized finance protocols. Analysis shows that the majority of the TVL drop was caused by the unwinding of leveraged positions rather than the loss of permanent capital. The breach has been associated with LayerZero’s verification stack and investigators have raised the possibility of involvement by North Korea’s Lazarus Group. Although the event triggered a short‑term outflow, TVL rebounded quickly, indicating that the DeFi ecosystem retains resilience and continues to attract institutional interest.

‘I suspected I was being socially engineered.’ Why crypto’s hacking epidemic is getting even worse

DL News • Security • Score: 6/10

Social‑engineering attacks on crypto projects are accelerating, according to the article. Recent high‑profile hacks include a $1.5 billion breach at Bybit and a $300 million loss at Drift Protocol, both traced to deceptive outreach. The broader cybercrime trend has already seen $786 million stolen from crypto projects in 2025. Institutional investors should view the rising prevalence of social‑engineering as a heightened operational risk that can erode capital and undermine confidence in digital‑asset platforms.
