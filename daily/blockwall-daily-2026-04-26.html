Why DeFi is not dead after the KelpDAO exploit

CoinDesk • DeFi • Score: 7/10

The KelpDAO exploit led to a theft of $292 million and a $13 billion decline in total value locked across DeFi protocols. Analysis indicates that most of the TVL drop was caused by the unwinding of leveraged positions rather than permanent capital loss. The breach has been linked to LayerZero’s verification stack and investigators have raised the possibility of involvement by North Korea’s Lazarus Group. Despite the short term outflow, TVL recovered quickly, underscoring the resilience of DeFi and its continued relevance for institutional investors.
