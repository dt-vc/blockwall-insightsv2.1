Satoshi’s 1.1M bitcoin and millions more can be saved from quantum attack, says expert

CoinDesk • Security • Score: 6/10

An expert says that the 1.1 million Bitcoin attributed to Satoshi Nakamoto and millions of other dormant coins could be protected from a future quantum‑computing attack. AmericanFortress has introduced a patent‑pending post‑quantum signature scheme that would be deployed through a backward‑compatible soft fork on Bitcoin and other major blockchains. The scheme is designed to freeze pre‑BIP32 addresses, which are considered vulnerable, until a governance process determines how to handle them. Backed by an $8 million seed round, the protocol could safeguard assets valued at more than $400 billion, offering institutional investors a potential risk mitigation tool.
