🔥 Key Developments (12)

Cardano won’t get left behind as partner chain Midnight inks £250m tokenisation deal, founder says

DL News • Tokenization • Score: 8/10

Midnight has partnered with UK challenger bank Monument to tokenise £250 million of customer deposits on a public blockchain. The tokenisation is the first FSCS‑protected, interest‑bearing token issuance by a UK‑regulated bank. Cardano founder Charles Hoskinson said Cardano will provide a supporting role as the partner chain for the project. The collaboration illustrates the growing convergence of traditional finance and blockchain tokenisation, offering institutional investors a regulated pathway to digital asset exposure.

Finally some clarity? Senators, White House strike deal to end crypto-banking standoff

DL News • Regulation • Score: 7/10

U.S. senators and the White House have reached an agreement in principle on the Clarity Act, which is intended to ease the deadlock over stablecoin rewards. The legislation seeks to protect innovation in the digital asset sector while reducing the risk of large deposit withdrawals from banks. The agreement is being closely monitored by major market participants such as Coinbase and JPMorgan Chase as a potential catalyst for a more crypto‑friendly regulatory environment.

Institutions are scooping up Bitcoin again as Iran war sparks $2.3bn ETF buying spree

DL News • Bitcoin • Score: 7/10

Institutional investors have poured $2.3 bn into Bitcoin exchange‑traded funds in the past month, reflecting renewed appetite for crypto exposure. The surge coincides with heightened geopolitical tension from the Iran war, which analysts say is driving demand for alternative assets. A crypto user accidentally swapped $50m for $37,000. Here’s where the money ended up

DL News • DeFi • Score: 7/10

An unknown trader on Ethereum mistakenly swapped $50 million of USDT for 327 Aave tokens valued at roughly $37 000. The trade hit a very small liquidity pool on Sushiswap, creating a massive price distortion. Arbitrage bots and the block builder Titan captured the imbalance and earned at least $35 million. The incident highlights the risk of low‑liquidity pools and the speed at which automated actors can extract value, a concern for institutional participants.

Why Washington won’t spoil crypto’s SEC victory

DL News • Regulation • Score: 6/10

The SEC has released a token taxonomy that categorizes digital assets to clarify regulatory expectations. It is also collaborating with Congress on a “Clarity Act” intended to define the overall structure of crypto markets. The chairs of the SEC and the CFTC have publicly affirmed their cooperation on the framework. Crypto industry participants anticipate that the legislation will reinforce the SEC’s guidance rather than impose additional restrictions, which is significant for institutional investors seeking regulatory certainty.

The Aave DAO voted near‑unanimously to approve the first phase of Aave v4, clearing a key hurdle for its rollout on Ethereum. The v4 upgrade is designed to boost capital efficiency and will implement a hub‑and‑spoke architecture that separates core protocol functions from specialized market modules. The vote also highlighted emerging governance tensions within the DAO, underscoring the need for careful coordination as the protocol expands.

Google is accelerating its timeline for quantum reckoning. Here’s when Bitcoin, Ethereum need to be ready

DL News • Security • Score: 6/10

Google has announced that it will transition its authentication and digital signature infrastructure to post‑quantum cryptography by 2029, accelerating its quantum‑readiness timeline. The Ethereum Foundation responded by publishing a four‑pronged roadmap that also targets a 2029 completion date for quantum‑resistant upgrades. Researchers caution that the cryptographic algorithms currently securing Bitcoin and Ethereum could become exploitable by future quantum computers. This emerging vulnerability is prompting institutional investors to monitor the readiness of major blockchain platforms for post‑quantum security measures.
