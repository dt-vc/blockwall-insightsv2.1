🔥 Key Developments (12)

Cardano won’t get left behind as partner chain Midnight inks £250m tokenisation deal, founder says

DL News • Tokenization • Score: 8/10

Midnight and UK challenger bank Monument have agreed to tokenise £250 million of customer deposits on a public blockchain. The arrangement is the first instance of a UK‑regulated bank issuing interest‑bearing tokens that are protected by the Financial Services Compensation Scheme. Cardano founder Charles Hoskinson confirmed that the Cardano network will provide a supporting layer for the tokenisation. The deal illustrates the increasing integration of traditional finance with blockchain technology and may signal new opportunities for institutional investors seeking regulated digital‑asset exposure.

Finally some clarity? Senators, White House strike deal to end crypto-banking standoff

DL News • Regulation • Score: 7/10

U.S. senators and the White House have reached an agreement in principle on the Clarity Act, which is intended to end the stalemate over stablecoin reward programs. The legislation seeks to balance protection of financial innovation with safeguards against large‑scale deposit withdrawals. The development is being closely monitored by major market participants such as Coinbase and JPMorgan Chase as a potential signal of a more crypto‑friendly regulatory environment.

Institutions are scooping up Bitcoin again as Iran war sparks $2.3bn ETF buying spree

DL News • Bitcoin • Score: 7/10

Institutional investors have poured $2.3 bn into Bitcoin exchange‑traded funds in the past month, a surge linked to heightened geopolitical tension from the Iran war. The inflow marks a renewed appetite for mainstream crypto exposure among large‑scale capital allocators. Analysts project that institutional Bitcoin holdings could reach as much as $13 trn by 2030 if the current trajectory continues. This optimism comes despite the broader market having lost roughly half of its value since October, underscoring the risk‑return calculus that institutions are weighing.

A crypto user accidentally swapped $50m for $37,000. Here’s where the money ended up

DL News • DeFi • Score: 7/10

An unknown Ethereum trader mistakenly swapped $50 million of USDT for 327 Aave tokens valued at roughly $37 k by interacting with a small liquidity pool on Sushiswap. The erroneous trade created a severe price distortion that was quickly exploited by arbitrage bots and the block builder known as Titan. Those actors captured the resulting arbitrage opportunity and earned at least $35 million, highlighting the risks of low‑liquidity pools and the profit potential for sophisticated market participants.
