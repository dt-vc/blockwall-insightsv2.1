Clarity Act failure could see US lose global crypto leadership, experts warn

DL News • Regulation • Score: 7/10

The Clarity Act, a bill designed to create a comprehensive regulatory framework for digital assets in the United States, has passed the House of Representatives and now awaits a Senate vote. Experts caution that a failure of the legislation could cause the United States to lose its position as a global leader in the crypto sector. They argue that uncertainty may prompt crypto firms to relocate to jurisdictions with clearer rules, reducing the domestic market size. Institutional investors are watching the outcome closely because regulatory certainty is a key factor in allocating capital to crypto‑related assets.

Appellate Court Affirms Blocking New Jersey Enforcement against Kalshi

Cointelegraph • Regulation • Score: 6/10

An appellate court has upheld a lower‑court order that prevents New Jersey from enforcing its own regulatory actions against Kalshi. Kalshi is an exchange that offers event‑contract products and is approved by the Commodity Futures Trading Commission. The decision underscores that federal regulators have primary authority over crypto‑related derivatives, limiting state attempts to impose separate rules.

North Korean Hackers Infiltrated Crypto For Seven Years

Cointelegraph • Security • Score: 7/10

North Korean state‑sponsored hackers have been operating covertly within the cryptocurrency ecosystem for seven years. Their activities have targeted exchanges, wallets, and decentralized finance platforms. The prolonged infiltration highlights ongoing security weaknesses in crypto infrastructure. Institutional investors should consider the heightened risk posed by nation‑state actors when assessing crypto exposure.

Polymarket To Replace USDC.e With USDC-Backed Token In Exchange Upgrade

Cointelegraph • DeFi • Score: 5/10

Polymarket, a decentralized prediction‑market platform, has announced an upgrade to its exchange infrastructure. The upgrade will retire the bridged USDC.e token and introduce a native token that is fully backed by USDC. By using a native USDC‑backed token the platform expects more stable liquidity for traders. The change also reduces the protocol’s exposure to cross‑chain bridge risks, which is relevant for institutional participants seeking lower operational risk.

South Korea’s $8.5B securities giant reportedly eyeing crypto exchange takeover deal

DL News • Enterprise • Score: 5/10

Korea Investment & Securities, an $8.5 billion South Korean securities firm, is reportedly evaluating a takeover of the crypto exchange Coinone. Its rival Mirae Asset is also reported to be pursuing a similar acquisition of another domestic exchange, Korbit. The interest from both traditional financial institutions signals a broader shift toward crypto assets in South Korea as tokenisation legislation remains stalled and follows earlier reports that Coinbase had looked at Coinone.

Circle announced a quantum‑proof roadmap for its upcoming layer‑1 blockchain, Arc, detailing cryptographic upgrades and research collaborations aimed at resisting future quantum attacks. The initiative is intended to position Arc as a secure infrastructure for digital assets and stablecoins, addressing concerns about quantum‑era vulnerabilities. Institutional investors may view the roadmap as a risk‑mitigation measure that could enhance the long‑term viability of assets hosted on the network.
