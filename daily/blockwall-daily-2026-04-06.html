Clarity Act failure could see US lose global crypto leadership, experts warn

DL News • Regulation • Score: 7/10

The Clarity Act is a bill designed to create a comprehensive regulatory framework for cryptocurrency in the United States. The measure has passed the House of Representatives and now awaits a Senate vote that remains uncertain. Analysts caution that failure of the legislation could prompt crypto firms to move overseas and diminish the U.S. market, jeopardizing the country’s leadership in the global crypto sector.

Appellate Court Affirms Blocking New Jersey Enforcement against Kalshi

Cointelegraph • Regulation • Score: 6/10

An appellate court has upheld a lower‑court order that prevents the state of New Jersey from enforcing its own regulatory actions against Kalshi. Kalshi operates as a CFTC‑approved exchange that offers event‑based contracts. The decision confirms that federal oversight of crypto‑related derivatives takes precedence over state‑level attempts to regulate such platforms. Institutional investors should note that the ruling clarifies the regulatory hierarchy and may reduce state‑specific compliance risk for federally authorized crypto derivatives exchanges.

Polymarket To Replace USDC.e With USDC-Backed Token In Exchange Upgrade

Cointelegraph • DeFi • Score: 5/10

The upgrade will introduce a native token that is directly backed by USDC. By using a native USDC‑backed token the protocol expects more stable liquidity for traders. The change also reduces the platform’s exposure to cross‑chain bridge risk, which is a key concern for institutional participants.
