Drift to issue ‘recovery tokens’ in wake of $295m hack

DL News • Security • Score: 7/10

Drift, a derivatives exchange built on Solana, suffered a hack that resulted in the loss of $295 million. In response, the platform’s team announced a recovery plan that will issue transferable “recovery tokens.” Each token is backed by a compensation pool that will be financed through future protocol revenue and stablecoins pledged by the community. The plan must be approved by token holders and the pool could require as much as eight years to fully reimburse affected users.

Why is crypto up today: Bitcoin (BTC) tops $81,500 as tokenization push lifts BLSH, GLXY

CoinDesk • Tokenization • Score: 7/10

Bitcoin surged past $81,500 as more than $500 million flowed into spot Bitcoin exchange‑traded funds, driving the day's market upside. The inflows helped spark a broader tokenization rally that lifted equities tied to the sector, including BLSH and GLXY. Tokenization won't disrupt banking rails but improve them, Wall Street executives say

CoinDesk • Tokenization • Score: 7/10

Wall Street banks and the DTCC are transitioning tokenized assets from pilot projects into production, handling real‑world volumes and integrating blockchain rails into existing market infrastructure. Executives say tokenization will augment, not replace, traditional banking systems, enabling 24/7 settlement and faster collateral management. The move signals that institutional investors can expect more efficient post‑trade processes while relying on familiar regulatory frameworks.

Strategy weighs selling bitcoin to fund dividends amid Q1 net loss

CoinDesk • Bitcoin • Score: 6/10

MicroStrategy reported a first‑quarter net loss of $12.54 billion. To cover $1.5 billion in dividend commitments the company said it may sell a portion of its 818,334 bitcoin holdings. The suggestion caused the company's shares to fall more than 4 percent in after‑hours trading. The news also contributed to bitcoin slipping below $81,000, a level that matters to investors tracking corporate exposure to the cryptocurrency.

Hyperliquid takes aim at Kalshi, Polymarket targeting $24bn prediction markets

DL News • DeFi • Score: 7/10

Hyperliquid has entered the prediction‑market sector by launching its first Bitcoin outcome market. In its debut the platform generated roughly three times the trading volume of established players Polymarket and Kalshi combined. Bernstein analysts interpret the surge as evidence that institutional investors are starting to employ binary contracts to hedge macro‑event risk. The move positions Hyperliquid as a new competitor targeting the roughly $24bn prediction‑market opportunity.

Coinbase shares pop as CEO Brian Armstrong cuts 700 jobs and blames AI

DL News • Enterprise • Score: 6/10

Coinbase CEO Brian Armstrong announced a 14% reduction in the company’s workforce, eliminating roughly 700 positions and refocusing remaining staff on AI‑driven initiatives. The restructuring is expected to generate a one‑time charge of $50‑60 million. Investors responded positively, lifting Coinbase shares by about 4% on the news. The move signals a broader effort to contain costs and improve margins as the cryptocurrency exchange navigates a volatile market environment.

MicroStrategy’s shares jumped more than 50% over the past month as Bitcoin surpassed the $80,000 level, fueling renewed institutional attention. The price rally coincides with the company’s upcoming Q1 earnings call, where investors will scrutinize its Bitcoin‑focused balance sheet. In the same period, Bitcoin spot exchange‑traded funds attracted $827 million of inflows during the first two trading days of May. Simultaneously, short positions exceeding $200 million were liquidated, highlighting the market’s bullish momentum.

DeFi rides Pokémon trading card boom as onchain marketplaces bring in $11m

DL News • DeFi • Score: 5/10

DeFi platforms that facilitate the purchase and sale of NFT versions of Pokémon, One Piece and other trading cards generated about $11 million in revenue in the most recent month, according to DefiLlama data. The revenue spike highlights a rapid expansion of on‑chain collectible markets. Physical card markets are experiencing supply constraints that are driving collectors toward digital alternatives. Institutional investors are monitoring the trend as speculative interest in blockchain‑based assets adds a new layer of liquidity and diversification potential.

넥서스(Nexus): FTX의 교훈, AI 에이전트 시대에 다시 쓰다.

Tiger Research • Infrastructure • Score: 5/10

Nexus is building a layer one financial stack that relies on zero knowledge proofs to make reserves, settlement and trade matching mathematically verifiable. The protocol combines its own exchange with the USDX stablecoin on a single blockchain, creating a three part architecture. By making core financial processes provably sound, Nexus seeks to deliver transparent trustless finance for markets increasingly driven by AI agents. The design is positioned as a response to the FTX collapse, offering institutional investors a framework that reduces custodial risk and enhances regulatory confidence.
