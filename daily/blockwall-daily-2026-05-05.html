Drift to issue ‘recovery tokens’ in wake of $295m hack

DL News • Security • Score: 7/10

Drift, a Solana‑based derivatives exchange, suffered a hack that resulted in the loss of $295 million. In response, the Drift team proposed a recovery plan that creates transferable recovery tokens. The recovery token pool will be financed through future protocol revenue and stablecoins pledged by the community. Implementation of the plan requires approval from token holders and may take as long as eight years for the pool to fully compensate affected users.

The rollout is handling real‑world volumes and embedding blockchain settlement rails into the existing market infrastructure. Strategy weighs selling bitcoin to fund dividends amid Q1 net loss

CoinDesk • Bitcoin • Score: 6/10

Strategy (MSTR) reported a Q1 net loss of $12.54 billion and indicated it may sell a portion of its 818,334 BTC holdings to fund $1.5 billion in dividend obligations. The prospect of a large bitcoin liquidation is significant for institutional investors because it could affect the company’s balance sheet and exert downward pressure on the cryptocurrency market. The announcement prompted a more than 4% after‑hours decline in MSTR stock and pushed bitcoin below $81,000.

Bankers Say CLARITY Act Stablecoin Provisions Still Flawed

Cointelegraph • Regulation • Score: 6/10

Bankers have criticized the CLARITY Act's stablecoin provisions as containing a significant loophole. They plan to submit detailed amendment suggestions to lawmakers to address the gap. The draft currently prohibits interest on stablecoin balances but still allows other reward mechanisms, prompting industry participants such as Coinbase to push for a Senate markup. The final rules will influence how banks and other large investors structure stablecoin holdings and meet regulatory requirements.

Crypto Spring Has Begun Tom Lee says, Bitmine Buys 101,745 ETH

Cointelegraph • Investment • Score: 6/10

Bitmine Immersion Technologies bought an additional 101,745 ETH valued at roughly $242 million. The purchase raises Bitmine’s total ETH holdings to more than 5.1 million, which represents about 4.29 % of the circulating supply. Chairman Tom Lee said the acquisition marks the beginning of a “crypto spring” and cited supportive trends from Wall Street tokenization and AI‑driven demand. The firm is pursuing an aggressive accumulation strategy aimed at reaching a 5 % stake in the circulating ETH supply, a move that could influence market dynamics and attract institutional capital.

Tennessee Bankers Association Taps Stablecore for Crypto infrastructure

Cointelegraph • Infrastructure • Score: 5/10

The Tennessee Bankers Association has selected Stablecore as its preferred provider of digital‑asset infrastructure, giving its member banks a turnkey solution for offering stablecoins, tokenized deposits and crypto‑backed lending. This partnership extends Stablecore’s platform to roughly 175 banks that belong to the association, potentially increasing the availability of regulated crypto services in the regional banking sector. The move aligns with ongoing U.S. regulatory discussions on stablecoin oversight and signals growing institutional interest in integrating crypto products into traditional banking services.

Polygon Launches Wallet Privacy Feature to Hide Senders, Receivers and Amounts Onchain

Cointelegraph • Layer2 • Score: 6/10

Polygon has introduced a wallet privacy feature that hides the sender, receiver and transaction amounts on‑chain. The feature still enables compliance checks through Know‑Your‑Transaction (KYT) screening and can generate audit files for regulators. This development follows similar privacy initiatives launched by Aptos. The addition is significant for institutional investors as it aims to protect transaction confidentiality while maintaining auditability amid growing stablecoin activity on Polygon.
