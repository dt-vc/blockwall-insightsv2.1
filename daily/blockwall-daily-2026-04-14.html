Deutsche Börse invests $200m in crypto exchange Kraken

DL News • Enterprise • Score: 9/10

Deutsche Börse AG has committed $200 million to acquire a 1.5% equity stake in Payward, the parent company of the Kraken cryptocurrency exchange. The investment is intended to deepen a partnership that links traditional finance infrastructure with digital‑asset services. As part of the deal Kraken’s trading, custody and tokenised‑asset capabilities will be integrated into Deutsche Börse’s electronic trading platforms, including 360T and 360X. The move underscores a broader institutional push to provide clients with access to crypto markets and tokenised assets.

Feds announce crypto scam OneCoin compensation plan. But small investors may suffer

DL News • Regulation • Score: 7/10

The U.S. Department of Justice has launched a remission compensation process for victims of the OneCoin pyramid scheme, which allegedly defrauded participants of about $4 billion. The program is intended to provide a mechanism for eligible claimants to recover a portion of their losses. Legal counsel cautions that many small investors may be unable to produce the documentation required to qualify for payouts. Institutional investors should monitor the rollout as it may set precedent for future crypto fraud restitution efforts.

Goldman Sachs to offer low-risk, low-reward Bitcoin ETF

DL News • Bitcoin • Score: 7/10

Goldman Sachs has filed to launch a Bitcoin Premium Income ETF that will not hold Bitcoin directly but will invest in existing spot Bitcoin ETFs and related options. The fund’s strategy is to generate income by selling call options on the Bitcoin ETFs, giving it a low‑risk, low‑reward profile compared with direct cryptocurrency exposure. This product adds to a growing market of bank‑backed Bitcoin ETFs that are competing on price and may appeal to institutional investors seeking modest returns with limited crypto volatility.

Aave founder declares 'zero room for friction' as DAO orgs accuse his firm of grabbing power

DL News • DeFi • Score: 6/10

The Aave DAO approved a $25 million stablecoin and an allocation of 75,000 AAVE tokens to Aave Labs. The allocation gives Aave Labs 100 percent of revenue from Aave‑branded products to the DAO treasury. Three long‑standing DAO service providers, including the Aave Chan Initiative, announced they will exit the DAO, arguing that power is becoming increasingly centralized around Aave Labs. The founder’s statement that there is “zero room for friction” underscores the tension between governance decentralization and operational efficiency, a dynamic that could affect the protocol’s risk profile and investor confidence.

Russian central bank wants all crypto traders to undergo identity verification checks

DL News • Regulation • Score: 6/10

The Russian central bank has issued a directive requiring all domestic cryptocurrency exchanges to carry out know‑your‑customer (KYC) verification for users. It also obliges Russian residents to disclose any crypto assets held abroad. In addition, the regulator intends to ban the transfer of funds from custodial wallets to non‑custodial wallets located outside Russia. The measures are part of a comprehensive crypto regulatory package slated for implementation in July, signaling tighter oversight that could affect cross‑border crypto trading and compliance requirements for institutional investors.
