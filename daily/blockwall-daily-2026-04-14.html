Deutsche Börse invests $200m in crypto exchange Kraken

Deutsche Börse AG has invested $200 million for a 1.5 percent equity stake in Payward, the parent company of the cryptocurrency exchange Kraken. The investment deepens a partnership that links Deutsche Börse’s traditional finance infrastructure with Kraken’s trading, custody and tokenised‑asset services. Feds announce crypto scam OneCoin compensation plan. But small investors may suffer

DL News • Regulation • Score: 7/10

The U.S. Department of Justice has launched a remission compensation process for victims of the OneCoin pyramid scheme, which defrauded participants of roughly $4 billion. The program is intended to provide a mechanism for eligible claimants to recover a portion of their losses. Legal counsel cautions that many small investors may be unable to produce the documentation required to substantiate their claims. Institutional investors should monitor the rollout of the plan as it highlights enforcement trends and potential exposure to similar crypto frauds.

Goldman Sachs to offer low-risk, low-reward Bitcoin ETF

DL News • Bitcoin • Score: 7/10

Goldman Sachs has filed to launch a Bitcoin Premium Income ETF that will not hold Bitcoin directly but will invest in existing spot Bitcoin ETFs and related options. The fund is designed to deliver a low‑risk, low‑reward profile by selling call options on the underlying Bitcoin ETFs. This product adds to a growing competitive market for Bitcoin‑linked offerings among major banks, providing institutional investors with a more conservative exposure to cryptocurrency price movements.

XRP Ledger adds zero-knowledge proofs targeting institutional privacy gap

CoinDesk • Infrastructure • Score: 7/10

The XRP Ledger has integrated the Boundless zero‑knowledge proving network, adding native ZK‑proof verification. This enables private transactions while remaining compliant with existing regulations. The upgrade is aimed at institutional users who require confidentiality for transaction size, counterparties and treasury positions. It may also enhance the ledger’s resilience against future quantum threats.

JPMorgan CFO warns stablecoins risk becoming ‘regulatory arbitrage’ play

CoinDesk • Stablecoins • Score: 7/10

JPMorgan CFO Jeremy Barnum warned that stablecoins could become a tool for regulatory arbitrage if they are not subject to the same oversight as bank deposits. The bank is urging U.S. regulators to issue clearer rules for digital assets to mitigate that risk. JPMorgan also points to its own blockchain initiatives, such as JPM Coin, as part of its broader digital‑asset strategy.

The funding will be allocated to develop services that enable users to generate yields on crypto holdings and secure loans backed by those assets. DAO Behind CoW Swap Urges Users to Stay off Platform after ‘Hijacking‘

The decentralized autonomous organization that governs the CoW Swap protocol has issued an advisory urging users to refrain from accessing the platform. Institutional investors should monitor the situation closely as the outcome could affect liquidity and risk exposure across the broader DeFi ecosystem.

Aave founder declares 'zero room for friction' as DAO orgs accuse his firm of grabbing power

DL News • DeFi • Score: 6/10

The Aave DAO approved a $25 million stablecoin and a 75,000 AAVE token allocation to Aave Labs, giving the lab full rights to revenue from Aave‑branded products for the DAO treasury. The founder of Aave emphasized a “zero room for friction” stance while three long‑standing DAO service providers, including the Aave Chan Initiative, announced they will exit the DAO. The departures underscore institutional concerns about growing centralisation of power and revenue flow within the Aave ecosystem.

Russian central bank wants all crypto traders to undergo identity verification checks

DL News • Regulation • Score: 6/10

The Russian central bank has ordered all domestic cryptocurrency exchanges to introduce know‑your‑customer procedures for users. It also requires Russian residents to report any crypto assets held abroad. In addition, the regulator will bar transfers from custodial wallets located in Russia to non‑custodial wallets outside the country. The measures are part of a broader crypto regulatory package that is slated to become operational in July and signal tighter oversight that could affect institutional investment strategies in the region.
