U.S. and Israel launch airstrikes against Iran after enormous military build-up in the Mideast, risking wider regional conflict

Jason Ma • Other • Score: 7/10

On February 28, 2026, the United States and Israel launched coordinated airstrikes under operations codenamed Roaring Lion, Operation Shield of Judah, and Operation Epic Fury, targeting Iranian cities including Tehran, Isfahan, Qom, Karaj, Kermanshah, and Bushehr to destroy missile capabilities, prevent nuclear weapon development, and weaken the regime. President Donald Trump announced the strikes alongside Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu, who described them as essential to eliminate Iran's existential threat after decades of hostility, while calling on IRGC members to surrender. The attacks killed or wounded thousands of IRGC personnel and hit key military sites, following prior U.S. strikes on Iranian nuclear facilities in June, amid Iran's missile retaliation alerts. For institutional investors, this escalation heightens risks to oil prices, supply chains through the Strait of Hormuz, and global energy markets, potentially amplifying volatility in commodities and defense sectors.
