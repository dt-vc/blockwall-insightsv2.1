CME Group to Launch Avalanche and Sui Futures Contracts

South Korea Tightens Crypto Rules with 5-minute Asset Verification Mandate

Cointelegraph • Regulation • Score: 7/10

South Korea's Financial Services Commission has issued a new regulation that obligates crypto exchanges to confirm their asset holdings within five minutes of each transaction. The rapid verification requirement is designed to increase market transparency and reduce the risk of price manipulation. Exchanges that fail to meet the five‑minute verification standard may be subject to penalties or could be barred from operating in the country. The rule signals tighter oversight of digital asset markets and is likely to affect institutional investors who rely on compliant platforms for trading and custody.
