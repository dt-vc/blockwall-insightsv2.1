CME Group to Launch Avalanche and Sui Futures Contracts

Cointelegraph • Infrastructure • Score: 7/10

CME Group has announced that it will introduce cash‑settled futures contracts for the Avalanche (AVAX) and Sui (SUI) tokens. By adding these contracts, CME broadens its suite of cryptocurrency derivatives and provides institutional investors with regulated exposure to two emerging layer‑1 blockchains. The launch reflects increasing institutional demand for diversified crypto assets and may deepen market liquidity for AVAX and SUI.

South Korea Tightens Crypto Rules with 5-minute Asset Verification Mandate

Cointelegraph • Regulation • Score: 7/10

South Korea's Financial Services Commission has issued a new regulation that obliges cryptocurrency exchanges to confirm their asset holdings within five minutes of any transaction. The rule is designed to increase market transparency and deter price manipulation in the rapidly growing digital asset sector. Exchanges that fail to meet the verification requirement could be subject to penalties or even a ban on their operations, underscoring the regulator’s tightening oversight of crypto activities.

Is Chaos Labs’ Exit the Privatization of Aave?

Tiger Research • DeFi • Score: 7/10

Chaos Labs, which serves as the risk curator for the Aave lending protocol, has announced that it will leave its role. The departure is attributed to budget shortfalls and disagreements over the design of Aave’s upcoming V4 architecture. The move underscores ongoing governance tensions within Aave Labs, where decision‑making appears increasingly centralized despite the protocol’s DAO structure. Institutional investors may view this development as a signal of heightened risk management challenges as Aave transitions from V3 to V4.

USDC currently accounts for the largest share of stablecoin trading volume on South Korean platforms, indicating strong market traction. The discussions will focus on deepening institutional adoption and evaluating the feasibility of a won‑pegged stablecoin, which could expand the ecosystem for cross‑border payments and liquidity management.

US Spot Bitcoin ETFs Hit Strongest Gains Since February

Cointelegraph • Investment • Score: 6/10

US spot Bitcoin exchange‑traded funds recorded net inflows of about $307 million in the first three trading days of April, pushing total assets under management past $90 billion. March saw $1.3 billion of inflows, delivering the first monthly net increase after outflows in January and February. Ether spot ETFs attracted $120 million of new capital, while the broader category of Ether ETFs remained in a three‑month loss streak. The inflow trends highlight renewed institutional appetite for regulated crypto exposure and suggest growing liquidity for the underlying assets.

Crypto ETPs Rebound With $224M Inflows Led by XRP: CoinShares

Cointelegraph • Investment • Score: 6/10

Crypto exchange‑traded products (ETPs) recorded a net inflow of $224 million, according to CoinShares data. Ripple’s XRP accounted for the largest share of the new capital, leading the inflows across the product category. ETPs provide regulated, exchange‑listed exposure to cryptocurrencies, allowing institutional investors to gain market participation without holding the underlying assets directly. The recent inflow surge is interpreted as a sign of renewed appetite for regulated crypto exposure among professional investors.
