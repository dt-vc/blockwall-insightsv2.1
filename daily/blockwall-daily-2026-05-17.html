The Clarity Act took a step forward: State of Crypto

CoinDesk • Regulation • Score: 7/10

The US Senate Banking Committee voted 15‑9 to advance the Digital Asset Market Clarity Act to the full Senate. The vote included all Republicans and two Democrats, indicating expanding bipartisan backing for a crypto market‑structure framework. Passage of the Clarity Act would establish clearer regulatory parameters for digital assets, a development that institutional investors are watching closely as it could affect compliance, custody and investment strategies, though additional Senate approval and possible compromises remain necessary.

Japan's SBI Securities, Rakuten Securities to offer crypto investment trusts, ETFs

CoinDesk • Regulation • Score: 6/10

SBI Securities and Rakuten Securities plan to launch cryptocurrency investment trusts and exchange‑traded funds in Japan pending final approval of the regulatory framework by the Financial Services Agency. The products would give institutional investors regulated access to digital assets through familiar vehicle structures. A Nikkei survey indicates that another eleven major securities firms are monitoring the same regulatory developments and could introduce comparable offerings.

Bitcoin DeFi's new front: VerifiedX bets BTC's next chapter is programmable and private

CoinDesk • Bitcoin • Score: 5/10

VerifiedX is building a Bitcoin sidechain, referred to as a “reliever chain,” designed to deliver native, programmable and privacy‑preserving DeFi on the Bitcoin network without relying on wrapped assets. The architecture uses self‑custodial threshold signatures and Taproot‑based addresses, allowing transactions to remain under Bitcoin’s native security model. With Bitcoin‑based DeFi assets currently exceeding $5 billion, the project positions itself to capture a significant portion of the anticipated growth in secure, on‑chain financial services for institutional portfolios.
