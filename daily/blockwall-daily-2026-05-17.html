The Clarity Act took a step forward: State of Crypto

CoinDesk • Regulation • Score: 7/10

The US Senate Banking Committee voted 15‑9 to advance the Digital Asset Market Clarity Act to the Senate floor. The vote included all Republicans and two Democrats, indicating growing bipartisan support for a clearer regulatory framework for digital assets. The bill, which aims to establish market‑structure rules for crypto, still requires additional approvals and potential compromises before it can become law, a development that institutional investors are watching closely.

Japan's SBI Securities, Rakuten Securities to offer crypto investment trusts, ETFs

CoinDesk • Regulation • Score: 6/10

SBI Securities and Rakuten Securities have announced that they will introduce cryptocurrency investment trusts and exchange‑traded funds in Japan. The products will be launched after the Financial Services Agency completes its pending regulatory framework for digital assets. A Nikkei survey indicates that eleven additional major securities firms are monitoring the same regulatory progress and are prepared to roll out comparable offerings. The move reflects a broader shift toward institutional‑grade crypto investment vehicles in the Japanese market.

Bitcoin DeFi's new front: VerifiedX bets BTC's next chapter is programmable and private

CoinDesk • Bitcoin • Score: 5/10

VerifiedX is building a Bitcoin sidechain called a reliever chain that seeks to deliver native, programmable and privacy‑preserving DeFi on Bitcoin without relying on wrapped assets. The design uses self‑custodial threshold signatures and Taproot‑based addresses to retain Bitcoin’s security model. Bitcoin DeFi currently holds just over $5 billion, indicating a sizable market. By adding programmable and private capabilities, the project aims to expand Bitcoin’s utility and attract institutional capital to the ecosystem.
