North Korea Tied to Heists Worth $578M in April After Kelp DAO Exploit

Cointelegraph • Security • Score: 9/10

In April, actors linked to North Korea executed two major crypto thefts, taking a total of $578 million from the Kelp DAO exploit and the Drift decentralized exchange. The operations demonstrate that DPRK cyber groups are expanding tactics to include in‑person social engineering at industry conferences. The incidents coincide with an increase in crypto‑crime complaints to the FBI, underscoring heightened regulatory and security risks for institutional participants in digital assets.

Justin Sun says he's suing Trump's $2.5bn crypto project over frozen WLFI tokens

DL News • Regulation • Score: 7/10

Tron founder Justin Sun has filed a lawsuit against World Liberty Financial, a cryptocurrency project linked to former President Donald Trump. UK government bets on stablecoins and tokenisation to drive fintech growth

DL News • Regulation • Score: 6/10

The UK Treasury has introduced a package of regulatory measures targeting stablecoins and tokenised payment systems. The package includes a new crypto bill that sets out rules for digital asset issuers and a dedicated wholesale digital markets champion to oversee implementation. By establishing clear oversight, the government seeks to attract fintech firms and institutional investors to the UK’s digital asset ecosystem. The approach balances the ambition to become a leading hub for digital assets with the need to protect consumers.

Crypto’s biggest selling point is responsible for $8.5bn in losses. But it can be made safe

DL News • Security • Score: 5/10

Private key compromises have resulted in $8.5 bn of crypto losses, highlighting the security challenges of self‑custody for investors. The magnitude of these losses underscores why self‑custody, often promoted as crypto’s biggest selling point, remains a risk factor for institutional portfolios. Cybersecurity specialist David Schwed contends that employing dedicated security officers and upgrading infrastructure can mitigate those risks. He points to recent DeFi hacks on Drift and Kelp DAO as evidence that weak security practices, not the self‑custody model itself, drive the losses.
