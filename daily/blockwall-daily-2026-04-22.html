Justin Sun says he's suing Trump's $2.5bn crypto project over frozen WLFI tokens

DL News • Regulation • Score: 7/10

Tron founder Justin Sun has filed a lawsuit against World Liberty Financial, the Trump‑linked crypto project, alleging that the platform froze approximately $75 million of his WLFI voting tokens. The legal action follows a previous settlement with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission involving the same project. WLFI’s token price has fallen about 76 percent from its peak, reducing the project's market valuation to roughly $2.5 billion. The dispute highlights heightened regulatory and operational risk for investors in politically associated crypto ventures.

DL News • Regulation • Score: 6/10

The UK Treasury has unveiled a suite of measures to regulate stablecoins and tokenised payments, including the introduction of a new crypto bill. It also created a dedicated wholesale digital markets champion to oversee implementation. The government says the steps are designed to position the UK as a leading destination for digital assets while preserving consumer safeguards, a signal that could attract institutional investment in fintech.

Crypto’s biggest selling point is responsible for $8.5bn in losses. But it can be made safe

DL News • Security • Score: 5/10

Private key compromises have resulted in $8.5 bn of crypto losses, highlighting the security challenges of self‑custody for institutional investors. The losses underscore why control of private keys, often touted as crypto’s biggest advantage, can become a liability without robust safeguards. Cybersecurity specialist David Schwed recommends that firms appoint dedicated security officers and upgrade their infrastructure to protect self‑custody assets. He points to recent DeFi hacks on Drift and Kelp DAO as evidence that weak security practices can lead to costly breaches.

Everyone is frothing about Bitcoin treasury company Strategy’s STRC bond. Should they be?

DL News • Bitcoin • Score: 5/10

Strategy’s STRC preferred equity is a high‑yield security that is collateralized by Bitcoin and currently offers an 11.5% dividend. The instrument has attracted strong demand from investors seeking exposure to digital assets through a fixed‑income‑style product. Analysts warn that the dividend can be suspended and that the Bitcoin underlying is speculative, which introduces significant risk. Consequently, the current enthusiasm may be short‑lived and institutional investors should weigh the yield against the potential volatility and credit risk.

Kalshi Selects Pyth to Set Prices for Commodities Trades

Cointelegraph • Infrastructure • Score: 5/10

Kalshi, a regulated derivatives platform that lists commodity contracts, has announced that it will adopt the Pyth network as its official price oracle for those contracts. Pyth delivers real‑time, tamper‑proof price data sourced from a consortium of market participants. By feeding this data directly into Kalshi’s trading system, the integration is intended to improve price accuracy and reduce the risk of manipulation across multiple commodity assets. The enhanced data reliability is expected to bolster market integrity and give institutional investors greater confidence in settlement and compliance processes.

Gun-wielding thieves hold family captive, make off with $800,000 worth of crypto

DL News • Security • Score: 5/10

Armed thieves broke into a home in Ploudalmézeau, Brittany and stole a cold wallet containing roughly $800,000 worth of cryptocurrency by obtaining its private keys. The victims were the family of a crypto‑industry professional who were held captive for three hours before the thieves fled. No arrests have been made in connection with the robbery. The incident highlights a rising trend of crypto‑related thefts in France this year, underscoring the need for enhanced physical and digital security measures for high‑value digital assets.

The offering aims to create a single platform where institutional investors can trade a broad set of asset classes without moving between separate venues. By emulating Robinhood’s multi‑asset approach, the product is expected to drive growth for both firms once it receives regulatory clearance.

DeFi Platform Volo Hit by $3.5M Vault Attack, Begins Recovery Efforts

Cointelegraph • DeFi • Score: 5/10

Volo, a decentralized finance platform that offers yield‑generating vaults, disclosed that it suffered a vault attack resulting in an approximate loss of $3.5 million. The breach highlights the operational risk inherent in permissionless smart‑contract systems that institutional investors must evaluate. In response, the Volo team has launched a recovery program that includes comprehensive security audits by external firms. The protocol also indicated that it is exploring compensation mechanisms for affected users while working to restore confidence among liquidity providers.
