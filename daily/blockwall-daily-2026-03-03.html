Spot bitcoin ETFs post $458 million in net inflows as institutions buy into global instability: analysts

Danny Park • Bitcoin • Score: 7/10

U.S. spot Bitcoin ETFs recorded $458 million in net inflows on Monday, led by BlackRock's iShares Bitcoin Trust (IBIT) with $263 million, signaling robust institutional accumulation amid recent weeks of outflows. This surge contrasts with retail investor exits from platforms like Binance, highlighting institutions' dominance as they position Bitcoin as a hedge against escalating Middle East conflicts and global geopolitical tensions. For institutional investors, the inflows underscore Bitcoin's maturation as a portfolio diversifier, with cumulative ETF assets exceeding $55 billion and BlackRock's IBIT alone surpassing $50 billion, enhancing liquidity and long-term exposure options. Analysts note this shift boosts price resilience near $68,000, with $60,000 as key support amid ongoing market volatility.
