CFTC Sues New York Over bid to Apply Gambling Laws to Prediction Markets

Cointelegraph • Regulation • Score: 6/10

The U.S. Commodity Futures Trading Commission has filed a lawsuit against New York for attempting to impose state gambling regulations on prediction‑market platforms. Thirty‑seven states have submitted an amicus brief backing Massachusetts, contending that state oversight is needed to ensure licensing, age verification, fraud controls and safeguards against gambling addiction. The regulatory clash follows a wave of state actions that include cease‑and‑desist letters and court orders targeting platforms such as Kalshi and Polymarket. Institutional investors should monitor the outcome because it could shape the legal framework governing U.S. prediction markets and affect the compliance risk of related digital‑asset businesses.

Brazil Bans 27 Prediction Platforms, Including Kalshi and Polymarket

Cointelegraph • Regulation • Score: 5/10

Brazil's financial regulator has issued a prohibition on 27 prediction‑market platforms, naming Kalshi and Polymarket among those affected. The regulator justified the ban on the basis that the platforms operate in violation of the country's gambling and financial‑services legislation. This action aligns Brazil with recent European measures that have also targeted prediction‑market services. The growing regulatory patchwork raises compliance and market‑access challenges for operators seeking to serve institutional investors in the region.

AI boom leaves crypto VC fundraising in the dust, founders warn

DL News • Investment • Score: 5/10

AI startups are drawing significantly more venture capital than crypto projects, creating a funding gap that crypto founders are publicly warning about. The disparity highlights a broader market trend where investors are prioritizing artificial‑intelligence ventures over blockchain‑based businesses.
