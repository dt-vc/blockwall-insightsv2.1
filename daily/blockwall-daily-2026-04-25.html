Bitcoin traders are getting bullish that a rally is neigh. Should they be?

DL News • Bitcoin • Score: 4/10

Analysts note that increased institutional buying, a softening of geopolitical tensions, and expectations of Federal Reserve rate cuts are fueling expectations of a Bitcoin rally that could lift the cryptocurrency toward the $80,000 level. Bitcoin Price Inertia Brings $73,000 Target Into Focus Next

Cointelegraph • Bitcoin • Score: 3/10

Technical analysis indicates that Bitcoin is poised to retest the $73,000 resistance zone, a level that could shape near‑term market sentiment. The 21‑week exponential moving average, positioned near $78,400, is identified as a decisive barrier that must be reclaimed for further upside. Analysts caution that failure to break back above the EMA could trigger a decline toward the $60,000 support area. These price thresholds are closely watched by institutional investors as they influence allocation decisions and risk management strategies.
