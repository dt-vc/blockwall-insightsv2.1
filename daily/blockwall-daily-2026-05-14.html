Here is why Wall Street is racing to tokenize the entire stock market

CoinDesk • Tokenization • Score: 9/10

Wall Street firms are accelerating efforts to tokenize equities, a move underscored by Bullish’s $4.2 billion acquisition of transfer agent Equiniti to enable on‑chain issuance and record‑keeping. Tokenized securities promise 24/7 trading, near‑instant settlement, and richer ownership data, which could improve liquidity and operational efficiency for institutional investors. However, the transition faces regulatory and market‑structure challenges that must be addressed before widespread adoption.

LIVEBLOG: Senate Banking Committee advances Clarity Act to full Senate floor

The Senate Banking Committee voted to move the bipartisan Clarity Act, which seeks to create durable regulatory rules for stablecoins, to the full Senate floor. The legislation is being hailed by crypto industry leaders as a defining moment for U.S. digital‑asset leadership. Consumer finance groups have criticized the bill while banking trade associations are urging additional adjustments.

Why is crypto up today: bitcoin (BTC) hits $82,000, Coinbase gains as Clarity Act advances

CoinDesk • Regulation • Score: 7/10

Bitcoin rose to $82,000 after the Senate Banking Committee moved forward the Clarity Act, a legislative proposal aimed at providing clearer rules for digital assets. Crypto‑related equities responded positively, with Coinbase stock gaining 8% on the news. CME dives further into $85 trillion digital assets market with Nasdaq CME Crypto Index futures

CoinDesk • Infrastructure • Score: 7/10

CME Group is extending its presence in the roughly $85 trillion digital assets market by introducing a new futures product tied to the Nasdaq CME Crypto Index. The exchange plans to launch its first market‑cap‑weighted index futures on June 8, subject to regulatory clearance. The cash‑settled contracts, available in micro and standard sizes, are designed to give institutional investors direct exposure to a basket of leading cryptocurrencies.

Bitcoin ETFs Post Largest Outflows Since January as BTC Slips

Cointelegraph • Bitcoin • Score: 7/10

Bitcoin spot ETFs experienced their biggest daily net withdrawals since January, with BlackRock’s iShares Bitcoin Trust losing roughly $285 million. The outflows coincided with Bitcoin slipping below the $80,000 level after a 37 percent rally. Analysts attributed the movement to profit‑taking pressure and a softening of demand among investors. Ether‑linked ETFs also recorded net outflows while funds tied to Solana saw modest net inflows, highlighting divergent sentiment across crypto‑focused products.

Stablecoin-powered neobank Fasset raises $51 million to expand across emerging markets

CoinDesk • Stablecoins • Score: 7/10

Los Angeles based neobank Fasset secured a $51 million Series B round with investors SBI Group, Investcorp and Arz Portföy. The capital is earmarked for geographic expansion into additional emerging market corridors and for launching lending and trade finance products. It will also support scaling of Fasset’s proprietary Own Network stablecoin infrastructure, which underpins its digital‑banking services and could enhance liquidity and cross‑border transaction efficiency for institutional participants.

Kraken to replace LayerZero with Chainlink for kBTC, future wrapped assets

CoinDesk • Infrastructure • Score: 7/10

Kraken announced that it will replace LayerZero with Chainlink’s Cross‑Chain Interoperability Protocol (CCIP) as the exclusive bridge for its kBTC token and any future wrapped assets. The move secures more than $3 billion in total value locked that Kraken currently manages after a recent $292 million exploit on a LayerZero bridge highlighted security risks. By adopting CCIP, Kraken aligns with other platforms that have migrated to Chainlink’s cross‑chain infrastructure, reflecting a broader industry shift toward proven interoperability solutions. Institutional investors can view the transition as a risk‑mitigation step that leverages Chainlink’s reputation for reliability and may improve confidence in Kraken’s wrapped‑asset offerings.

BlackRock, Janus Henderson tokenized funds get instant redemptions with new $1 billion facility

CoinDesk • Tokenization • Score: 7/10

Grove has launched the Basin liquidity network to provide up to $1 billion of daily stablecoin liquidity for tokenized Treasury funds managed by BlackRock and Janus Henderson. The network enables instant redemptions, reducing settlement times from days to minutes. By bridging blockchain‑based fund trading with traditional settlement rails, the facility improves liquidity and operational efficiency for institutional investors. The initiative reflects growing collaboration between major asset managers and crypto infrastructure firms to expand the tokenized U.S. Treasury market.

Kraken Owner Payward Trims Workforce Ahead of Potential IPO

CoinDesk • Enterprise • Score: 7/10

Payward, the parent company of the Kraken cryptocurrency exchange, is reducing its workforce by roughly 150 employees. The layoffs are part of a broader effort to streamline operations as the firm prepares for a potential initial public offering. Payward is also seeking new capital at a valuation of $20 billion to support its growth strategy. Recent acquisitions of Reap, Bitnomial and the earlier purchase of NinjaTrader signal the company's intent to strengthen its market position before a public listing when conditions improve.

Strive launches first daily dividend security in U.S. markets

CoinDesk • Security • Score: 6/10

Strive has launched its Variable Rate Series A Perpetual Preferred Shares, a security that will pay cash dividends each business day, making it the first U.S.-listed instrument with daily payouts. The daily dividend structure raises the effective yield to roughly 13.88 percent, offering a high‑yield option for investors. With all debt retired and a holding of more than 15,000 Bitcoin, the security is positioned as a crypto‑backed alternative to conventional money‑market products.
