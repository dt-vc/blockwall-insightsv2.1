🔥 Key Developments (12)

Here is why Wall Street is racing to tokenize the entire stock market

CoinDesk • Tokenization • Score: 9/10

Wall Street firms are accelerating efforts to tokenize equities, highlighted by Bullish’s $4.2 billion acquisition of transfer agent Equiniti to enable on‑chain issuance and record‑keeping. Proponents say tokenized securities could provide 24/7 trading, near‑instant settlement, and richer ownership data. The push matters because it could reshape market infrastructure, but regulatory and market‑structure challenges remain.

Cerebras shares skyrocket 100% after $5.5 billion IPO amid AI stock frenzy

CoinDesk • Enterprise • Score: 9/10

Cerebras Systems completed a $5.5 billion IPO, with its shares debuting at roughly double the offering price. The market valuation after the debut was about $40 billion, reflecting strong investor appetite for AI infrastructure. The rally coincided with a shift of capital away from crypto assets, as Bitcoin slipped below $70,000, underscoring the broader AI stock frenzy.

LIVEBLOG: Senate Banking Committee advances Clarity Act to full Senate floor

CoinDesk • Regulation • Score: 8/10

The Senate Banking Committee voted to advance the bipartisan Clarity Act, which is designed to create durable regulatory rules for stablecoins, to the full Senate floor. Crypto industry leaders hailed the vote as a defining moment for U.S. digital‑asset leadership, while consumer finance groups expressed criticism of the proposal. Banking trade groups said further tweaks are needed, underscoring the ongoing debate over how stablecoin oversight will affect institutional investors and the broader financial system.

CME dives further into $85 trillion digital assets market with Nasdaq CME Crypto Index futures

CoinDesk • Infrastructure • Score: 7/10

CME Group is expanding its presence in the estimated $85 trillion digital‑assets market by introducing Nasdaq CME Crypto Index futures. The contracts, which are cash‑settled and market‑cap‑weighted, are slated to begin trading on June 8 pending regulatory approval. They are offered in micro and standard contract sizes to provide institutional investors with direct exposure to a basket of leading cryptocurrencies.

Clarity Act clears U.S. Senate committee, on its way to a final test in Congress

CoinDesk • Regulation • Score: 7/10

The U.S. Senate Banking Committee approved the Digital Asset Market Clarity Act by a 15‑9 vote, giving the crypto market‑structure bill bipartisan backing. The approval followed last‑minute amendments that secured Democratic support and will allow the measure to be merged with a similar proposal from the Agriculture Committee. The combined legislation now moves toward a full Senate and House vote, where it could establish clearer regulatory parameters that are important for institutional investors seeking certainty in digital‑asset markets.

BlackRock, Janus Henderson tokenized funds get instant redemptions with new $1 billion facility

CoinDesk • Tokenization • Score: 7/10

Grove has launched the Basin liquidity network, which supplies up to $1 billion of daily stablecoin liquidity for instant redemptions of tokenized Treasury funds managed by BlackRock and Janus Henderson. By linking blockchain‑based fund trading with traditional settlement rails, the facility reduces settlement times from days to minutes. The partnership signals growing collaboration between major asset managers and crypto infrastructure firms to expand the tokenized U.S. Treasury market.

Kraken to replace LayerZero with Chainlink for kBTC, future wrapped assets

CoinDesk • Infrastructure • Score: 7/10

Kraken announced it will replace LayerZero with Chainlink’s Cross‑Chain Interoperability Protocol (CCIP) as the exclusive bridge for its kBTC and future wrapped assets. The move secures more than $3 billion in total value locked that was previously routed through the LayerZero bridge. The decision follows a $292 million exploit on a LayerZero bridge that prompted the migration. Kraken’s shift aligns with similar migrations by other platforms and signals increasing institutional confidence in Chainlink’s cross‑chain infrastructure.

Kraken Owner Payward Trims Workforce Ahead of Potential IPO

CoinDesk • Enterprise • Score: 7/10

Payward, the parent company of the Kraken cryptocurrency exchange, announced a reduction of roughly 150 positions to streamline its operations. The workforce cut is part of a broader effort to prepare for a planned initial public offering while seeking new capital at a valuation of $20 billion. Payward has also pursued an acquisition strategy, recently buying Reap and Bitnomial and previously acquiring NinjaTrader. These moves are intended to strengthen the firm’s market position and improve its prospects for a public listing when market conditions become favorable.

Stablecoin-powered neobank Fasset raises $51 million to expand across emerging markets

CoinDesk • Stablecoins • Score: 7/10

Los Angeles‑based neobank Fasset raised $51 million in a Series B round backed by SBI Group, Investcorp and Arz Portföy. The capital will be used to expand into additional emerging‑market corridors, launch lending and trade‑finance products, and scale its proprietary Own Network stablecoin infrastructure. The move underscores growing institutional interest in stablecoin‑enabled banking platforms that can provide financial services to underbanked populations in developing economies.

Strive launches first daily dividend security in U.S. markets

CoinDesk • Security • Score: 6/10

Strive has launched Variable Rate Series A Perpetual Preferred Shares (SATA), the first U.S.-listed security to pay cash dividends each business day. The daily dividend structure lifts the effective annualized yield to roughly 13.88 percent. With all debt retired and a holding of more than 15,000 Bitcoin, the security offers institutional investors a crypto‑backed, high‑yield alternative to conventional money‑market products.

Cerebras IPO fuels AI stock frenzy as crypto loses investor attention

CoinDesk • Investment • Score: 6/10

Cerebras Systems priced its initial public offering at $185 per share, raising roughly $5.5 billion and giving the AI chipmaker a market value of about $40 billion. The large inflow of equity capital into AI related companies is creating a broader stock market frenzy around artificial intelligence technologies. At the same time Bitcoin has slipped below $70,000 and retail participation in the cryptocurrency has weakened, indicating a shift of investor attention toward AI assets.

BTC ETFs lose $635 million in a single day. What next?

CoinDesk • Bitcoin • Score: 6/10

Bitcoin spot exchange‑traded funds recorded a net outflow of $635 million in a single day, the largest single‑day withdrawal since January. The outflow pushed five‑day cumulative withdrawals to $1.26 billion. The redemptions occurred as Bitcoin fell below its 200‑day moving average to roughly $79,400, a move linked to renewed U.S. inflation concerns. Analysts caution that ongoing macro pressure could suppress price momentum even after earlier periods of inflows, a risk that institutional investors monitor closely.
