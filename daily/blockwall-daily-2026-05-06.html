Bullish’s Equiniti deal could remake it into a tokenization powerhouse, analysts say

CoinDesk • Tokenization • Score: 8/10

Bullish has announced a $4.2 billion acquisition of transfer‑agent Equiniti. The transaction gives Bullish direct access to issuer records and a regulated framework for securities issuance. Analysts say the combined capabilities will enable Bullish to build a tokenization infrastructure platform for equities. This positions the firm to compete in the emerging market for tokenized securities and could make it a leading provider of tokenization services for institutional investors.

Eric Trump takes shot at JPMorgan rethinking bitcoin after 'crapping' on asset

CoinDesk • Bitcoin • Score: 6/10

Eric Trump, co‑founder of the mining firm American Bitcoin, criticized JPMorgan for changing its stance on bitcoin after previously dismissing the asset. He noted that the bank now offers mortgages that are backed by bitcoin, indicating a shift toward acceptance. JPMorgan CEO Jamie Dimon, who was once a vocal critic of the cryptocurrency, is now publicly supporting blockchain technology and tokenization. American Bitcoin claims it can mine bitcoin at roughly 50 cents on the dollar, positioning the company as the lowest‑cost producer and potentially influencing institutional demand.

Turnkey raises $12.5 million in round backed by Circle Ventures and Sequoia Capital

CoinDesk • Infrastructure • Score: 6/10

Turnkey announced a $12.5 million strategic funding round led by Circle Ventures, Sequoia Capital and other investors, taking its total capital raised to over $65 million. The proceeds will fund development and launch of Turnkey Verifiable Cloud, a secure verifiable computing platform designed for digital‑asset workloads. The investment highlights expanding institutional appetite for crypto infrastructure and stable‑coin‑related services.

Morgan Stanley

Bankless • Enterprise • Score: 6/10

Morgan Stanley has launched a pilot spot crypto trading service on its E*TRADE platform, extending direct digital‑asset access to its 8.6 million retail clients. The offering is priced at a 0.5 % transaction fee, positioning it below competitors such as Charles Schwab and providing a fee‑free alternative to BlackRock’s IBIT product. The initiative aligns with the firm’s recent rollout of a spot Bitcoin ETF, which has already attracted $211 million of inflows.

블랙록(BlackRock)의 BUIDL, $2.63B의 온체인 기초 자산이 되다

Tiger Research • Tokenization • Score: 6/10

BlackRock's BUIDL token now controls $2.63 billion of on‑chain assets. The token has moved from being limited to institutional investors to serving as a core reserve asset across several decentralized finance protocols. Platforms such as Ethena, Ondo and Frax have integrated BUIDL because it provides a clear legal claim, regulatory compliance and on‑chain composability. This adoption enables tokenized institutional capital to be used in decentralized dollar‑denominated products, expanding the bridge between traditional finance and DeFi.

BlackRock BUIDL: $2.63B On-Chain Reserve Asset

Tiger Research • Infrastructure • Score: 6/10

BlackRock's BUIDL token now holds roughly $2.63 billion in on‑chain assets, making it a sizable reserve for the crypto ecosystem. Although initially created for qualified institutional investors, the token is being integrated by DeFi protocols including Ethena, Ondo, Frax and Spark as a core reserve asset. Its legal clarity, regulatory compliance and on‑chain composability provide a new distribution channel for tokenized assets, effectively turning an institutional product into infrastructure for decentralized dollar products.

Bitcoin tops $81,000 as Strategy mulls selling BTC to fund dividend obligations

CoinDesk • Bitcoin • Score: 5/10

Bitcoin broke the $81,000 level, driven by a broad risk‑on rally. MicroStrategy indicated it may sell a portion of its 818,334‑bitcoin portfolio to meet its $1.5 billion annual dividend commitment. The potential liquidation caused MicroStrategy’s shares to fall 4 percent. This marks the first time the publicly traded firm has signaled a sale of its bitcoin holdings, highlighting the intersection of corporate finance and cryptocurrency exposure for institutional investors.

The Protocol: AI Agents form their own firm

CoinDesk • Infrastructure • Score: 5/10

ClawBank’s Manfred AI agent became the first autonomous entity to legally incorporate a company in the United States, obtaining an employer identification number, an FDIC‑insured bank account and a crypto wallet. This milestone demonstrates that AI‑driven financial agents can meet regulatory requirements for corporate formation and banking access, opening a new frontier for automated asset management. The development occurs alongside Solana’s upcoming Alpenglow upgrade and Ripple’s threat‑intel sharing initiative, underscoring broader shifts in the crypto ecosystem toward advanced technology integration.
