🔥 Key Developments (8)

Bullish’s Equiniti deal could remake it into a tokenization powerhouse, analysts say

CoinDesk • Tokenization • Score: 8/10

Bullish announced a $4.2 billion acquisition of transfer‑agent Equiniti. The transaction gives Bullish direct access to issuer records and a regulated framework that can support tokenisation of equities. Analysts say the deal positions Bullish to become a tokenization infrastructure powerhouse and to compete in the emerging tokenized securities market, a development that could be material for institutional investors seeking blockchain‑based asset solutions.

Eric Trump takes shot at JPMorgan rethinking bitcoin after 'crapping' on asset

CoinDesk • Bitcoin • Score: 6/10

Eric Trump, co‑founder of American Bitcoin, criticized JPMorgan for reversing its stance on bitcoin after previously dismissing the asset. He noted that the bank now permits mortgages that are collateralized by bitcoin, indicating a significant policy shift. JPMorgan CEO Jamie Dimon, who had been a vocal critic of the cryptocurrency, is reportedly embracing blockchain technology and tokenization. American Bitcoin asserts that its mining operation can produce bitcoin at a cost of roughly 50 cents per dollar, positioning the firm as a low‑cost supplier for institutional portfolios.

Turnkey raises $12.5 million in round backed by Circle Ventures and Sequoia Capital

CoinDesk • Infrastructure • Score: 6/10

Turnkey has secured $12.5 million in a strategic funding round backed by Circle Ventures, Sequoia Capital and other investors. The new capital brings Turnkey’s total funding to more than $65 million. The proceeds will be allocated to develop and launch Turnkey Verifiable Cloud, a secure verifiable computing platform designed for digital‑asset workloads. The investment highlights increasing institutional interest in crypto infrastructure and services linked to stablecoins.

Morgan Stanley

Bankless • Enterprise • Score: 6/10

Morgan Stanley has begun a pilot on its E*TRADE platform that allows spot cryptocurrency trading for its 8.6 million retail clients. The pilot charges a 0.5 percent transaction fee, which is lower than the fees charged by competitors such as Charles Schwab. It also positions the service as a fee‑free alternative to BlackRock’s IBIT offering. The launch aligns with Morgan Stanley’s recent rollout of a spot Bitcoin ETF that has already attracted $211 million in inflows, signaling the firm’s broader push into digital assets.

블랙록(BlackRock)의 BUIDL, $2.63B의 온체인 기초 자산이 되다

Tiger Research • Tokenization • Score: 6/10

BlackRock's BUIDL token now holds $2.63 B in on‑chain assets. It has moved from being limited to institutions to serving as a core reserve asset for several DeFi protocols. Protocols such as Ethena, Ondo and Frax are using BUIDL because it provides a clear legal claim, regulatory compliance and on‑chain composability. This adoption enables tokenized institutional capital to back decentralized dollar products.

BlackRock BUIDL: $2.63B On-Chain Reserve Asset

Tiger Research • Infrastructure • Score: 6/10

BlackRock's BUIDL token now holds $2.63 billion in on‑chain assets, reflecting its scale as a reserve asset. Although originally designed for qualified institutional investors, the token is being integrated by DeFi protocols including Ethena, Ondo, Frax and Spark as a foundational reserve. Its legal clarity, regulatory compliance and on‑chain composability create a new distribution channel for tokenized assets, positioning the product as core infrastructure for decentralized dollar products.

Bitcoin tops $81,000 as Strategy mulls selling BTC to fund dividend obligations

CoinDesk • Bitcoin • Score: 5/10

Bitcoin surged past $81,000 amid a broad risk‑on rally. MicroStrategy signaled it may sell part of its 818,334‑BTC holdings to fund its $1.5 billion annual dividend, sending the stock down 4 percent. This would be the first time the corporate bitcoin holder may liquidate its position. Institutional investors should watch the potential impact on Bitcoin supply dynamics and the precedent of a large corporate holder using crypto assets to meet dividend obligations.

The Protocol: AI Agents form their own firm

CoinDesk • Infrastructure • Score: 5/10

ClawBank’s Manfred AI agent has become the first autonomous entity to legally incorporate a company in the United States. The newly formed entity obtained an Employer Identification Number, opened an FDIC‑insured bank account and created a crypto wallet. This development marks a new frontier for AI‑driven financial agents and could reshape how institutional investors interact with automated services. The move occurs alongside Solana’s upcoming Alpenglow upgrade and Ripple’s threat‑intel sharing, underscoring broader innovation in the crypto ecosystem.
