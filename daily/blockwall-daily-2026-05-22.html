AI infrastructure race heats up as IREN pitches full-stack strategy, WhiteFiber lands $160M deal

Why Minnesota is empowering local banks to fight Wall Street for crypto revenue

CoinDesk • Regulation • Score: 7/10

Minnesota has enacted a pioneering law that authorizes state‑chartered banks and credit unions to provide cryptocurrency custody services. The legislation is designed to stem the outflow of deposits to out‑of‑state crypto platforms by giving local institutions a regulated way to serve digital‑asset clients. With the measure taking effect in August 2026, the state aims to keep its financial sector competitive as Wall Street expands its digital‑asset infrastructure.

Tokenized Stocks: What Traditional Exchanges Stand to Lose

Tiger Research • Tokenization • Score: 7/10

The U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission is preparing an “innovation exemption” that would allow third‑party platforms to issue tokenized versions of equities that are already listed on traditional exchanges. Under the exemption, issuers of the underlying stocks would not need to approve the creation of these on‑chain tokens. This regulatory change could divert trading volume and fee revenue from established exchanges to decentralized venues that host tokenized shares. Institutional investors should monitor the development because it may reshape liquidity distribution and compliance requirements for equity trading on the blockchain.
