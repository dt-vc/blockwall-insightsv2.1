AI infrastructure race heats up as IREN pitches full-stack strategy, WhiteFiber lands $160M deal

CoinDesk • Infrastructure • Score: 8/10

IREN co‑founder Dan Roberts says the primary constraint on AI growth is physical infrastructure and the firm is pursuing a vertically integrated model that includes power generation, data centers, GPUs and software. Why Minnesota is empowering local banks to fight Wall Street for crypto revenue

CoinDesk • Regulation • Score: 7/10

Minnesota has enacted a pioneering law that authorizes state‑chartered banks and credit unions to provide cryptocurrency custody services. The legislation is designed to stem the outflow of deposits to out‑of‑state crypto platforms. By allowing local institutions to offer digital‑asset custody, the state aims to keep them competitive with Wall Street firms that are expanding into digital‑asset infrastructure. The measure will take effect in August 2026 and signals a regulatory approach that could attract institutional crypto activity to regional banks.

Wall Street and crypto are merging again as OKX rolls out traditional oil benchmarks

CoinDesk • Infrastructure • Score: 7/10

Intercontinental Exchange (ICE) and the cryptocurrency exchange OKX have announced a partnership to issue perpetual oil futures linked to ICE’s Brent and WTI benchmarks. The products will be made available on OKX’s platform, which serves roughly 120 million retail users. By tying the contracts to established energy benchmarks, the collaboration brings regulated commodity exposure into the crypto ecosystem. Regulators have indicated that they will oversee these perpetual derivatives, underscoring the increasing convergence of traditional finance and digital asset markets.

CME FedWatch data indicates that the probability of an interest‑rate cut in 2026 is effectively zero and market participants are pricing in additional hikes later in the year. THORChain

Cointelegraph • Security • Score: 6/10

THORChain, a cross‑chain liquidity protocol, experienced a $10.7 million exploit when a malicious node reconstructed a vault private key using a GG20 threshold‑signature vulnerability. In response, the network halted all trading activity and deployed a software patch to close the identified flaw. The team is now evaluating a recovery strategy that does not involve minting or selling additional RUNE tokens, aiming to protect existing token holders and maintain market confidence.

Trump Media moved but 'did not sell' $205 million in bitcoin amid rising losses on crypto bets

CoinDesk • Bitcoin • Score: 6/10

Trump Media & Technology Group transferred 2,650 Bitcoin, valued at roughly $205 million, to the Crypto.com platform. The company emphasized that the assets were moved and not sold, leaving a $455 million unrealized loss on its Bitcoin holdings. The transfer comes after Trump Media withdrew its application for a spot Bitcoin exchange‑traded fund. The move follows a reported $405.9 million net loss for the first quarter, underscoring the firm’s exposure to cryptocurrency market volatility.

Tokenized Stocks: What Traditional Exchanges Stand to Lose

Tiger Research • Tokenization • Score: 7/10

Institutional investors should note that the SEC's proposed innovation exemption would allow third parties to create tokenized representations of listed equities without the underlying company's consent. This could divert trading volume and fee income from established stock exchanges to decentralized platforms. The shift may alter the infrastructure for equity settlement and raise regulatory and custody considerations for large investors.
