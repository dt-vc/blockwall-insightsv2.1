‘Slow, expensive’ fate awaits if Clarity Act fails, warn experts as clock ticks on bill

DL News • Regulation • Score: 7/10

The Crypto Clarity Act, which would establish a uniform regulatory framework for digital assets, must be approved by the Senate Banking Committee by early May or it could be removed from the legislative calendar. Industry analysts assess the bill’s likelihood of passage at roughly fifty percent. They caution that a failure to enact the measure would leave the crypto sector operating under a fragmented and costly regulatory regime. Institutional investors are watching closely because clear rules are essential for risk management and long‑term capital allocation.

U.S. military runs a Bitcoin (BTC) node, sees crypto as 'power projection' vs China

CoinDesk • Bitcoin • Score: 7/10

The U.S. Indo‑Pacific Command has publicly disclosed that it is operating a live Bitcoin node. The node is used to monitor blockchain activity and conduct security tests rather than to mine cryptocurrency. This capability is being framed as part of a broader strategy to leverage crypto technology for American power projection in competition with China. The initiative represents an uncommon instance of direct U.S. military participation in the Bitcoin network, which may signal heightened institutional interest in blockchain resilience and geopolitical risk management.

FBI Security Flaw to Extract Readable Previews of Signal Messages

Cointelegraph • Security • Score: 6/10
