U.S. military runs a Bitcoin (BTC) node, sees crypto as 'power projection' vs China

CoinDesk • Bitcoin • Score: 7/10

The U.S. Indo‑Pacific Command has disclosed that it is operating a live Bitcoin node to monitor network activity and conduct security testing. The command describes Bitcoin as a strategic tool for American power projection against China. The node is used solely for testing and does not mine, representing a rare instance of direct U.S. military involvement in the Bitcoin network. This signals that federal agencies consider cryptocurrency infrastructure relevant to national security, a factor that could influence regulatory and market dynamics for institutional investors.

‘Slow, expensive’ fate awaits if Clarity Act fails, warn experts as clock ticks on bill

DL News • Regulation • Score: 7/10

The Crypto Clarity Act, a legislative proposal intended to establish a clearer regulatory framework for digital assets, is under pressure to pass the Senate Banking Committee by early May. If it does not clear the committee, the bill is likely to be removed from the legislative calendar. Industry analysts assess the probability of passage at roughly 50 percent. A failure would create a slower, more costly operating environment for the crypto sector, which could affect institutional investors seeking compliance certainty.

Tether helps US feds by freezing $344m in USDT tied to crime

DL News • Stablecoins • Score: 6/10

Tether has frozen $344 million of its USDT stablecoin that was identified as tied to illicit activity. The company said the action demonstrates its ongoing cooperation with law‑enforcement agencies around the world. The freeze underscores the stablecoin’s ability to block malicious funds and contrasts with criticism that rival Circle has been slower to act in similar cases. Institutional investors may view the move as a signal of enhanced compliance controls and reduced regulatory risk for assets held on the Tether platform.
