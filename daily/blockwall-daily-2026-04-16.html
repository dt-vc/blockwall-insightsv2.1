Tether throws $128m lifeline to hacked Drift. But the USDT issuer wants something in return

DL News • Stablecoins • Score: 9/10

Tether is extending a $127.5 million liquidity injection to the Drift Protocol after it suffered a major security breach. The funding is contingent on Drift replacing USDC with USDT as its settlement token. The hack, attributed to a North‑Korean‑linked actor, had previously removed about $286 million from the Solana‑based decentralized exchange, highlighting the strategic importance of stablecoin choice for risk‑averse institutional participants.

Morgan Stanley Bitcoin ETF rakes in $100m as Wall Street follows suit: ‘Excellent number’

DL News • Investment • Score: 7/10

Morgan Stanley’s newly launched Bitcoin exchange‑traded fund pulled in $100 million of assets in its first six days, the first in‑house Bitcoin product offered by a major Wall Street bank. The rapid inflow has been described as an “Excellent number,” underscoring the growing appetite for regulated crypto exposure among institutional investors. The strong debut is prompting other large financial firms such as Goldman Sachs and Charles Schwab to prepare their own cryptocurrency offerings.

Prediction markets, crypto get bipartisan scrutiny at congressional hearing

DL News • Regulation • Score: 6/10

CFTC Chair Mike Selig appeared before a bipartisan congressional hearing to defend the agency’s current approach to prediction markets. Republican members pressed the agency for guidance on onshoring crypto platforms and for measures to curb insider trading in digital assets. Democratic lawmakers used the forum to denounce unregulated prediction markets and to highlight perceived gaps in the regulatory framework, signaling possible legislative action that could affect institutional exposure to these assets.

Jupiter: A Gassed Up Giant

Delphi Digital • DeFi • Score: 7/10

Jupiter is a Solana‑based decentralized finance protocol that has not raised a traditional venture round. Its sole known external funding was a $35 million investment from ParaFi Capital in February 2026, leaving the platform with a near‑zero capital base. In 2025 the protocol generated roughly $176 million of revenue while processing $1.16 trillion of trade volume across 42.9 million wallets, demonstrating strong profitability and scale that may interest institutional investors.

The Clarity Act is a ‘threat to economic sovereignty.’ So why is Donald Trump so keen on it?

DL News • Regulation • Score: 6/10

The U.S. Senate is reviewing the pending Clarity Act, which would broaden regulation of stablecoins and related platforms. Critics describe the legislation as a threat to economic sovereignty because it would permit interest‑like rewards on stablecoins that could compete with traditional bank products. Such rewards are seen as potentially undermining the role of conventional banks in the financial system. Former President Donald Trump has expressed strong interest in the bill, aligning his stance with the broader political debate over crypto regulation.

Solana news (SOL): Wall Street tech is coming to crypto as DoubleZero rolls out high-speed data for blockchain

CoinDesk • Infrastructure • Score: 6/10

DoubleZero has introduced DoubleZero Edge, a dedicated high‑speed data feed that streams raw Solana blockchain data to traders over a private fiber network. The service is designed to cut latency to the millisecond level, bringing Wall Street‑style speed to crypto markets. Solana validators can monetize the feed by selling market data to subscribers in USDC. The offering signals a convergence of traditional finance infrastructure with blockchain, which may attract institutional investors seeking faster execution and new revenue models.

Nomura: Four out five institutional investors plan to pile into crypto with DeFi a top focus

DL News • Investment • Score: 6/10

Nomura’s Digital Asset Institutional Investor Survey reports that almost four out of five institutional investors plan to increase crypto exposure. The respondents, who collectively manage more than $60 billion, expect to allocate between two and five percent of assets under management to digital assets in the coming year. DeFi protocols and stablecoins are cited as the primary focus areas for these allocations. Investors view crypto as a diversification tool and are attracted to yield‑generating strategies such as staking, lending and tokenised assets.

World Liberty Financial wants to unlock 62 billion tokens. Justin Sun isn’t happy

DL News • Tokenization • Score: 5/10

World Liberty Financial has filed a governance proposal to release 62 billion WLFI tokens over a five‑year horizon, with most of the allocation earmarked for insiders. The proposal also calls for a 10 percent burn of insider holdings and sets a staggered distribution that would start in 2028. Crypto entrepreneur Justin Sun publicly denounced the plan as an “absurd governance scam,” raising concerns about token dilution and governance risk for investors.

Bitcoin price news: BTC slides after failing at key resistance levels

CoinDesk • Bitcoin • Score: 5/10

Bitcoin fell below $74,000 after failing to break the $75,000‑$76,000 resistance zone. The price weakness prompted a modest decline in equities linked to cryptocurrency. Simultaneously, investors withdrew $1.67 billion from digital‑asset investment products, the largest weekly outflow for Bitcoin funds this year. The combined price slide and fund outflows highlight heightened risk sentiment among institutional participants.

Trump issues ultimatum to Fed chair as Bitcoin trembles at $75,000. ‘I’ll have to fire him’

DL News • Bitcoin • Score: 5/10

President Donald Trump publicly warned that he would fire Federal Reserve Chair Jerome Powell if Powell does not step down when his term ends in May. The statement introduces a potential shift in monetary policy that could affect interest‑rate expectations and liquidity conditions. Bitcoin has risen to about $75,000 amid analyst commentary that the Fed leadership transition is a key driver for risk‑on assets. The prospect of a pro‑crypto nominee such as Kevin Warsh being considered for the chairmanship adds further uncertainty for institutional portfolios exposed to digital assets.
