Trump Media Files Bitcoin, Ether and Cronos Crypto ETFs with SEC

News • Investment • Score: 6/10

Truth Social Funds, the cryptocurrency investment arm of Trump Media & Technology Group, filed registration statements with the SEC on February 13, 2026, for two digital asset exchange-traded funds: the Truth Social Cronos Yield Maximizer ETF and the Truth Social Bitcoin and Ether ETF. Crypto.com will serve as the digital asset custodian, liquidity provider, and staking services provider for both funds, while Yorkville America Equities will act as investment adviser with a 0.95% annual management fee. The Cronos fund will provide exposure to the Cronos token plus staking rewards, while the Bitcoin and Ether fund will offer combined exposure to both cryptocurrencies including Ether staking returns. The funds cannot commence operations or offer shares until the SEC declares the registration statement effective, marking Trump Media's expansion into cryptocurrency investment products.
