Nakamoto to Acquire David Bailey's Crypto Empire for $107M All-Stock Deal

Jack Inabinet • Bitcoin • Score: 7/10

Nakamoto Inc. (NASDAQ: NAKA) has announced a $107.3 million all-stock acquisition of BTC Inc. and UTXO Management, two Bitcoin-focused companies led by CEO David Bailey, with the transaction expected to close in the first quarter of 2026. The deal will issue approximately 363.5 million NAKA shares at $0.2951 per share to consolidate Bailey's media, events, and investment advisory operations under a single public entity positioned as a diversified Bitcoin operating company. This related-party transaction has drawn scrutiny from analysts and investors due to Bailey's leadership across all three entities, though a special committee of independent directors approved the deal with input from outside legal and financial advisors. The acquisition aims to strengthen Nakamoto's balance sheet through recurring earnings from BTC Inc. and UTXO while supporting future Bitcoin accumulation and strategic acquisitions as part of Bailey's stated vision to build a scaled Bitcoin ecosystem.
