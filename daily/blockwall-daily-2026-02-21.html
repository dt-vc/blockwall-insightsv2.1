Spot Bitcoin ETFs Post Five Consecutive Weeks of Outflows Reaching $3.8B

News • Bitcoin • Score: 7/10

Spot Bitcoin ETFs recorded $315.9 million in outflows last week, marking the fifth consecutive week of net withdrawals totaling $3.8 billion, driven by institutional de-risking amid geopolitical tensions and macroeconomic uncertainty. This trend reflects short-term portfolio adjustments by hedge funds and leveraged investors rather than a complete exit from the asset, as evidenced by sustained redemptions from leaders like BlackRock's IBIT and Fidelity's FBTC. Despite the outflows, cumulative inflows since the January 2024 launch remain robust at over $54 billion, underscoring enduring long-term institutional demand for Bitcoin exposure. Ether ETFs have mirrored this pressure with their own negative flows, highlighting a broader caution in crypto allocations.
