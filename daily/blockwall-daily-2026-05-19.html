🔥 Key Developments (10)

Echo Protocol suffers $76 million exploit in eBTC minting attack on Monad

CoinDesk • Security • Score: 9/10

Echo Protocol, a Bitcoin‑focused DeFi platform on the Monad blockchain, suffered a major security breach. An attacker who obtained a compromised admin key minted roughly 1,000 eBTC tokens valued at about $76‑$77 million. The exploit also involved borrowing $3.45 million in WBTC and laundering the proceeds through Tornado Cash. After regaining control, the protocol burned the remaining illicit tokens to mitigate further loss.

The SEC wants to let newly public companies raise cash instantly in its biggest rule change in decades

CoinDesk • Regulation • Score: 8/10

The U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission has proposed its most extensive overhaul of IPO and public‑company rules in more than two decades. The proposal would let companies that have just gone public use shelf registrations right away instead of waiting for a minimum public float. It also eliminates the previous $75 million float threshold that limited immediate capital‑raising. By reducing compliance burdens, the changes are expected to give mid‑size crypto firms faster and cheaper access to public‑market financing, a development that could be significant for institutional investors seeking new sources of liquidity.

Block by Block: How the rsETH Exploit Rattled Aave Lending Markets

Galaxy • Security • Score: 8/10

The rsETH exploit in mid‑April caused the largest shock to Aave V3 lending markets. The attack froze both stablecoin and WETH liquidity pools and drove utilization rates close to 100 percent. Within the first six hours, $1.8 billion of stablecoins withdrew from the platform, with USDT and RLUSD accounting for the largest outflows. The event highlights systemic risk for institutional exposure to DeFi lending protocols that rely on cross‑asset collateral.

Former BNY exec Anthony Moro bets tokenization will remake Wall Street

CoinDesk • Tokenization • Score: 7/10

Former BNY Mellon executive Anthony Moro has created NUVA, a marketplace built on Ethereum that is designed to be chain agnostic. NUVA aggregates about $19 billion of tokenized home‑equity lines of credit and Treasury securities and makes them available on decentralized finance protocols. By partnering with Figure Technologies and Animoca Brands the platform issues a yield‑bearing stablecoin that is structured as a registered security, aiming to use regulated tokenized assets to connect traditional finance with the crypto ecosystem.

Former CFTC chairman Timothy Massad says the United States is quietly exploring a central bank digital currency despite President Trump’s public opposition. He points to U.S. involvement in the Bank for International Settlements’ Project Agora as evidence of ongoing work. Even a mountain of T-bills won't save Tether and Circle from a sudden liquidity crisis, expert says

CoinDesk • Stablecoins • Score: 6/10

Union Investment’s head of digital assets, Christoph Hock, said that USDT and USDC do not qualify as true stablecoins because a portion of their reserves is held in gold and bitcoin, assets he described as speculative. He pointed to recent de‑pegging episodes, noting that USDC fell 13% from its dollar peg, as a sign of systemic liquidity risk for institutional investors. Hock added that even large holdings of Treasury bills would not be sufficient to prevent a sudden liquidity shortfall at Tether and Circle and that European regulators are tightening oversight of private stablecoins.

A DeFi exchange just became the first to offer equity perpetuals powered by Nasdaq data

CoinDesk • DeFi • Score: 7/10

Ostium, a decentralized finance exchange operating on the Arbitrum layer‑2 network, announced a partnership with Nasdaq. The collaboration enables Ostium to launch equity perpetual contracts that are powered by official Nasdaq market data, making it the first on‑chain platform to offer U.S. stock perpetuals with institutional‑grade data. This development expands the range of tradable assets for crypto traders and provides institutional investors a bridge between decentralized finance and traditional equity markets.

Polymarket unlocks $5 trillion private market for retail traders, previously reserved for elites

Polymarket has introduced crypto‑based prediction markets that allow retail participants to place bets on the achievement of milestones by privately held companies. The platform’s contracts are settled using data supplied by Nasdaq Private Market, which provides an independent source for outcome verification. By tokenising these events, Polymarket opens access to a private‑company market estimated at $5 trillion that was previously limited to elite investors. The resulting price‑discovery signals could give institutional investors early insight into startup performance and valuation trends.

Nasdaq’s market data will serve as the definitive resolution mechanism for pre‑IPO events traded on Polymarket’s platform. This on‑chain settlement framework allows participants to hedge or speculate on unicorn‑scale valuations before companies go public.
