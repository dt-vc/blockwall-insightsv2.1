Iran’s revenge: drones damage data centers for Amazon Web Services, reveal west’s Achilles Heel

Kelvin Chan,The Associated Press • Security • Score: 7/10

Drone strikes damaged multiple Amazon Web Services data centers across the Middle East, with two facilities in the United Arab Emirates directly struck and another site in Bahrain sustaining infrastructure damage during retaliatory Iranian military operations following Operation Epic Fury. The attacks disrupted power systems and caused structural damage that impaired two of three data center sites in AWS's UAE region, forcing the company to urge customers to activate disaster recovery plans and migrate critical workloads out of the region. This incident marks the first publicly confirmed case of a hyperscale U.S. commercial data center being physically affected in real-world combat, demonstrating how precision drone technology has made critical digital infrastructure vulnerable in geopolitically contested regions. For institutional investors, the attack underscores systemic risks to cloud service providers operating in volatile regions and raises questions about the resilience of distributed infrastructure models that underpin global digital economies.
