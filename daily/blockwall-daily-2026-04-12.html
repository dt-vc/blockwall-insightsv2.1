Bhutan Bitcoin ‘sell-off’ gathers pace, amid crypto mining halt claims

DL News • Bitcoin • Score: 6/10

Bhutan’s sovereign wealth fund has sold roughly $216 million worth of Bitcoin, reducing its Bitcoin position by about 70% since October 2024. The divestment occurs as reports indicate that the country’s hydropower‑backed crypto mining operations have been halted. The mining shutdown has resulted in mining revenue staying below $100,000 for more than a year, raising concerns for investors about the fund’s exposure to crypto assets.

Iran war, debanking drive commodity traders toward stablecoins, says Haycen CEO

CoinDesk • Stablecoins • Score: 6/10

Haycen CEO Luke Sully reports that banks are withdrawing from commodity trade‑finance activities tied to Iran. As a result, commodity traders are increasingly turning to stablecoins such as USDT to settle cross‑border transactions. This behavior underscores the growing role of stablecoins as a liquidity and settlement layer within the broader $2 trillion non‑bank trade‑finance market, a development that could reshape financing flows for institutional participants.
