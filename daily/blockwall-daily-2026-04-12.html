Bhutan Bitcoin ‘sell-off’ gathers pace, amid crypto mining halt claims

DL News • Bitcoin • Score: 6/10

Bhutan’s sovereign wealth fund has sold roughly $216 million of Bitcoin, reducing its exposure by about 70 % since October 2024. The divestment occurs as the nation’s hydropower‑backed mining operations are reported to have halted, with mining revenue falling below $100,000 for more than a year. Iran war, debanking drive commodity traders toward stablecoins, says Haycen CEO

CoinDesk • Stablecoins • Score: 6/10
