Bitcoin Heads For Worst Quarter Since 2018 With 22% Drop

News • Bitcoin • Score: 6/10

Bitcoin has declined approximately 22% year-to-date in Q1 2026, trading near $68,670 after opening the year at $87,700, potentially marking the worst quarterly performance since 2018. Despite the sharp pullback, institutional investors remain selectively constructive, with survey data showing that 70% of institutional respondents view Bitcoin as undervalued and nearly half would buy additional positions if prices dropped further. The decline reflects broader macro headwinds and liquidity concerns rather than a structural breakdown, as institutional positioning has shifted toward defensive options strategies and large-cap exposure amid geopolitical uncertainty. Market observers note that while Q1 volatility is historically common in crypto markets, the underlying institutional adoption and halving-cycle dynamics could support a potential recovery later in 2026.
