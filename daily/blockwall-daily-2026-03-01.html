Vitalik: Ethereum Smart Accounts Coming in Hegota Fork

News • Ethereum • Score: 7/10

Ethereum co-founder Vitalik Buterin announced that smart accounts, enabled by account abstraction via EIP-8141, will launch in the Hegota hard fork scheduled for the second half of 2026. This upgrade follows the Glamsterdam fork in early 2026 and introduces flexible gas payments, enhanced privacy, and features like FOCIL for censorship resistance, alongside potential Verkle Trees to cut node hardware needs and boost decentralization. For institutional investors, these improvements promise scalable infrastructure supporting high-volume Layer 2 transactions, reduced centralization risks, and broader network participation, aligning with Ethereum's semiannual upgrade cadence for steady growth.
