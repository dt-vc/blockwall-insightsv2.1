Crypto startups raised $5 billion in Q1 — here are the top 10 funding rounds

DL News • Investment • Score: 7/10

Crypto startups raised roughly $5 billion in Q1 2026, representing a 16% decline from the prior year but still indicating robust investor appetite. The largest share of capital went to prediction‑market platforms which secured about $1.7 billion, while payments and trading‑infrastructure firms captured the next biggest portions. Among the ten biggest rounds Kalshi attracted $1 billion and Polymarket raised $600 million, underscoring continued institutional focus on high‑growth verticals within the crypto ecosystem.

Tokenised finance is a double-edged sword without proper oversight, IMF warns

DL News • Tokenization • Score: 6/10

The International Monetary Fund has warned that the rapid tokenisation of financial assets could increase systemic risk when oversight and policy frameworks are insufficient. It notes that faster settlement and automated market structures may compress the window for regulatory intervention during periods of stress. Major Wall Street participants including BlackRock, JP Morgan and the New York Stock Exchange are actively pursuing tokenised securities offerings. Institutional investors should monitor the development of regulatory safeguards as the speed of tokenised transactions grows.
