Tether May Delay Fundraising If Demand Falls Short at $500B Valuation

Crypto startups raised $5 billion in Q1 — here are the top 10 funding rounds

DL News • Investment • Score: 7/10

Crypto startups secured roughly $5 billion in funding during Q1 2026, representing a 16 percent decline from the same quarter a year earlier but still reflecting robust investor appetite. The largest share of capital went to prediction‑market platforms, which attracted about $1.7 billion, while payments and trading‑infrastructure firms captured the next biggest portions. Among the ten biggest rounds, Kalshi raised $1 billion and Polymarket secured $600 million. The distribution of funds underscores continued institutional confidence in niche crypto verticals despite a modest slowdown in overall venture activity.

Tokenised finance is a double-edged sword without proper oversight, IMF warns

DL News • Tokenization • Score: 6/10

The International Monetary Fund has warned that the rapid tokenisation of financial assets could increase systemic risk when oversight and policy frameworks are insufficient. Major Wall Street participants including BlackRock JP Morgan and the NYSE are accelerating efforts to issue tokenised securities. The Fund cautions that faster settlement and automated market structures may limit regulators’ ability to act during periods of stress, raising concerns for institutional investors.
