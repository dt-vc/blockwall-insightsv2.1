🔥 Key Developments (12)

IREN lands $3.65 billion A-rated financing for Microsoft AI buildout

The Block • Infrastructure • Score: 9/10

IREN has secured a $3.65 billion A-rated financing package to fund the purchase of graphics processing units needed for its contract to supply Microsoft’s AI cloud services. The financing, which includes a $2.1 billion private placement and a $1.55 billion delayed-draw loan, provides roughly 96 percent of the $5.81 billion total spend and carries a blended borrowing cost of 6 percent. The deal follows a recent $3 billion convertible notes offering and underscores IREN’s role in scaling hardware capacity for large-scale AI workloads, a point of interest for institutional investors monitoring exposure to AI infrastructure financing.

Kelp DAO Recovery Hopes Fade as Hacker Launders About $220 Million

Cointelegraph • Security • Score: 9/10

A hacker has laundered roughly $220 million from the Kelp DAO exploit, raising total losses to about $293 million. The scale of the theft has eroded expectations of a financial recovery for the decentralized autonomous organization. In response, Kelp DAO, Solv Protocol and Tydro have migrated from the compromised LayerZero bridge to Chainlink’s Cross‑Chain Interoperability Protocol to safeguard future cross‑chain transactions.

Aave overhauls listing standards after $230 Million rsETH exploit exposed bridge risks

CoinDesk • DeFi • Score: 9/10

Aave, a major decentralized finance lending platform, announced a sweeping overhaul of its asset‑listing standards after a $230 million rsETH exploit. The attack exploited a verification failure in the LayerZero bridge and minted 116,500 unbacked rsETH tokens on Ethereum, highlighting hidden bridge risks. In response, Aave will tighten risk assessments for bridges, oracles, custodians and operational security to protect institutional and retail users.

Unable to recover from roughly $50 million hack, Radiant Capital is winding down

The Block • Security • Score: 8/10

Radiant Capital is an omnichain DeFi money market protocol that operated on multiple blockchains. A recent exploit that affected its Arbitrum and BNB Chain deployments resulted in an estimated $50 million loss. The DAO announced that, lacking a viable path forward, it will wind down the platform, raising concerns for liquidity providers and institutional investors with exposure to the protocol.

Anthropic, a leading AI developer, disclosed a $65bn fundraising round that lifts its post‑money valuation to near $1tn. The size of the raise highlights the scale of capital flowing to US‑based AI firms compared with the more modest funding environment in Europe. Investors are watching the debate over how the influx of US AI capital could reshape the European tech landscape and influence cross‑border competitive dynamics.

Mecka AI raises $60 million to train robots with human data sourced from body sensors and iPhones

Fortune • Investment • Score: 8/10

Mecka AI has secured $60 million in new funding, comprising a $25 million Series A and a $35 million follow‑on round. The round was led by Framework Ventures, a venture‑capital firm that focuses on crypto‑related investments. Debate on CLARITY Act Continues this Week as US Senate Returns

Cointelegraph • Regulation • Score: 7/10

U.S. senators are preparing to merge multiple drafts of the CLARITY Act, a bill that would establish a new market‑structure framework for the cryptocurrency sector. The legislation is expected to be brought to a vote as early as August, pending final consolidation. Lawmakers are encountering resistance to the bill’s ethics provisions, which critics say could affect compliance costs for crypto firms. If enacted, the CLARITY Act would alter how crypto companies manage interest on user deposits and on stablecoin balances, and it arrives as the comment period for the related GENIUS Act is nearing its end.

Florida sues OpenAI and CEO Sam Altman over allegations of marketing ChatGPT despite serious risks of user safety

Fortune • Regulation • Score: 7/10

Florida Attorney General James Uthmeier has filed the first state‑led lawsuit against OpenAI and its chief executive Sam Altman, alleging that the company marketed its ChatGPT product despite known safety concerns. The complaint claims OpenAI concealed warnings about the technology, collected data from minors and encouraged self‑harm, and it seeks accountability under Florida consumer‑protection statutes. The action introduces regulatory and litigation risk for investors in AI firms, highlighting potential exposure to consumer‑protection enforcement and reputational damage.

The platform also permits fractional share purchases starting at $5, expanding the addressable market for smaller capital allocations. In parallel, Binance introduced bStocks, a tokenization service that creates BNB‑Chain tokens that represent equity positions and can be integrated with decentralized finance protocols, offering institutional investors a bridge between traditional equities and blockchain‑based liquidity.

Why Amy Lee, the niece of Singapore’s first prime minister, helped launch a crypto-friendly bank

Fortune • Enterprise • Score: 6/10

Amy Lee, the niece of Singapore’s first prime minister, played a key role in launching Singapore Gulf Bank (SGB) and now chairs its advisory board. SGB is a Bahrain‑licensed digital bank that integrates fiat and cryptocurrency services. The bank is a joint venture between Whampoa Group and Bahrain’s sovereign wealth fund Mumtalakat and is positioned to serve the expanding Asia‑Middle East trade corridor. In 2025 SGB reported $4 billion in deposits and $12 billion in transaction volume, indicating strong demand for blended financial solutions among institutional clients.

Binance Launches Tokenized US Equities with 24/5 Trading

Cointelegraph • Tokenization • Score: 7/10

Binance has launched tokenized US equities that can be traded on a 24‑hour, five‑day schedule. The initial catalog comprises 7,000 stocks that are purchasable with USDC and other stablecoins. Binance intends to add tokenized stocks and ETFs under the bStocks label once it receives regulatory approval. The service also offers Fully Paid Securities Lending so participants can earn passive income, mirroring similar tokenized‑stock programs from Bitget, Kraken and Bitpanda.

Anchorage Launches Settlement Network for Institutional Crypto Trading

Cointelegraph • Infrastructure • Score: 6/10

Anchorage Digital has introduced a blockchain‑based settlement network designed for institutional cryptocurrency trading. The network’s first integration is with the Spotex platform, allowing institutional participants to settle trades on a distributed ledger. This launch follows recent collaborations among the Depository Trust & Clearing Corporation, Digital Asset, Fireblocks and several banking firms. The development reflects a broader acceleration of infrastructure that supports tokenized assets for large‑scale investors.

Dear Founders, Investors, and Friends, The past month has reminded us how quickly the landscape can shift from massive capital deployment to urgent risk mitigation. IREN just announced a $3.65 billion A‑rated financing package that will fund the GPUs needed for its contract with Microsoft’s AI cloud services. By tightening risk assessments for bridges, oracles, custodians and operational security, Aave aims to protect both institutional and retail participants from similar attacks. These events raise a fundamental question for all of us building in Web3: how can we balance the lure of rapid growth with the need for robust security and transparent risk frameworks?
