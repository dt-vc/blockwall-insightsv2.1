US spot bitcoin ETFs bleed $410 million as BTC slips below $66,000

Brian Danga • Bitcoin • Score: 7/10

US spot Bitcoin ETFs recorded $410 million in outflows on February 12, 2026, marking a second straight day of heavy selling and a fourth consecutive week of losses amid Bitcoin's decline to around $66,000-$68,000 from its October 2025 peak near $127,000. This reversal follows $5.7 billion in net withdrawals from November 2025 to January 2026, driven by stronger-than-expected US jobs data that dims prospects for Federal Reserve rate cuts and prompts institutional de-risking. For institutional investors, these outflows signal fading demand for Bitcoin exposure, amplify short-term supply pressure equivalent to multi-day mined supply, and heighten volatility as funds liquidate holdings to meet redemptions. Standard Chartered's cut to a $100,000 2026 price target underscores the macro risks, with average ETF buyers now facing losses from entries around $90,000.
