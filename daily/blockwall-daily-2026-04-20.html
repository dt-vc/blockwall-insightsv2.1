Why cross-chain bridges are DeFi’s weakest link after $293m Kelp DAO hack

DL News • Security • Score: 9/10

An attacker used a forged cross‑chain message to drain $293 million of rsETH from Kelp DAO, exposing a single‑validator design flaw in LayerZero’s messaging system. This loss, together with a prior $286 million breach at Drift, pushes total DeFi hacks this month above $550 million. The combined incidents underscore cross‑chain bridges as a critical security vulnerability that institutional investors must evaluate when allocating capital to DeFi protocols.

Kelp DAO hits back at LayerZero for trying to shift the blame after a massive exploit

CoinDesk • Security • Score: 9/10

Kelp DAO attributes the $290 million rsETH bridge hack to LayerZero’s default single‑verifier architecture. LayerZero counters that Kelp DAO disregarded the security guidance it had provided. The conflict stems from a compromised LayerZero verifier that let attackers siphon 116,500 rsETH and has led LayerZero to suspend all single‑verifier bridges, raising concerns for institutional investors about cross‑chain bridge risk.

Investors pull $15bn from DeFi as latest hack sparks security fears

DL News • DeFi • Score: 9/10

North Korean hackers stole roughly $600 million from DeFi applications, including $294 million from Kelp DAO. In response, investors withdrew more than $15 billion of total value locked from the sector. The outflows hit major lenders such as Aave, Morpho and Sky and underscore rising security concerns for institutional participants.

Tether Takes 8.2% Stake in Antalpha, Backs Bitcoin Mining Finance

How crypto exchange Grinex shuttering poses ‘serious damage’ to Russian shadow financial economy

DL News • Regulation • Score: 6/10

Grinex, a crypto exchange that facilitated trading of Russia’s sanctioned stablecoin A7A5, has announced it will cease operations. The decision follows a cyber‑attack that resulted in the theft of more than 1 billion rubles, roughly $13 million. By closing, Grinex eliminates a primary channel that allowed conversion of rubles into foreign currency. The loss of this conduit is expected to deepen the pressure of Western sanctions on the Russian shadow financial system, raising compliance and liquidity concerns for investors with exposure to the region.

Ripple building government bonds trading platform with $89bn South Korean insurance giant

DL News • Tokenization • Score: 6/10

Ripple is partnering with South Korean insurer Kyobo Life Insurance, a $89bn insurance giant, to develop a blockchain‑based platform that tokenises government bonds for real‑time trading. The proof‑of‑concept pilot will be executed on Ripple’s institutional blockchain infrastructure. The platform is designed to reduce settlement times from days to seconds. The initiative follows a surge in XRP trading volume on South Korean exchanges, underscoring growing institutional interest in digital‑asset solutions.

'Basis traders' are done dragging down Bitcoin price, says investment adviser

DL News • Bitcoin • Score: 5/10

Investment adviser Alex Blume of Two Prime says the long‑running Bitcoin basis trade is winding down, removing a key source of downward pressure on the cryptocurrency. The basis trade historically involved selling Bitcoin futures while holding spot Bitcoin, which has helped keep prices lower. Concurrently, treasury firm Strategy has accelerated its Bitcoin buying, acquiring roughly 24,800 BTC, valued at about $2.7 billion, in early April. The combination of reduced basis‑trade selling and increased institutional buying could provide fresh price support that is relevant for large‑scale investors.

Post-Quantum Readiness on the XRP Ledger

Ripple • Infrastructure • Score: 5/10

Ripple announced a multi‑phase roadmap to make the XRP Ledger quantum‑resistant, aiming for full readiness by 2028. The plan begins with immediate testing of quantum‑safe cryptography and then moves to a hybrid rollout that blends existing and new security mechanisms. A “Quantum‑Day” contingency is defined to enable a secure migration and the effort is being carried out in collaboration with Project Eleven, underscoring Ripple’s commitment to protecting institutional assets against future quantum threats.
