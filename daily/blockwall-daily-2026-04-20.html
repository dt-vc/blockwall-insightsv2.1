Why cross-chain bridges are DeFi’s weakest link after $293m Kelp DAO hack

DL News • Security • Score: 9/10

An attacker used a forged cross‑chain message to drain $293 million of rsETH from Kelp DAO, revealing a single‑validator design flaw in LayerZero’s messaging system. The same month also saw a $286 million loss at Drift, bringing total DeFi hacks to over $550 million. These incidents highlight cross‑chain bridges as a critical security vulnerability that institutional investors must scrutinize when allocating capital to DeFi protocols.

Kelp DAO hits back at LayerZero for trying to shift the blame after a massive exploit

CoinDesk • Security • Score: 9/10

Kelp DAO attributes the $290 million rsETH bridge hack to LayerZero’s default single‑verifier architecture. LayerZero counters that Kelp DAO failed to follow the security guidance it provided. The conflict revolves around a compromised LayerZero verifier that enabled attackers to siphon 116,500 rsETH. In response, LayerZero has suspended all single‑verifier bridges, highlighting the broader risk to cross‑chain liquidity providers.

Investors pull $15bn from DeFi as latest hack sparks security fears

The episode highlights heightened security concerns that could affect capital allocation decisions by institutional participants.

Post-Quantum Readiness on the XRP Ledger

Ripple • Infrastructure • Score: 5/10

Ripple announced a multi‑phase roadmap to make the XRP Ledger quantum‑resistant. The initiative aims for full quantum‑readiness by 2028, reflecting concerns that future quantum computers could threaten current cryptographic safeguards. The plan begins with immediate testing of quantum‑safe cryptography, followed by a hybrid rollout that integrates new algorithms alongside existing ones. A “Quantum‑Day” contingency, developed in collaboration with Project Eleven, will enable a secure migration of ledger assets if a quantum threat emerges.
