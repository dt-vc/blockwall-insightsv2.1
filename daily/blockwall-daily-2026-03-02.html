Dow futures plunge nearly 500 points as U.S. attack on Iran sends oil prices soaring, while first American casualties reported

Jason Ma,Amanda Gerut • Other • Score: 7/10

Dow futures plunged nearly 500 points after U.S. and Israeli attacks on Iranian facilities prompted Iran to warn shipping away from the Strait of Hormuz. Insurers withdrew coverage, halting tanker traffic through this critical chokepoint that carries 15-20% of global oil supply, or about 20 million barrels per day. This real-time supply shock renders much of OPEC+ spare capacity inaccessible, as bypass routes like Saudi Arabia's East-West pipeline and UAE's Fujairah line offer only partial mitigation of around 5-7 million barrels per day. Institutional investors face heightened volatility with oil prices potentially exceeding $100 per barrel amid escalating conflict risks.
