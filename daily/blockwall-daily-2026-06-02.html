Robinhood closes $180 million WonderFi acquisition, entering Canadian crypto market

The Block • Enterprise • Score: 9/10

Robinhood completed a $180 million acquisition of Toronto‑based WonderFi, giving it control of the regulated Canadian platforms Bitbuy and Coinsquare. The transaction adds roughly 300,000 Canadian users to Robinhood’s crypto business. It also raises the number of funded customers outside the United States to more than one million. The move expands Robinhood’s international crypto footprint and provides a regulated entry point into the Canadian market, which is relevant for institutional investors seeking diversified exposure.

Radiant to Wind Down After Failing to Recover From 2024 Hack

Cointelegraph • DeFi • Score: 9/10

Radiant is a cross‑chain DeFi liquidity platform that was hit by a large hack attributed to North Korea’s Lazarus Group in October 2024. The attack reduced its total value locked from $386.8 million to roughly $5 million and caused the RDNT token to fall sharply. The team announced that the protocol will move into a maintenance mode with no further development, effectively winding down the project and raising risk considerations for investors exposed to its liquidity and token.

Mt. Gox Moves $739M in Bitcoin for First Time in Two Months

Cointelegraph • Bitcoin • Score: 8/10

Mt. Gox transferred roughly $739 million worth of Bitcoin after a two‑month lull, marking the first movement of its holdings in that period. The transfer has revived speculation that the exchange may soon begin creditor payouts. Mt. Gox still controls about 34,500 BTC, valued at approximately $2.4 billion, with a repayment deadline of Oct 31 2026. Recent BTC sales by Strategy and ProCap Financial have pushed Bitcoin below $70 000, indicating potential sell pressure that could affect the timing and pricing of any distributions.

Mt. Gox moves 10,422 bitcoin worth $739 million to a new wallet as deadline nears

CoinDesk • Bitcoin • Score: 7/10

Mt. Gox transferred 10,422 bitcoin, valued at roughly $739 million, to a newly generated wallet. The transfer occurs ahead of the October 31 2026 deadline for creditor repayments. This move follows previous administrative transfers made by the exchange. Institutional investors should note that the additional supply could increase selling pressure while Bitcoin prices have recently slipped below $71 k.

Veda brings the vault stack behind Kraken DeFi Earn to Privy

The Block • Infrastructure • Score: 6/10

Veda is extending its non‑custodial BoringVault infrastructure to the developer teams that use Privy’s embedded wallet platform. Privy serves more than 2,000 developer teams, giving them access to Veda’s vault stack through a self‑serve API. The vault stack previously powered institution‑only products such as Kraken DeFi Earn and EtherFi. By making the technology a standard, scalable offering, Veda aims to enable fintech startups to integrate secure, non‑custodial yield solutions.

MoneyGram launches stablecoin on Stellar (XLM), joining rush toward digital dollar payments

CoinDesk • Stablecoins • Score: 6/10

MoneyGram has launched its own U.S. dollar‑backed stablecoin, MGUSD, on the Stellar (XLM) blockchain. The token is initially available to U.S. users with a planned global rollout. MGUSD is issued through Stripe’s Bridge, utilizes smart contracts from M0 and is supported by Fireblocks wallet services. The move positions MoneyGram to compete in the rapidly expanding digital‑dollar cross‑border payments market, a sector of growing interest to institutional investors.

Grayscale’s Hyperliquid ETF Likely to Launch This week

Cointelegraph • Investment • Score: 6/10

Grayscale is expected to launch an exchange‑traded fund that will track Hyperliquid’s HYPE token later this week. The product will join similar offerings from 21Shares and Bitwise, which together have attracted $140 million of net inflows. The launch coincides with Hyperliquid reporting more than $170 billion of monthly trading volume and HYPE reaching a new all‑time high that lifts its market capitalisation to $16.7 billion.

Stablecoin remittance app goes live in UK after $1m in transactions during silent beta

Sifted • Stablecoins • Score: 6/10

Yousend has launched its stablecoin‑based remittance application in the United Kingdom and Canada. The platform aims to settle cross‑border transfers in under 15 seconds, a speed demonstrated during a silent beta that processed more than $1 million in transactions. Regulatory clearance has been obtained from the UK Financial Conduct Authority, Canada’s FINTRAC and the Central Bank of Nigeria. The service is supported by a roster of prominent crypto investors, positioning it as a potentially scalable solution for institutional remittance needs.
