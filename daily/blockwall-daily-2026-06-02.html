🔥 Key Developments (9)

Robinhood closes $180 million WonderFi acquisition, entering Canadian crypto market

The Block • Enterprise • Score: 9/10

Robinhood has completed a $180 million acquisition of WonderFi, a Toronto‑based crypto firm. The transaction gives Robinhood control of the Canadian regulated exchanges Bitbuy and Coinsquare. The addition of roughly 300,000 Canadian users lifts Robinhood’s funded customer base outside the United States to more than one million. This expansion broadens Robinhood’s international crypto footprint and positions the platform for growth in the Canadian market.

Mt. Gox moves 10,422 bitcoin worth $739 million to a new wallet as deadline nears

CoinDesk • Bitcoin • Score: 7/10

Mt. Gox transferred 10,422 bitcoin, valued at roughly $739 million, to a newly generated address. The transfer occurs ahead of the creditor repayment deadline of October 31 2026. This move follows previous administrative transfers made by the exchange’s trustee. Analysts note that moving such a large amount could increase selling pressure in a market where Bitcoin has recently slipped below $71,000.

Veda brings the vault stack behind Kraken DeFi Earn to Privy

The Block • Infrastructure • Score: 6/10

Veda is making its non‑custodial BoringVault infrastructure available through a self‑serve API to the more than 2,000 developer teams that use Privy’s embedded wallet platform. The integration allows fintech startups to access the same vault stack that previously powered Kraken DeFi Earn and EtherFi. By turning a bespoke, institution‑only solution into a standardized product, Veda aims to broaden adoption of secure, composable DeFi earning tools across the broader crypto ecosystem.

What next for BTC prices as Bitcoin slides below $70,000 on Strategy's sale

CoinDesk • Bitcoin • Score: 6/10

Bitcoin fell below $70,000, registering a 3.8% decline over the past 24 hours. The move followed Strategy’s disclosure of its first publicized sale of 32 BTC for $2.5 million. Strategy indicated the proceeds will fund preferred‑stock distributions, a corporate financing activity that added selling pressure. The price drop occurs amid a market lacking bullish catalysts and experiencing negative ETF inflows, factors that institutional investors monitor closely.

MoneyGram launches stablecoin on Stellar (XLM), joining rush toward digital dollar payments

CoinDesk • Stablecoins • Score: 6/10

MoneyGram has launched a U.S. dollar‑backed stablecoin called MGUSD on the Stellar (XLM) blockchain. The token is issued through Stripe’s Bridge, uses smart‑contract technology from M0 and is supported by Fireblocks wallet services. The rollout begins with U.S. customers and is intended to expand globally, giving MoneyGram a foothold in the rapidly expanding digital‑dollar cross‑border payments market.

CME

The Block • Infrastructure • Score: 6/10

CME Group has introduced a continuous 24/7 crypto futures and options market. In its opening weekend the platform recorded more than 7,200 contracts and approximately $50 million in notional value. The launch also includes a 24/7 bitcoin volatility futures contract. These products extend CME’s regulated derivatives offering and give institutional investors round‑the‑clock access to crypto exposure.

Stablecoin remittance app goes live in UK after $1m in transactions during silent beta

Sifted • Stablecoins • Score: 6/10

Yousend has launched a stablecoin‑based remittance application in the United Kingdom and Canada. The platform claims settlement times of less than fifteen seconds, a speed demonstrated during a silent beta that processed over one million dollars in transactions. It operates under regulatory approvals from the Financial Conduct Authority, FINTRAC and the Central Bank of Nigeria. Institutional backing from prominent crypto investors underscores its potential relevance for cross‑border payment infrastructure.

The U.S. and Europe feared the Iran conflict would curtail the Gulf's appetite for global investments. The opposite is true

Fortune • Investment • Score: 5/10

Despite U.S. and European concerns that the Iran conflict would dampen Gulf investment appetite, sovereign wealth funds in the Gulf have actually accelerated dealmaking. In the first half of 2024 they deployed roughly $26 bn across a broad set of transactions. Their capital is being spread into developed markets, China and other emerging economies while also being redirected toward domestic projects such as tourism, clean‑energy infrastructure and smart‑city initiatives. This pattern of continued outflow and diversification signals resilience that institutional investors should monitor for partnership and co‑investment opportunities.

Strategy (MSTR) sold bitcoin in late May, and told the market in June. Here's how Polymarket bettors are fighting over when it counts.

CoinDesk • DeFi • Score: 5/10

Strategy (MSTR) sold 32 BTC between May 26 and May 31, a transaction valued at about $79 million. The sale was disclosed in an 8‑K filing on June 1, prompting a Polymarket contract to ask whether the trade should be treated as a May 31 event or a June 1 disclosure. UMA token holders will vote on the contract resolution, a decision that could affect how similar corporate crypto sales are reported and priced in the market.
