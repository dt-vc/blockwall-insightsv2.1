North Korea stole $293m in crypto. Then it used a victim's products to launder the proceeds

DL News • Security • Score: 9/10

North Korean state‑backed hackers stole $293 million from the Kelp DAO by exploiting a vulnerability in the LayerZero cross‑chain messaging protocol. After the exfiltration they used the same LayerZero infrastructure to launder a portion of the stolen funds. The theft is part of a broader wave of on‑chain thefts totaling $579 million in less than 20 days. It represents the first documented instance of a blockchain bridge serving both as the attack vector and as a laundering channel, underscoring heightened operational risk for institutional crypto exposure.

Weekly Top Stories: Kelp Needs Help After $290m Exploit

Galaxy • DeFi • Score: 9/10

The $290 million exploit of KelpDAO’s LayerZero bridge drained rsETH from an Ethereum escrow, marking the largest DeFi hack of 2026. The attacker moved the stolen ETH off‑chain and swapped it for Bitcoin via THORChain, triggering a cascade of liquidity freezes and a 45% drop in Aave’s TVL. The incident underscores systemic risk in DeFi lending and collateral markets, prompting calls for additional capital support for KelpDAO.

Bitcoin developer wants to hard fork the network — and give away Satoshi coins

DL News • Bitcoin • Score: 8/10

Long‑time Bitcoin developer Paul Sztorc has announced a hard fork of the Bitcoin network named eCash. The eCash fork is scheduled for August and would add drivechains, a layer‑2 scaling solution, to the protocol. The proposal also includes a plan to reallocate up to half of the original coins attributed to Satoshi Nakamoto to fund ongoing development. This approach is expected to generate controversy among the community and could have implications for institutional exposure to Bitcoin governance and funding mechanisms.

DeFi United: United We Stand, Divided We Fall

Tiger Research • DeFi • Score: 9/10

A single‑node vulnerability in KelpDAO’s LayerZero bridge allowed $292 M of rsETH to be minted without collateral. The unauthenticated rsETH was then used on Aave to borrow $190 M, which precipitated a $13 B total value locked collapse across DeFi protocols. In response, a self‑organized rescue coalition called DeFi United has launched a fundraising campaign with a target of up to $100 M to compensate affected participants. As of the latest update the coalition has raised less than half of its goal, highlighting funding shortfalls for the remediation effort.

Prediction Market Battle Gets Closer to Supreme Court

Cointelegraph • Regulation • Score: 7/10

State gambling regulators in the United States have intensified legal actions against prediction‑market platforms. In response, the Commodity Futures Trading Commission has filed lawsuits in three states, moving the regulatory clash toward the Supreme Court. The core issue is whether platforms such as Kalshi, Robinhood and Coinbase may offer contracts tied to sports events, with the CFTC asserting its authority over such products.

DOJ charges US soldier with using classified information to win $400,000 in Polymarket Maduro capture bet

DL News • Regulation • Score: 6/10

The U.S. Department of Justice has charged Army Special Forces master sergeant Gannon Ken Van Dyke with exploiting classified intelligence to earn more than $400,000 on the Polymarket prediction‑market platform.
