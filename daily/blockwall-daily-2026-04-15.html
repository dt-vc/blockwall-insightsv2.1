‘AI-based super attacker’ threat looms as top crypto exchanges scramble for access to powerful Claude model

DL News • Security • Score: 7/10

Anthropic has released a new AI model called Claude Mythos that can autonomously discover zero‑day vulnerabilities. The capability has triggered a defensive scramble by leading crypto exchanges, including Coinbase and Binance, to seek access to the model. Security experts warn that the model’s ability to uncover previously unknown flaws could expose the entire crypto infrastructure to financial losses measured in the hundreds of millions to billions of dollars. Institutional investors are therefore monitoring the situation closely as the threat could impact the risk profile of digital asset holdings.

Apple defends App Store record after fake Ledger app drains $10m in Bitcoin and Solana

DL News • Security • Score: 6/10

A counterfeit Ledger wallet application was listed on the Apple App Store and was used to siphon almost $10 million in Bitcoin and Solana from more than 50 users. Apple responded by removing the fraudulent app and suspending the developer’s account. Investigations traced the stolen assets through the KuCoin exchange and a centralized mixing service. The incident highlights the importance of rigorous app vetting and monitoring for institutional investors exposed to crypto custody solutions.

Elizabeth Warren slams Elon Musk over X Money’s rumored crypto feature

She warns that the feature could create consumer protection, national security, and financial stability risks, particularly if it integrates stablecoins. The letter signals heightened regulatory scrutiny of X as the platform moves toward broader financial services. Institutional investors should monitor the evolving oversight environment because it may affect X’s ability to operate as a de facto financial institution.

Bitwise Launchdx Avalanche ETF with Staking Exposure

Cointelegraph • Investment • Score: 6/10

Bitwise Asset Management has launched the BAVA spot Avalanche ETF on the NYSE, providing institutional investors direct exposure to the Avalanche blockchain. Approximately 70 % of the fund’s AVAX holdings are actively staked to generate yield, and the ETF trades at $25.50 per share with a 0.34 % sponsor fee that is waived for the first month on the first $500 M of assets. The product expands the growing suite of crypto‑focused exchange‑traded products, offering a regulated vehicle for yield‑enhanced exposure to a leading layer‑1 protocol.

Iran's Bitcoin toll 'virtually impossible' to collect, says policy institute

DL News • Payments • Score: 5/10

Iran has announced plans to accept Bitcoin for toll payments through the Strait of Hormuz. Analysts say the current Bitcoin network and its Lightning layer lack the speed, cost efficiency, and liquidity needed for large‑scale toll settlements. The policy reflects Tehran’s broader strategy to adopt censorship‑resistant financial rails. However, stablecoins remain vulnerable to freezes by issuers such as Tether, highlighting ongoing regulatory risk for digital‑currency based infrastructure.
