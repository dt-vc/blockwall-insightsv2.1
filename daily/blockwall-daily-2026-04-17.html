🔥 Key Developments (12)

Drift degen sues Circle, alleging stablecoin giant ‘did nothing’ during $295m hack

DL News • Security • Score: 9/10

A class‑action lawsuit has been filed alleging that Circle was negligent in the aftermath of a hack that stole $295 million from the Solana‑based DeFi platform Drift, which is linked to North‑Korean actors. The complaint asserts that Circle’s USDC stablecoin and its cross‑chain transfer protocol (CCTP) enabled the attackers to move the stolen assets onto the Ethereum network and convert them into Ether. Circle has responded that its role is limited to complying with court orders and it cannot unilaterally intervene in the transfer of funds. Institutional investors are watching the case because it could set precedent for liability and risk management of stablecoin issuers and cross‑chain infrastructure in the broader crypto ecosystem.

Weekly Top Stories: Tether bails out Drift in bid for Solana market share

Galaxy • DeFi • Score: 9/10

Tether is committing up to $147.5 million to stabilize the Drift Protocol, a Solana‑based decentralized finance platform that suffered a $295.7 million exploit. The rescue package comprises a $100 million revenue‑linked credit facility together with ecosystem grants and market‑maker loans designed to restore liquidity. As part of the remediation, Drift will transition its settlement layer from USDC to USDT, which could enhance its integration with Tether’s stablecoin ecosystem and support its market‑share ambitions on Solana.

Payward To Acquire CFTC-Regulated Crypto Derivatives Platform Bitnomial

Cointelegraph • Infrastructure • Score: 8/10

Payward, the parent company of the Kraken exchange, has signed a definitive agreement to acquire Bitnomial, a U.S. crypto derivatives platform that is fully licensed by the U.S. Commodity Futures Trading Commission. The transaction values Bitnomial’s equity at $20 billion. Through the deal Payward will obtain native infrastructure for spot‑margin, perpetual futures and options trading that can be offered to its U.S. client base. The acquisition also broadens Payward’s API‑based crypto services, giving it a regulated foothold in the U.S. derivatives market.

Russia Introduces Bill To Criminalize Unregistered Crypto Services

Cointelegraph • Regulation • Score: 7/10

The draft legislation prescribes penalties of up to five years in prison and fines for entities that fail to register. By tightening AML/KYC controls, the measure could compel exchanges and other crypto providers to obtain official licensing or cease operations, affecting institutional exposure to the Russian market.

Neo Co-Founder Proposes $461M Overhaul to End ‘Trust Me’ Governance

Cointelegraph • Enterprise • Score: 7/10

Neo co‑founder Da Hongfei has announced a $461 million treasury restructuring that would replace the current founder‑centric governance model with a Cayman‑registered foundation. The new structure would be overseen by a five‑member board and an independent supervisor to provide checks and balances. The plan also includes returning almost 50 million NEO tokens to the broader community and instituting transparent multi‑signature controls over treasury spending. Institutional investors view the changes as an effort to improve governance transparency and reduce concentration risk.
