Crypto analytics firm Elliptic lands $120 million as AI reshapes blockchain compliance

CoinDesk • Infrastructure • Score: 9/10

Elliptic, a London‑based blockchain analytics firm, announced a $120 million growth‑equity financing round. The round was led by One Peak and included investors Nasdaq Ventures, Deutsche Bank and the British Business Bank. The proceeds are earmarked for scaling AI‑driven monitoring tools that detect illicit activity on blockchain networks. This expansion comes as institutional demand for compliance solutions around stablecoins and tokenized assets intensifies due to heightened hack activity and regulatory scrutiny.

The facility is projected to triple the arm’s revenue and broaden its margin‑financing services for institutional clients. Concurrently XRP is testing key resistance levels, which analysts view as a sign of weakening seller pressure and a potentially more bullish price outlook.

JPMorgan (JPM) to launch new tokenized fund as Wall Street tokenization race heats up

CoinDesk • Tokenization • Score: 7/10

JPMorgan has filed a registration statement with the SEC to issue a tokenized U.S. Treasury money‑market fund built on the Ethereum blockchain. The fund is designed to satisfy the reserve‑backing requirements that the GENIUS Act imposes on stablecoins. This filing follows BlackRock’s recent submission for a tokenized Treasury product, indicating that major asset managers are moving toward blockchain‑based securities. The initiative signals a broader Wall Street effort to place real‑world assets on distributed ledger technology, which could enhance liquidity and settlement efficiency for institutional investors.

The CFTC is in talks with every major pro sports league to crack down on insider trading

CoinDesk • Regulation • Score: 7/10

The Commodity Futures Trading Commission is currently in discussions with every major U.S. professional sports league to establish data‑sharing arrangements aimed at detecting insider trading in sports‑related prediction markets. A memorandum of understanding for data sharing has already been executed with Major League Baseball. The CFTC says the cooperation will help the agency monitor and enforce federal securities laws in markets that bet on the outcomes of sporting events. The regulator is also pursuing legal action against states that attempt to block contracts for federally regulated events, underscoring its broader effort to standardize oversight across jurisdictions.

DTCC taps Chainlink for its tokenized collateral platform ahead of Q4 launch

CoinDesk • Tokenization • Score: 7/10

DTCC will integrate Chainlink’s Runtime Environment into its new Collateral AppChain, a Besu‑based platform that tokenizes collateral and automates pricing valuation margining and settlement around the clock. The integration builds on DTCC’s earlier Smart NAV pilots with JPMorgan and BNY Mellon and is intended to support a launch in October. By combining tokenization with Chainlink’s oracle technology, the service aims to provide institutional investors with continuous, reliable data for collateral management and could represent a significant enterprise adoption of blockchain and oracle solutions.

U.S.-listed spot XRP ETFs saw a net inflow of $25.8 million on Monday, the largest single‑day inflow since early January, bringing total inflows to $1.35 billion. At the same time Ripple completed a tokenized U.S. Treasury settlement on the XRP Ledger with JPMorgan, Mastercard and Ondo Finance, highlighting growing institutional use of the technology.

Aave launches binding Arbitrum vote to move $71 million in disputed ETH

CoinDesk • DeFi • Score: 7/10

Aave and other stakeholders have initiated a binding Arbitrum governance vote to transfer roughly 30,765 ETH, valued at about $71 million, to an address controlled by Aave. The vote is intended to comply with a U.S. court order that arose from a judgment involving North Korean terrorism creditors. The vote, scheduled to begin on May 15, follows the Kelp DAO hack that froze the assets and highlights complex legal questions about ownership and future use of the funds.

Ethereum news (ETH): Foundation unveils new 'Clear Signing' standard to stop users from approving malicious crypto transactions

CoinDesk • Ethereum • Score: 6/10

The Ethereum Foundation together with leading wallet developers has introduced a new security standard called Clear Signing, designated ERC‑7730. The standard replaces cryptic transaction data with human‑readable explanations to prevent blind‑signing attacks. A public registry of verified transaction descriptions is included to improve safety for mainstream and institutional users.
