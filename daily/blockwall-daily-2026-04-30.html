Vitalik-backed MegaETH sees token fall 55% on first day trading

DL News • Layer2 • Score: 8/10

MegaETH, an Ethereum layer‑2 network backed by Vitalik Buterin, launched its MEGA token and saw the price drop 55 percent on the first day of trading, reaching roughly $0.17. The project has raised about $108 million through token sales, a $10 million Echo round, venture funding and NFT sales. Despite the sizable capital raise, on‑chain activity on the network remains modest. The sharp price decline and limited usage are material considerations for institutional investors evaluating exposure to emerging layer‑2 protocols.

Democrat Senators question Howard Lutnick over Tether ties

DL News • Regulation • Score: 6/10

U.S. Senators Elizabeth Warren and Ron Wyden have sent a letter to Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick seeking clarification about a loan that Lutnick’s children’s trust received from the stablecoin issuer Tether. The request was made as Lutnick is in the process of selling his stake in the financial services firm Cantor Fitzgerald. Lawmakers say the timing raises potential conflict‑of‑interest concerns and could indicate undue influence by Tether on policy decisions. The inquiry adds to the broader regulatory focus on stablecoins and the need for transparency in relationships between crypto firms and U.S. officials.

Backing Fence: Rearchitecting Credit Infrastructure

Galaxy • Infrastructure • Score: 7/10

Fence has secured a $20 million Series A round led by Galaxy Ventures as part of a broader effort to rearchitect credit infrastructure with an automated platform for asset‑backed finance. The platform is designed to replace traditional manual servicing processes that dominate the market. Fence says the technology can lower borrowers’ cost of capital by as much as 40 percent and cut operational overhead by up to 80 percent. The company is already managing $1.4 billion in assets, indicating early traction for its solution.

Stablecoins Surpass Bitcoin in Latin America Crypto Purchases: Bitso Report

Cointelegraph • Stablecoins • Score: 6/10

Bitso’s adoption report indicates that stablecoins accounted for 40% of crypto purchases in Latin America, surpassing Bitcoin’s 18% share. The data reflects a growing demand for a digital dollar in economies experiencing high inflation. Companies such as Mercado Libre are responding by launching remittance products that rely on stablecoins, signaling expanding commercial use cases for the asset class.

Senator Warren questions Commerce Secretary Lutnick on Tether loan to family

CoinDesk • Regulation • Score: 6/10

U.S. Senators Elizabeth Warren and Ron Wyden have formally requested information from Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick and Tether CEO Paulo Ardoino. The letters focus on a reported loan that Tether allegedly extended to a family trust linked to Lutnick. Lawmakers are probing whether the loan creates a conflict of interest that could affect regulatory oversight of the stablecoin market. The inquiry underscores growing scrutiny of how major crypto issuers may influence U.S. policy decisions.

Bitcoin Cost Basis Cluster Forms Near $75K Support

Cointelegraph • Bitcoin • Score: 5/10

Bitcoin has closed above its adjusted realized price, meaning that many holders are now sitting in profit. This price action has created a cost‑basis cluster around the $75,000 level where the long‑term, short‑term and ETF cost bases converge. The $75K zone therefore acts as a key support area, with billions of long and short positions vulnerable to liquidation if the price falls below it, a dynamic that institutional investors monitor closely.
