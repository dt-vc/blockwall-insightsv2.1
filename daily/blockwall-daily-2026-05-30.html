🔥 Key Developments (8)

U.S. says it seized about $1 billion in Iranian crypto as pressure campaign expands

CoinDesk • Regulation • Score: 8/10

The U.S. Treasury disclosed that it has seized roughly $1 billion in cryptocurrency tied to Iran under Operation Economic Fury. The seizure is intended to disrupt the regime’s ability to finance illicit activities by cutting off digital funding channels. This action expands a wider sanctions effort that targets Iran’s shadow banking system, weapons procurement networks and overseas assets, signaling heightened enforcement risk for entities dealing with Iranian crypto flows.

SEC sues Privvy founder over $12.3 million crypto scheme as AI 'bots' turn out to be neither

The Block • Regulation • Score: 6/10

The SEC has filed a civil action against Nathan Fuller, the founder of Privvy Investments, accusing him of orchestrating a $12.3 million cryptocurrency fraud. The complaint alleges that Fuller promoted non‑existent AI trading bots and used fabricated licenses and insurance documents to attract investors. Prosecutors say he diverted a substantial portion of the raised capital for personal expenses and operated a Ponzi‑style payout structure. The case highlights regulatory scrutiny of AI‑linked crypto offerings and underscores the need for institutional investors to conduct rigorous due‑diligence on technology claims and sponsor credentials.

Cosmos-based Gravity Bridge drained of $5.4 million in suspected key compromise, researchers say

The Block • Security • Score: 6/10

The Gravity Bridge, a cross‑chain bridge built on the Cosmos ecosystem, experienced a security breach that led to the loss of roughly $5.4 million. Researchers say the theft appears to stem from a compromise of the private key that governs the bridge’s operations. The incident highlights the persistent vulnerability of cross‑chain bridge infrastructure and may prompt institutional investors to reassess exposure to similar protocols.

Grayscale says Hyperliquid could become a ‘financial services juggernaut’

CoinDesk • DeFi • Score: 5/10

Grayscale’s analyst report says Hyperliquid, a decentralized perpetual futures exchange, generated $800 million in revenue and $2.9 trillion in trading volume in 2025. The platform is extending its product suite to include tokenized equities, commodities and prediction markets. Grayscale views this diversification as positioning Hyperliquid to challenge established derivatives exchanges and become a major financial services player.

XRP news: Ripple-linked ETFs drew inflows last week as bitcoin, ether funds lost $2 billion

CoinDesk • Investment • Score: 5/10

U.S.-listed spot XRP exchange‑traded funds recorded a net inflow of $11.88 million on May 29, lifting weekly inflows to roughly $35 million and bringing total assets to about $1.12 billion. Over the same week, Bitcoin and Ether ETFs experienced net outflows of approximately $2 billion, indicating a pronounced reallocation away from the two largest crypto assets. The divergent flows suggest institutional capital is favoring Ripple‑linked products while reducing exposure to broader crypto market vehicles, a trend that could influence portfolio construction and risk management strategies.

Bitcoin’s biggest quantum risk may not be wallet keys. An early investor fears something bigger

CoinDesk • Security • Score: 5/10

Security experts say the most urgent quantum threat to Bitcoin is the encrypted authentication data exchanged between institutions rather than the private keys stored in wallets. Venture capitalist Andrew Gault warns that a “harvest now, decrypt later” approach is being modeled by firms such as Google and Citi to capture that data before quantum computers become capable of breaking it. Bitcoin networks and major custodians have not yet publicly committed to adopting post‑quantum cryptographic protections, leaving institutional investors exposed to potential future decryption attacks.

SEC sues Texas man over $12.3 million alleged crypto scheme built on fake AI trading bots

CoinDesk • Regulation • Score: 6/10

The U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission has sued Texas resident Nathan Fuller for allegedly raising $12.3 million from roughly 150 investors through a purported AI‑powered cryptocurrency trading bot. According to the complaint, Fuller diverted $6.2 million for personal use and used $5.5 million to make Ponzi‑style payouts to earlier investors. The filing says only about 3 percent of the collected funds were actually used to purchase cryptocurrency. The case highlights heightened regulatory focus on deceptive AI‑driven crypto schemes and the importance of thorough due‑diligence for institutional capital allocators.

US Seizes Nearly $1 Billion in Iranian Crypto Assets, Treasury Secretary Says

Cointelegraph • Regulation • Score: 6/10

The U.S. Treasury announced that it has seized nearly $1 billion in cryptocurrency assets linked to Iran. This amount is roughly double the value disclosed in the agency’s April enforcement action. The seizure is part of a broader sanctions strategy intended to cut off the Iranian regime’s illicit financing channels. It comes as reports indicate Iran is exploring Bitcoin‑based marine insurance to monetize activity in the Strait of Hormuz, highlighting the regime’s effort to leverage digital assets for revenue.
