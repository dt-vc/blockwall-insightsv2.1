Hackers target DeFi protocol Kelp DAO in massive $300m exploit

DL News • Security • Score: 9/10

Hackers drained approximately $293.7 million worth of rsETH from the Ethereum‑based liquid restaking protocol Kelp DAO. The breach was first identified by analyst ZachXBT and the stolen assets were subsequently transferred across Ethereum, Arbitrum and a Tornado Cash mixer. In response, Aave froze rsETH markets to contain further exposure. The exploit triggered a sharp decline in the price of rsETH and related tokens, highlighting systemic risk for institutional exposure to liquid restaking platforms.

Bitcoin and Ethereum slide after Iran says Strait of Hormuz is shut

DL News • Bitcoin • Score: 5/10

Bitcoin and Ethereum fell 2‑3% after reports that Iran had closed the Strait of Hormuz, reviving geopolitical risk concerns. In the same week, crypto exchange‑traded funds recorded strong institutional inflows, with Bitcoin‑focused funds drawing close to $1 billion. Ethereum‑focused funds attracted roughly $276 million of new capital. The price movement underscores the market’s sensitivity to developments in U.S., Iran cease‑fire negotiations, a factor institutional investors monitor closely.

X’s Cashtags Feature Drives $1B Trading Volume

Within the first two days of launch, the feature generated approximately $1 billion in trading volume. The rapid uptake signals strong demand from the platform’s user base for integrated financial services and may attract institutional interest in the ecosystem.

Coinbase's VC arm reveals the trends crypto investors are betting on in 2026

DL News • Investment • Score: 5/10

Coinbase Ventures principal Jonathan King identified four key investment themes for 2026: tokenisation, specialised exchanges, next‑gen DeFi and AI. He said the crypto market is moving into a “builders” phase even though Q1 venture funding fell 15% year‑over‑year to just under $5 billion. Despite the short‑term funding dip, King highlighted a long‑term growth outlook, pointing to a projected $20 trillion tokenisation market by 2030. Institutional investors may view these themes as the primary drivers of capital allocation as the sector seeks to scale infrastructure and new use cases.

Worldcoin Falls 13% as World Expands Iris-Scanning Tech

Cointelegraph • Infrastructure • Score: 5/10

Worldcoin’s native token WLD fell 13% to $0.28 as the project expanded its iris‑scanning identity verification technology to new partners including AWS, Shopify and Coinbase.
