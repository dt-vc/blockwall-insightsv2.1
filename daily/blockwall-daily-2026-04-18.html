Hackers target DeFi protocol Kelp DAO in massive $300m exploit

DL News • Security • Score: 9/10

Hackers drained approximately $293.7 million worth of rsETH from the Ethereum‑based liquid restaking protocol Kelp DAO. The breach was first identified by analyst ZachXBT. The stolen assets were subsequently transferred across Ethereum, Arbitrum and a Tornado Cash mixer. In response Aave froze rsETH markets and the incident triggered a sharp decline in the prices of associated tokens.

Bitcoin and Ethereum slide after Iran says Strait of Hormuz is shut

DL News • Bitcoin • Score: 5/10

Bitcoin and Ethereum fell 2‑3% after reports that Iran closed the Strait of Hormuz, reviving geopolitical risk. The decline came despite a week of strong inflows into crypto exchange‑traded funds. Bitcoin‑focused funds drew nearly $1 billion while Ethereum‑focused funds received about $276 million. Market participants remain attentive to cease‑fire negotiations between the United States and Iran because further developments could affect risk sentiment.

Coinbase's VC arm reveals the trends crypto investors are betting on in 2026

DL News • Investment • Score: 5/10

Coinbase Ventures principal Jonathan King identified four key investment themes for 2026: tokenisation, specialised exchanges, next‑gen DeFi and AI. He said the crypto market is moving into a “builders” phase even though Q1 funding fell 15% year‑over‑year to just under $5 billion. The outlook remains positive because of the long‑term growth potential, including a forecast that the tokenisation market could reach $20 trillion by 2030. Institutional investors may view these themes as strategic entry points as the sector seeks to scale beyond short‑term funding volatility.
