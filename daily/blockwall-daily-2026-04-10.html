Crypto startups kick off Q2 by raising $76m in the first week

DL News • Investment • Score: 7/10

Crypto startups raised a total of $76 million in the first week of Q2 2026, signaling a robust capital inflow at the start of the quarter despite ongoing geopolitical headwinds. The lead round was a $44 million Series A for Pharos, a high‑performance Layer‑1 blockchain platform, while AI‑focused Oh and payments‑oriented Kulipa secured the remaining funding. The fundraising activity highlights continued institutional appetite for infrastructure and application layers in the digital asset ecosystem. However, investors remain cautious, citing the US‑Iran conflict and broader macro‑economic uncertainty as factors that could temper future deployment of capital.

Scott Bessent is heading the Trump administration’s effort to move the Clarity Act through Congress. Morgan Stanley’s Bitcoin ETF on track to pull in $7bn in year one, says Ric Edelman

Polymarket says it’s committed to US national security after another lawmaker slams betting platform

DL News • Regulation • Score: 6/10

U.S. Senator Richard Blumenthal sent a letter to the CEO of Polymarket alleging that the prediction‑market platform enables insider trading and allows wagers on events tied to national security. Polymarket responded that it has adopted new insider‑trading rules and reaffirmed its commitment to market integrity and U.S. national security. The exchange’s statements come as regulators maintain heightened scrutiny of the rapidly expanding crypto‑betting sector, which is of interest to institutional investors monitoring compliance and systemic risk.

The negative market reaction underscores the importance of robust risk management for institutional investors in digital‑asset exposures.

‘Cautious optimism’: Bitcoin, Ethereum shoot up following CPI release. How long will it last?

Bitcoin and Ethereum posted notable gains after U.S. consumer price index data indicated that core inflation increased by only 0.2% in March. The modest inflation reading has sparked cautious optimism among market participants that the Federal Reserve may consider a rate cut in the near term. The rally was further supported by a de‑escalation in tensions between the United States and Iran and by market speculation of a short squeeze, factors that institutional investors monitor for risk‑adjusted exposure decisions.

Trump-backed WLFI token drops 12% to record lows after team defends multi-million lending position

CoinDesk • DeFi • Score: 5/10

The WLFI token issued by World Liberty Financial fell 12 percent to a record low after the company defended a multi‑million dollar lending position on the Dolomite DeFi platform. The firm disclosed that it had pledged its own governance token as collateral to borrow stablecoins, a practice that critics argue creates a circular risk loop. WLFI said it would increase token collateral to prevent liquidation of the loan. The episode has heightened concerns among investors about the protocol’s stability and the exposure of token holders to compounded risk.

Ethereum is abandoning layer 2s. Arbitrum’s creator still sees a bright future ahead

DL News • Layer2 • Score: 5/10

Ethereum leadership is shifting focus to scaling the mainnet, which has prompted speculation about the future relevance of layer‑2 solutions such as Arbitrum. Offchain Labs co‑founder Ed Felten maintains that layer‑2 platforms continue to provide faster transaction processing and lower fees, keeping them pertinent to the network’s growth. The contrasting views illustrate a broader strategic debate within the Ethereum ecosystem that could influence investment decisions.

Ripple's XRP may be less exposed to quantum computer threats than bitcoin

CoinDesk • Security • Score: 5/10

Ripple’s XRP is considered potentially less exposed to quantum computer threats than Bitcoin. Experts point to XRP’s built‑in key‑rotation and escrow time‑lock mechanisms that reduce vulnerability compared with Bitcoin’s practice of exposing public keys when transactions are broadcast. An audit of the XRP Ledger identified 300,000 accounts holding 2.4 billion XRP that have never sent funds, meaning their public keys remain hidden and quantum‑safe. This structural resilience may be relevant for institutional investors evaluating long‑term security of crypto holdings against emerging quantum‑computing risks.

Pyth Network Launches Data Marketplace For Price Feeds Across Asset Classes

Cointelegraph • Infrastructure • Score: 5/10

Pyth Network has introduced a data marketplace that aggregates price feeds covering a broad spectrum of asset classes. The marketplace enables data providers to list their feeds and earn revenue from them. Developers can access the aggregated price data directly on-chain, which is intended to improve the quality and reliability of price information used in decentralized applications. By offering a decentralized source of price data, the platform seeks to reduce the reliance on centralized data providers within the crypto ecosystem.
