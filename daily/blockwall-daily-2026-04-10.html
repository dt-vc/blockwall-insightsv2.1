Trump-backed WLFI token drops 12% to record lows after team defends multi-million lending position

CoinDesk • DeFi • Score: 5/10

The WLFI token, issued by World Liberty Financial and linked to former President Trump, fell 12 percent to a record low after the company defended a multi‑million dollar lending position on the Dolomite DeFi platform. The firm revealed that it had pledged its own governance token as collateral to borrow stablecoins. Critics argue that this creates a circular risk loop that could amplify losses if the token price declines further. WLFI said it will add additional token collateral to prevent liquidation, but the episode has heightened concerns about the protocol’s stability and the exposure of its users.

Ethereum is abandoning layer 2s. Arbitrum’s creator still sees a bright future ahead

DL News • Layer2 • Score: 5/10

Ethereum’s leadership is shifting focus to scaling the mainnet, which has prompted speculation that the network may be moving away from reliance on layer‑2 solutions. Offchain Labs co‑founder Ed Felten counters that layer‑2 platforms such as Arbitrum continue to deliver faster transaction throughput and lower fees, keeping them relevant for users and developers. The contrasting views illustrate a strategic debate within the Ethereum ecosystem about the optimal path for long‑term scalability and investment risk.

Ripple's XRP may be less exposed to quantum computer threats than bitcoin

CoinDesk • Security • Score: 5/10

Ripple's XRP is considered potentially less vulnerable to future quantum computer attacks than Bitcoin. The reduced exposure stems from XRP’s built‑in key‑rotation and escrow time‑lock mechanisms that limit the exposure of public keys. An audit of the XRP Ledger identified 300,000 accounts that collectively hold 2.4 billion XRP and have never sent funds, meaning their public keys remain undisclosed. For institutional investors, this architecture may lower the quantum‑risk profile of XRP holdings relative to Bitcoin assets.
