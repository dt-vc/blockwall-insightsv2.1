US has seized nearly $1 billion in crypto from Iran, Bessent says

The Block • Regulation • Score: 8/10

U.S. authorities have seized almost $1 billion worth of cryptocurrency that was linked to Iran. The seizure was announced by Bessent, who confirmed the assets were part of Iran’s illicit crypto operations. This action is part of the United States’ broader enforcement of sanctions aimed at curbing Iran’s ability to finance prohibited activities through digital assets. Institutional investors should note the heightened regulatory scrutiny of crypto transactions involving sanctioned jurisdictions and the potential impact on compliance and risk‑management frameworks.

OKX Ventures buys $53 million stake in Korea's Coinone exchange

CoinDesk • Investment • Score: 9/10

OKX Ventures and Korea Investment & Securities each invested 80 billion won, roughly $53 million, for a combined 19.6 percent stake in the South Korean cryptocurrency exchange Coinone. The $106 million transaction is subject to regulatory approval. The investment makes OKX Ventures and Korea Investment & Securities joint third‑largest shareholders of Coinone. CEO Cha Myunghun remains the largest shareholder and retains management control, underscoring the strategic partnership’s impact on the exchange’s governance and growth prospects.

The CFTC Greenlights Crypto Perps in America on Bankless

Bankless • Regulation • Score: 9/10

The Commodity Futures Trading Commission has approved KalshiEX’s BTCPERP, establishing the United States’ first regulated Bitcoin perpetual futures contract. In a separate action the CFTC issued a no‑action letter that permits Coinbase to direct U.S. customers to Deribit for access to global crypto derivatives markets. The approvals allow Bitcoin, Ether and stablecoins to be used as margin collateral and are viewed as a historic step toward broader institutional adoption of crypto derivatives.

Bitcoin ETFs suffer record 9-day outflow streak as $2.8 billion exits funds

CoinDesk • Bitcoin • Score: 6/10

U.S. spot Bitcoin exchange‑traded funds have recorded a nine‑day streak of net outflows, with about $2.8 billion leaving the products. The withdrawals occurred as Bitcoin’s price fell, prompting institutional investors to sell. Among the redemptions, BlackRock’s iShares Bitcoin Trust posted its largest single‑day redemption on record. The sustained outflow pressure raises concerns for fund liquidity and may signal a broader shift in institutional appetite for crypto exposure.

ICE CEO calls Hyperliquid bigger than NASDAQ, says he's met its founders

CoinDesk • DeFi • Score: 6/10

ICE CEO Jeffrey Sprecher said Hyperliquid’s decentralized perpetual futures platform now trades more volume than the Nasdaq exchange. He noted that the platform commands over 70 percent of the perpetual derivatives market and offers continuous oil futures trading. Sprecher warned that Hyperliquid’s offshore, unregulated status creates a regulatory gap that will require attention from policymakers.

What American crypto asset perpetuals mean for the future of crypto

CoinDesk • Regulation • Score: 8/10

The CFTC has granted historic approval for a CFTC‑registered exchange to list a true Bitcoin perpetual contract, placing the high‑liquidity perpetual market under U.S. regulatory oversight. This regulatory step provides clearer rules for market participants and creates domestic access for institutional investors who previously relied on offshore venues. By bringing American crypto firms onto a level playing field with global competitors, the approval is expected to shape the future of crypto by encouraging broader adoption and integration into traditional finance.
