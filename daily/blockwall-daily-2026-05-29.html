US has seized nearly $1 billion in crypto from Iran, Bessent says

The Block • Regulation • Score: 8/10

U.S. authorities have seized almost $1 billion worth of cryptocurrency that was linked to Iran, according to a statement from Bessent. The assets were taken as part of the United States’ ongoing enforcement of sanctions targeting Iran’s illicit crypto operations. The action underscores the heightened regulatory scrutiny of cross‑border digital‑asset transactions and signals that institutional investors must maintain robust compliance frameworks when dealing with counterparties in sanctioned jurisdictions.

OKX Ventures buys $53 million stake in Korea's Coinone exchange

CoinDesk • Investment • Score: 9/10

OKX Ventures and Korea Investment & Securities each invested 80 billion won, roughly $53 million, for a combined 19.6 % stake in the South Korean cryptocurrency exchange Coinone. The $106 million transaction is pending regulatory approval. With the investment, OKX Ventures and Korea Investment & Securities become joint third‑largest shareholders of Coinone. CEO Cha Myunghun remains the largest shareholder and retains management control, which may influence the exchange’s strategic direction and governance.

The CFTC Greenlights Crypto Perps in America on Bankless

Bankless • Regulation • Score: 9/10

The Commodity Futures Trading Commission has approved KalshiEX’s BTCPERP, establishing the first regulated Bitcoin perpetual futures contract in the United States. In a complementary action the CFTC issued a no‑action letter that permits Coinbase to direct U.S. customers to Deribit for access to global crypto derivatives markets. The approvals allow Bitcoin, Ethereum and stablecoins to be used as margin collateral on regulated platforms. Institutional investors view the development as a historic step toward broader adoption of crypto derivatives within the mainstream financial system.

Bitcoin ETFs suffer record 9-day outflow streak as $2.8 billion exits funds

CoinDesk • Bitcoin • Score: 6/10

U.S. spot Bitcoin ETFs have recorded a nine‑day streak of net outflows, with about $2.8 billion withdrawn. The withdrawals align with a recent decline in Bitcoin’s price and heightened institutional selling pressure. The trend was highlighted by BlackRock’s iShares Bitcoin Trust registering its largest single‑day redemption, underscoring the sensitivity of ETF inflows to market sentiment.

ICE CEO calls Hyperliquid bigger than NASDAQ, says he's met its founders

CoinDesk • DeFi • Score: 6/10

ICE chief executive Jeffrey Sprecher said Hyperliquid’s decentralized perpetual futures platform now trades more volume than the Nasdaq exchange. He noted that the platform commands over 70 percent of the perpetual‑DEX market. Sprecher also pointed to Hyperliquid’s 24/7 oil derivatives offering as a differentiator. He warned that the platform’s offshore, unregulated status creates a regulatory gap that policymakers will need to address.

Paxos wins SEC approval to clear U.S. stocks on blockchain

CoinDesk • Regulation • Score: 8/10

Paxos Securities Settlement Company has received full registration from the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission, making it the first blockchain‑based central securities depository authorized to clear U.S. equities. The approval places Paxos on equal regulatory footing with established post‑trade infrastructure providers such as the Depository Trust & Clearing Corporation. Institutional investors can now access near‑instant settlement of stock trades on a blockchain platform, potentially reducing settlement risk and operational costs.
