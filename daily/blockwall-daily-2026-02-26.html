Aave Notches $1T in Lending Volume, an Industry First

News • DeFi • Score: 7/10

Aave has achieved a DeFi industry first by surpassing $1 trillion in cumulative on-chain lending volume, with $27.2 billion in total value locked and $83.3 million in fee revenue over the past 30 days. This milestone underscores Aave's dominance as the largest decentralized lending protocol, holding a 60% market share and over $52 billion in net deposits. For institutional investors, Aave Horizon, an Ethereum-based lending market launched in August, enables borrowing stablecoins against tokenized real-world assets like treasury bonds, offering real-time pledging, 70-75% collateralization rates, and compliance safeguards that boost capital efficiency over traditional finance. Early participants including VanEck, WisdomTree, and Securitize highlight its appeal in bridging TradFi and DeFi liquidity.
