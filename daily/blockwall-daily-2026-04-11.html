🔥 Key Developments (8)

IMF warns stablecoins are only as stable as their reserves

DL News • Stablecoins • Score: 6/10

The International Monetary Fund has issued a new report warning that the stability of stablecoins depends on the transparency and regulation of the assets backing them. The report points to the Bitcoin‑backed reserves of Tether as a specific vulnerability that could undermine confidence in the broader market. It also references the collapse of the Terra‑UST algorithmic stablecoin as a precedent for a rapid loss of value that resembles a bank run. The IMF urges regulators to impose stricter oversight of reserve holdings to protect investors and maintain financial stability.

The crypto honeymoon is over for now as analysts warn of a major first-quarter profit squeeze

CoinDesk • Enterprise • Score: 6/10

Wall Street analysts have downgraded Coinbase and cut its revenue outlook after crypto trading volumes fell roughly 30% quarter‑over‑quarter. The decline represents the lowest trading activity since late 2023. The reduced volume puts first‑quarter profitability at risk for Coinbase and other digital‑asset exchanges. Analysts caution that the earnings season could reveal a significant profit squeeze for the sector.

Crypto Clarity bill has 30% chance of passing this year, Wintermute’s Hammond says

CoinDesk • Regulation • Score: 6/10

Ron Hammond, head of policy at Wintermute, estimates that the U.S. Crypto Clarity Act has roughly a 30 percent chance of being enacted this year. He cites political friction and bank opposition, especially over stablecoin yields, as key factors limiting its prospects. The bill would codify SEC and CFTC authority over digital assets and is viewed as a potential catalyst for broader institutional adoption.

Arizona Judge Blocks Gambling Enforcement Against Kalshi Contracts

Cointelegraph • Regulation • Score: 6/10

An Arizona judge ruled that state gambling statutes do not cover the contracts offered by prediction‑market platform Kalshi. The ruling blocks any enforcement action against Kalshi under Arizona gambling law. The decision is viewed as a legal win for the company and could influence how U.S. regulators approach similar prediction‑market and crypto‑based platforms.

DL News • Bitcoin • Score: 5/10

Bitcoin and Ethereum recorded double‑digit weekly gains, confirming continued strength in the leading cryptocurrencies. Privacy‑focused assets Zcash and Dash outperformed, each climbing about 50% over the same period. The surge is attributed to recent geopolitical developments and has been amplified by crypto influencers and venture capital support, highlighting heightened investor interest in privacy solutions.

Iran war oil shock revives inflation trade and a new stablecoin play

CoinDesk • Stablecoins • Score: 5/10

The recent oil price shock linked to the Iran war has reignited market focus on inflation‑linked strategies. In response, USDi, an inflation‑linked stablecoin co‑founded by Michael Ashton and Andrew Fately, is designed to preserve purchasing power by tracking the U.S. consumer price index instead of a fixed dollar peg. The token is marketed as a bridge for the “store‑of‑value” gap in the crypto monetary system. It specifically targets institutional participants such as insurers and corporate treasurers who are seeking protection against rising inflation.

GWEI - ETHGas - AMBCrypto

AMBCrypto • Infrastructure • Score: 5/10

ETHGas’s GWEI token is currently priced near $0.10 and carries a market capitalization of about $210 million. Over the past 24 hours the token posted modest gains while its price has fallen over the last seven days. The underlying protocol tokenizes Ethereum blockspace into tradable units, aiming to deliver subsecond block confirmations and to enable a futures market for gas. Trading activity is spread across several centralized exchanges with LBank leading.

WLFI Drops to Record Low After Token-Backed Borrowing Raises Risk Concerns

Cointelegraph • DeFi • Score: 5/10

The WLFI token, which underpins a decentralized finance platform, fell to its lowest price ever after risk concerns about its token‑backed borrowing model emerged. Investors and traders highlighted the model’s reliance on the token as collateral as a source of heightened volatility. The perception of elevated risk triggered a rapid sell‑off that pushed the token to a record low. This episode underscores how borrowing structures can affect crypto asset stability and may shape institutional risk assessments.
