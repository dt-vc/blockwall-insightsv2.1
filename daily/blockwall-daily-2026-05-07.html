KelpDAO/LayerZero Exploit Drains $290m, Freezes DeFi Markets

Galaxy • DeFi • Score: 9/10

KelpDAO’s liquid restaking token rsETH was compromised in a hack that extracted $290 million by exploiting a misconfiguration in the LayerZero OFT bridge. The attacker then posted the stolen rsETH as collateral on major lending platforms Aave, Compound and Euler to borrow $236 million. These leveraged positions triggered rapid liquidations that froze trading activity across affected DeFi markets. The incident also contributed to a $15 billion reduction in total value locked across the DeFi ecosystem, highlighting systemic risk from bridge vulnerabilities.

Aave to Tighten Collateral Standards Following Near-Disaster KelpDAO Crisis on Bankless

Bankless • DeFi • Score: 6/10

The changes introduce mandatory reviews of cybersecurity, interoperability and technical architecture for all assets accepted as collateral. The move follows the KelpDAO exploit that caused a $200M borrowing loss and exposed weaknesses in the protocol’s risk controls. By raising minimum collateral standards, Aave aims to lower future risk exposure for its lending markets and protect institutional participants.

CLARITY Act 2026: Senate Banking Markup & Stablecoin Yield

Galaxy • Regulation • Score: 6/10

The CLARITY Act, which cleared the House in July 2025, has moved to the Senate Banking Committee for markup. Lawmakers are still debating how the bill will address yield generation on stablecoins and the integration of decentralized finance provisions. The outcome of the markup will determine whether the legislation can secure the bipartisan support needed for timely passage. If the committee delays further, enactment could slip past 2026, limiting the act’s immediate influence on the crypto market and affecting institutional exposure to regulated digital assets.
