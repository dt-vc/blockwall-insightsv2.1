KelpDAO/LayerZero Exploit Drains $290m, Freezes DeFi Markets

Galaxy • DeFi • Score: 9/10

KelpDAO’s liquid restaking token rsETH was compromised in a hack that drained approximately $290 million by exploiting a misconfiguration in the LayerZero OFT bridge. The attacker leveraged the stolen rsETH as collateral on major lending platforms Aave, Compound and Euler to borrow about $236 million. This activity triggered freezes in several DeFi markets and contributed to a roughly $15 billion decline in total value locked across the sector. The incident highlights systemic risk from cross‑chain bridge vulnerabilities and the importance of robust collateral management for institutional participants.

Core Scientific sold $208 million of bitcoin in Q1 as AI pivot continues

CoinDesk • Infrastructure • Score: 7/10
