Nomura survey shows rising institutional crypto adoption driven by regulation and diversification

Illegal Russian crypto exchange operators to face 7 years of hard labour under new laws

DL News • Regulation • Score: 5/10

Russia is drafting legislation that would criminalise unlicensed crypto exchange activity, imposing fines and forced‑labour sentences of up to seven years for operators and up to four years for individual traders. If the bill is enacted by July 2027, crypto trading would be required to occur only on platforms approved by banks. The legislation also extends penalties to cryptocurrency miners who fail to declare their operations. Institutional investors should note that the new regime could restrict access to Russian crypto markets and increase compliance risk for any exposure to unlicensed exchanges or mining activities.
