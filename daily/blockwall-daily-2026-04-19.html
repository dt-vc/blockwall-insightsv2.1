'DeFi is dead': Here is how crypto community is reacting after massive $292 million hack

CoinDesk • DeFi • Score: 9/10

A cross‑chain exploit of Kelp DAO’s rsETH token caused a $292 million loss, prompting the crypto community to label DeFi as “dead.” The breach created a liquidity crunch that forced major lending platforms such as Aave to experience massive outflows. Total value locked across DeFi fell from $26.4 billion to $20 billion. The AAVE token dropped more than 18%, underscoring systemic security concerns for institutional investors.

Nomura survey shows rising institutional crypto adoption driven by regulation and diversification

CoinDesk • Investment • Score: 6/10

A recent survey by Nomura and Laser Digital of more than 500 investment professionals indicates that institutional interest in crypto is on the rise. Respondents cite clearer regulatory frameworks and the desire to diversify portfolios as the primary drivers of this shift. The majority of institutions expect to allocate between two and five percent of assets to crypto over the next three years. They are also exploring exposure beyond spot markets, including staking, lending, derivatives and tokenized assets.

Illegal Russian crypto exchange operators to face 7 years of hard labour under new laws

DL News • Regulation • Score: 5/10

Russia is drafting legislation that would criminalise unlicensed crypto exchange activity and impose fines and forced‑labour sentences of up to seven years for operators. Individual traders could face forced‑labour terms of up to four years under the same bill. If enacted by July 2027, the law would require crypto trading to occur on bank‑approved platforms and would extend penalties to miners who fail to declare their operations.
