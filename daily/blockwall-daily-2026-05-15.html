🔥 Key Developments (11)

The man who built Saudi Arabia’s digital payment rails has a bold 2030 plan to bring trillions onchain

CoinDesk • Tokenization • Score: 9/10

Faisal Monai, who designed Saudi Arabia’s digital payments infrastructure, is leading a $12.5 billion project under the droppRWA brand to tokenize real‑world assets, starting with real estate. The plan calls for a sovereign‑grade, stablecoin‑based tokenized financial system to be operational across the kingdom by 2030. Real‑estate settlement on the blockchain is slated to go live by late 2026, a move that could position Saudi Arabia as a benchmark for other G20 economies seeking similar on‑chain financial modernization.

SpaceX targets June 11 IPO pricing, picks Nasdaq for historic market debut

CoinDesk • Investment • Score: 9/10

SpaceX has announced that it will price its initial public offering on June 11 and expects shares to begin trading on Nasdaq around June 12. The company is targeting a raise of roughly $75 billion, which would represent the largest public offering in history. At a proposed valuation of $1.75 trillion, the IPO would position SpaceX among the most valuable publicly listed firms. The listing will also introduce the firm’s substantial Bitcoin holdings to the public market, providing investors exposure to its crypto treasury.

Hana Bank agrees to buy $670 million stake in Upbit operator Dunamu

CoinDesk • Investment • Score: 9/10

Hana Bank will purchase a 6.55% stake in Dunamu, the operator of South Korea’s Upbit exchange, for roughly 1 trillion won ($670 million). The investment makes Hana the fourth‑largest shareholder in Dunamu and includes collaboration on a won‑pegged stablecoin, blockchain‑based remittances and tokenized securities. The deal aligns with South Korea’s new digital‑asset regulations and signals increased institutional participation in the country’s cryptocurrency market.

AI miner IREN raises $3 billion to accelerate AI cloud and data center buildout

CoinDesk • Infrastructure • Score: 9/10

Bitcoin miner IREN announced it raised approximately $3 billion through a convertible senior notes offering. The proceeds are earmarked for the company’s transition into AI cloud and hyperscale data‑center infrastructure, including GPU purchases from Nvidia and Dell and other AI‑related capital expenditures. The financing round expanded beyond an initial $2 billion target, reflecting strong investor demand. IREN’s shares slipped about 3% in pre‑market trading, underscoring market sensitivity to the shift toward AI assets.

Winklevoss’ Gemini (GEMI) jumps 25% on $100 million bitcoin infusion despite deepening losses

CoinDesk • Enterprise • Score: 8/10

Gemini (ticker GEMI) posted a first‑quarter net loss of $109 million but its shares surged more than 25 percent after Winklevoss Capital injected $100 million in bitcoin. The company’s revenue grew 42 percent year‑over‑year to $50.3 million while operating expenses jumped 73 percent, widening the loss. Management is responding by reducing headcount and withdrawing from multiple international markets, a move that investors will watch as the firm seeks profitability.

Strategy (MSTR) buying back convertible debt at a discount

CoinDesk • Enterprise • Score: 7/10

MicroStrategy announced a plan to repurchase roughly $1.5 billion of its 0% convertible senior notes due 2029. The company expects to spend about $1.38 billion on the buyback, financing the transaction with cash on hand, proceeds from a recent equity offering and, if needed, sales of its Bitcoin holdings. By purchasing the notes at a discount to par, MicroStrategy will lower its outstanding debt while signaling potential liquidity pressure on its Bitcoin treasury. The news prompted a roughly 2% decline in MicroStrategy’s shares in pre‑market trading and contributed to a modest pull‑back in the price of Bitcoin.

CME, ICE push U.S. regulators to scrutinize Hyperliquid over manipulation risks

CoinDesk • Regulation • Score: 7/10

CME Group and Intercontinental Exchange have formally asked the Commodity Futures Trading Commission and U.S. lawmakers to examine Hyperliquid’s decentralized perpetual futures platform. They argue that the platform’s anonymous, 24‑hour trading environment creates heightened risks of market manipulation and could be used to evade sanctions. This request reflects a broader regulatory focus on crypto derivatives that operate outside traditional exchange oversight. Institutional investors should monitor the outcome because any regulatory action could affect the pricing and reliability of commodity benchmarks linked to these markets.

Crypto market structure bill clears key hurdle as ethics debate looms over floor vote

CoinDesk • Regulation • Score: 7/10

The Senate Banking Committee voted to approve the crypto market structure bill, moving it closer to a full Senate floor vote. The bill, which has bipartisan backing, contains amendments that address staking rewards, tokenization and developer protections. However, the committee did not resolve language related to ethics, leaving that issue for further debate. Institutional investors are watching the legislation because its final shape could affect how banks and other financial firms interact with digital assets.

Crypto firms move $4 billion in assets to Chainlink as bridge security comes under scrutiny

CoinDesk • Infrastructure • Score: 7/10

Around $4 billion in assets are being reallocated from LayerZero‑based bridges to Chainlink’s Cross‑Chain Interoperability Protocol (CCIP) after a $292 million exploit on the Kelp DAO bridge raised security concerns. The shift includes more than $1 billion of bitcoin‑backed tokens moved by the DeFi protocol Lombard, indicating a broader flight toward what is perceived as a safer cross‑chain infrastructure. Institutional investors are watching the migration because secure interoperability is critical for protecting large, multi‑chain positions and for maintaining confidence in decentralized finance ecosystems.

Thorchain halts trading after $10 million cross-chain exploit, RUNE token drops 12%

CoinDesk • Security • Score: 6/10

Thorchain’s cross‑chain liquidity network was hit by a $10.8 million exploit that spanned Bitcoin, Ethereum, BSC and Base. The protocol responded by pausing all trading and signing activities. The incident pushed the native RUNE token down about 12%, underscoring security risks for cross‑chain bridges and liquidity platforms.

Hyperliquid (HYPE) leads 24-hour gains as altcoins pace bitcoin (BTC)

CoinDesk • Tokenization • Score: 6/10

Hyperliquid’s native token HYPE posted a 16% increase, leading the 24‑hour gainers while altcoins moved in line with Bitcoin. The price surge followed Bitwise’s launch of a spot Hyperliquid exchange‑traded fund and Coinbase’s appointment as the token’s official USDC treasury manager. The broader rally lifted multiple altcoins and raised crypto futures volume, signaling a bullish yet orderly market environment that may be of interest to institutional investors.
