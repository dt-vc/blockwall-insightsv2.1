The man who built Saudi Arabia’s digital payment rails has a bold 2030 plan to bring trillions onchain

CoinDesk • Tokenization • Score: 9/10

Faisal Monai, who designed Saudi Arabia’s digital payments system, is leading a $12.5 billion effort through droppRWA to tokenize real‑world assets, starting with real estate. The project aims to create a sovereign‑grade stablecoin‑based tokenized financial infrastructure that operates across the kingdom. Real‑estate settlement on the blockchain is slated to be live by late 2026. Monai’s roadmap positions Saudi Arabia as a reference model for other G20 economies by 2030.

SpaceX targets June 11 IPO pricing, picks Nasdaq for historic market debut

CoinDesk • Investment • Score: 9/10

SpaceX has announced that it will price its initial public offering on June 11. Trading of the shares is expected to begin on Nasdaq around June 12, marking a historic market debut for the aerospace firm. The offering aims to raise roughly $75 billion and values the company at about $1.75 trillion, which would make it the largest public offering to date. The listing will also introduce SpaceX’s sizable Bitcoin treasury to public investors, adding a crypto exposure component to the deal.

Hana Bank agrees to buy $670 million stake in Upbit operator Dunamu

CoinDesk • Investment • Score: 9/10

Hana Bank is acquiring a 6.55% stake in Dunamu, the operator of South Korea’s Upbit exchange, for approximately 1 trillion won ($670 million). The purchase makes Hana the fourth‑largest shareholder in Dunamu and aligns the bank with the rapidly growing Korean digital‑asset market. The partnership will focus on developing a won‑pegged stablecoin, blockchain‑based remittance services and tokenized securities, leveraging the recent regulatory framework for digital assets in South Korea.

AI miner IREN raises $3 billion to accelerate AI cloud and data center buildout

CoinDesk • Infrastructure • Score: 9/10

Bitcoin miner IREN announced a convertible senior notes offering that raised approximately $3 billion. The proceeds are earmarked for a transition into AI cloud services and hyperscale data‑center infrastructure. The financing, which expanded from an original $2 billion target, will fund GPU purchases from Nvidia and Dell as well as other AI‑related capital expenditures. IREN’s shares fell about 3 percent in pre‑market trading following the announcement.

Winklevoss’ Gemini (GEMI) jumps 25% on $100 million bitcoin infusion despite deepening losses

CoinDesk • Enterprise • Score: 8/10

Gemini (ticker GEMI) saw its shares rise over 25% after receiving a $100 million bitcoin‑funded investment from Winklevoss Capital. The company posted a Q1 net loss of $109 million despite revenue increasing 42% year‑over‑year to $50.3 million. Operating expenses grew 73% in the quarter, prompting Gemini to reduce headcount and withdraw from several international markets. The infusion and share rally highlight investor confidence in the firm’s long‑term crypto infrastructure despite short‑term profitability challenges.

Strategy (MSTR) buying back convertible debt at a discount

CoinDesk • Enterprise • Score: 7/10

MicroStrategy said it will repurchase roughly $1.5 billion of its 0% convertible senior notes due 2029. The company expects to pay about $1.38 billion for the notes, drawing on cash, proceeds from a recent equity offering and, if needed, sales of its Bitcoin holdings. Buying the notes at a discount reduces the firm’s outstanding debt but also signals potential pressure on its Bitcoin treasury. The announcement coincided with a 2% pre‑market decline in MicroStrategy shares and a modest pull‑back in Bitcoin price, underscoring the market’s sensitivity to the company’s financing moves.

CME, ICE push U.S. regulators to scrutinize Hyperliquid over manipulation risks

CoinDesk • Regulation • Score: 7/10

CME Group and Intercontinental Exchange have formally asked the Commodity Futures Trading Commission and members of Congress to examine Hyperliquid’s decentralized perpetual futures platform. They argue that the platform’s anonymous, 24‑hour trading model creates opportunities for market manipulation and could be used to evade sanctions. The request reflects a broader regulatory focus on crypto‑derived derivatives that operate outside traditional exchange oversight. Institutional investors should monitor potential regulatory actions because any restrictions could affect the pricing and liquidity of crypto contracts that intersect with established commodity benchmarks.

Crypto market structure bill clears key hurdle as ethics debate looms over floor vote

CoinDesk • Regulation • Score: 7/10

The Senate Banking Committee has approved a crypto market structure bill, moving it closer to a full Senate floor vote. The legislation, which received bipartisan support, contains amendments addressing staking rewards, tokenization and developer protections. However, the bill still lacks finalized language on ethics and additional lobbying by banks could influence its ultimate shape. Institutional investors are watching the bill because its outcome could affect regulatory clarity and operational frameworks for digital asset services.

Crypto firms move $4 billion in assets to Chainlink as bridge security comes under scrutiny

CoinDesk • Infrastructure • Score: 7/10

Around $4 billion in assets are being shifted from LayerZero‑based bridges to Chainlink’s CCIP after a $292 million exploit on the Kelp DAO bridge raised security concerns. The move includes more than $1 billion of bitcoin‑backed tokens being transferred by DeFi protocol Lombard, indicating a broader flight to what is perceived as a safer cross‑chain infrastructure. Institutional investors are watching the migration as it could reshape liquidity distribution across interoperable blockchain networks.

Thorchain halts trading after $10 million cross-chain exploit, RUNE token drops 12%

CoinDesk • Security • Score: 6/10

Thorchain’s cross‑chain liquidity network suffered a multi‑chain exploit that drained roughly $10 million, affecting assets on Bitcoin, Ethereum, BSC and Base. In response, the protocol halted all trading and signing operations to contain the breach. The native RUNE token fell about 12% following the incident. The event underscores the persistent security challenges facing cross‑chain bridges and liquidity platforms.

Hyperliquid (HYPE) leads 24-hour gains as altcoins pace bitcoin (BTC)

CoinDesk • Tokenization • Score: 6/10

Hyperliquid’s native token HYPE surged 16% in a 24‑hour period, leading gains across the cryptocurrency market. The price jump followed Bitwise’s launch of a spot Hyperliquid exchange‑traded fund, providing institutional exposure to the token. Coinbase was simultaneously named the official USDC treasury deployer for Hyperliquid, adding a reputable custodial partner. The combined catalyst lifted several altcoins, increased crypto futures volume and signaled a bullish yet orderly market environment that may be of interest to institutional investors.
