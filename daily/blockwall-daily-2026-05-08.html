Weekly Top Stories: DTCC and Bullish are bullish on tokenized securities

Galaxy • Tokenization • Score: 8/10

DTCC, the leading post‑trade market infrastructure provider, said it will roll out a tokenization service for assets held in its DTC custodial system later this year. The service is intended to create digital representations of traditional securities that can be moved and settled on a blockchain while retaining DTC’s custody and settlement safeguards. At the same time, crypto exchange Bullish announced a $4.2 billion purchase of transfer‑agent Equiniti, a move that brings a major traditional back‑office function onto its platform. Together, these initiatives signal a coordinated push by established market participants to bring on‑chain securities infrastructure to institutional investors.

Kraken parent goes for the OCC charter in bid to become a federal crypto bank

CoinDesk • Regulation • Score: 7/10

Payward, the parent company of Kraken, has submitted an application to the U.S. Office of the Comptroller of the Currency for a federal trust charter. If granted, the charter would make Payward the first federally chartered crypto bank. The new charter would operate alongside Kraken’s existing Wyoming bank charter and its Federal Reserve master account, broadening its regulatory presence in the United States. Institutional investors see the development as a step toward a more regulated environment for crypto custody and financing services.

LayerZero Discourse Erupts in

Bankless • Security • Score: 7/10

Security researchers have warned that more than $3 billion of LayerZero OFTs are vulnerable because a default library contract can be upgraded instantly without a timelock. This design flaw could allow forged cross‑chain messages that compromise the integrity of token transfers. While many projects have already migrated away, roughly $178.5 million of assets remain exposed. The issue also raises concerns about the potential misuse of LayerZero multisig signing keys, a risk that institutional investors should monitor closely.
