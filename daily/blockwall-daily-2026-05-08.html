Kraken parent goes for the OCC charter in bid to become a federal crypto bank

CoinDesk • Regulation • Score: 7/10

Payward, the parent of Kraken, has submitted an application to the U.S. Office of the Comptroller of the Currency for a federal trust charter. If granted, the charter would make Kraken the first federally chartered crypto bank and would sit alongside its existing Wyoming bank charter and Federal Reserve master account. The additional charter would broaden Kraken’s regulatory footprint in the United States and could provide institutional investors with greater confidence in the firm’s compliance and access to traditional banking infrastructure.

LayerZero Discourse Erupts in

Bankless • Security • Score: 7/10

Security researchers have flagged a critical vulnerability in LayerZero’s default library contract that can be upgraded instantly without a timelock. The flaw puts more than $3 billion worth of LayerZero OFTs at risk of forged cross‑chain messages. While a majority of projects have already migrated away, roughly $178.5 million of assets remain exposed under the current contract. The issue also raises concerns about the potential misuse of LayerZero’s multisig signing keys, a factor institutional investors should monitor given the protocol’s role in cross‑chain DeFi operations.

Aptos Ecosystem Commits $50 Million to AI Agent Adoption

Cointelegraph • Payments • Score: 7/10

The Aptos Foundation has earmarked $50 million to accelerate the adoption of AI agents within its blockchain ecosystem. The funding will support integrations such as Amazon Web Services Bedrock AgentCore combined with Coinbase’s x402 payments protocol to enable USDC transactions on the network. The initiative also includes the launch of a privacy‑focused Confidential APT token and the development of encrypted mempools. These efforts aim to deliver confidential perpetual trading capabilities on the Aptos stack, enhancing security and functionality for institutional participants.

Arbitrum DAO Approves $70M ETH Release Despite Court-Imposed Restraining Order on Bankless

Bankless • Layer2 • Score: 6/10

The Arbitrum DAO, which governs the Arbitrum layer‑2 scaling platform, voted to approve the release of roughly $70 million in ETH that was frozen after the rsETH exploit.
