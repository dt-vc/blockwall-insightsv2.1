Bitcoin News: A massive $1.26 billion sale of BlackRock’s IBIT was likely a rapid exit by a large investor, NYDIG says

CoinDesk • Bitcoin • Score: 9/10

A $1.26 billion block sale of BlackRock’s iShares Bitcoin Trust (IBIT) shares was executed at a 2.3 percent discount to the net asset value. NYDIG’s analysis interprets the discount and the trade size as evidence of a rapid exit by a large investor rather than a typical basis‑trade unwind. The firm also points to the absence of any corresponding activity in CME Bitcoin futures, which undermines the hypothesis that a hedge fund was unwinding a hedged position. Institutional investors should note that such sizable, swift exits can pressure IBIT’s market price and signal shifts in large‑holder sentiment toward Bitcoin exposure.

How Stellar (XLM) became part of DTCC's plan to bring securities onchain

CoinDesk • Tokenization • Score: 8/10

DTCC has chosen the Stellar public blockchain as the technical foundation for its forthcoming tokenized securities settlement platform, building on a decade‑long partnership with Securrency now operating as DTCC Digital Assets. The integration will embed compliance mechanisms such as clawbacks and transfer restrictions directly into the Stellar ledger, allowing on‑chain issuance and settlement of assets including U.S. Treasuries and index products. By leveraging Stellar, DTCC aims to provide institutional investors with faster, more transparent and potentially lower‑cost settlement of securities while maintaining regulatory safeguards.

Coinbase makes a major play for India’s booming $3 billion crypto market with local currency launch

CoinDesk • Payments • Score: 7/10

Coinbase is entering India’s rapidly expanding crypto market, estimated at $3 billion. Starting June 1 2026 the exchange will enable direct INR deposits and withdrawals through the Immediate Payment Service, eliminating reliance on peer‑to‑peer or third‑party channels. The platform will also offer spot and perpetual futures contracts backed by local liquidity, broadening product access for institutional traders. Registration with the Financial Intelligence Unit underscores Coinbase’s intent to maintain a long‑term regulated presence in the region.

How the House Financial Services Committee is taking on tokenization: State of Crypto

CoinDesk • Tokenization • Score: 6/10

The House Financial Services Committee announced tokenization as its next major focus, building on prior work on stablecoins and market structure. Chair French Hill noted that the committee has already advanced bipartisan legislation such as the GENIUS Act and the Clarity Act to provide regulatory clarity for digital assets. Hill said the Senate is likely to adopt many of the House provisions, indicating coordinated federal action that could shape institutional investment strategies in tokenized assets.

Dev helps rescue $2 million locked in 2016 ICO contract for nine years with whitehat exploit

The Block • Security • Score: 5/10

Security researcher Florent rescued roughly 1,003 ETH, valued at about $2 million, that had been locked in the 2016 HongCoin ICO contract for nine years. He exploited an overflow bug in an admin mint function to enable the HongCoin multisig team to release the funds. The recovery shows how white‑hat interventions can unlock dormant crypto assets and protect investor capital. Two investors have already reclaimed a portion of the released ETH.
