Bitcoin News: A massive $1.26 billion sale of BlackRock’s IBIT was likely a rapid exit by a large investor, NYDIG says

How Stellar (XLM) became part of DTCC's plan to bring securities onchain

CoinDesk • Tokenization • Score: 8/10

DTCC has chosen the Stellar public blockchain as the technical foundation for its forthcoming tokenized securities settlement platform. This decision builds on a partnership that began a decade ago with Securrency, now operating as DTCC Digital Assets. By embedding compliance mechanisms such as clawbacks and transfer restrictions directly into Stellar, the platform will meet regulatory requirements for on‑chain transactions. The solution is intended to support the issuance and settlement of traditional assets including U.S. Treasuries and index products, offering institutional investors a blockchain‑based settlement infrastructure.

Coinbase makes a major play for India’s booming $3 billion crypto market with local currency launch

CoinDesk • Payments • Score: 7/10

Coinbase is entering India’s rapidly expanding crypto market, which is estimated at $3 billion. The exchange will enable direct Indian‑rupee deposits and withdrawals through the Immediate Payment Service starting June 1, 2026, eliminating the need for peer‑to‑peer or third‑party channels. It will also launch spot and perpetual futures products backed by local liquidity. Registration with the Financial Intelligence Unit underscores Coinbase’s intention to maintain a long‑term regulated presence in the region.

How the House Financial Services Committee is taking on tokenization: State of Crypto

CoinDesk • Tokenization • Score: 6/10

The House Financial Services Committee, led by Chair French Hill, announced that tokenization will become its next major legislative focus. This shift follows the committee’s recent work on stablecoins and broader market‑structure reforms. The panel has already advanced bipartisan proposals such as the GENIUS Act and the Clarity Act, which aim to provide regulatory clarity for digital assets. Hill indicated that the Senate is expected to adopt many of the House’s provisions, suggesting a coordinated congressional effort that could shape the regulatory landscape for tokenized assets.

Dev helps rescue $2 million locked in 2016 ICO contract for nine years with whitehat exploit

The Block • Security • Score: 5/10

Security researcher Florent used a white‑hat exploit to free roughly 1,003 ETH, valued at about $2 million, that had been locked in the HongCoin ICO contract since 2016. The funds remained inaccessible for nine years because an overflow bug in the contract’s admin mint function prevented withdrawals. By identifying and safely triggering the overflow, Florent enabled the HongCoin multisig team to release the assets. Two investors have already reclaimed a portion of the recovered ETH, highlighting the potential for similar recoveries in legacy token sales.
