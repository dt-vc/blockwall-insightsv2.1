DBS, Southeast Asia’s largest bank, partners with VC giant Granite Asia to counter the region's lack of capital

Angelica Ang,Andrew Staples • Investment • Score: 7/10

DBS, Southeast Asia's largest bank by assets, has partnered with Granite Asia, a VC firm managing $10 billion in assets, to launch a $110 million AI-focused IPO fund exclusively for DBS wealth clients. This fund, the first in a three-year collaboration, targets initial public offerings of high-growth AI and tech startups in Asia, addressing the region's capital shortage amid limited funding options compared to Western markets. DBS will support Granite Asia's funds and portfolio companies with services like subscription financing, M&A advisory, and IPO preparation, while Granite Asia offers early access to listings from its network that recently achieved five IPOs and 10 filings. The partnership matters for institutional investors as it provides differentiated exposure to Asia's expanding AI ecosystem, with broad participation from Southeast Asia, South Asia, and Europe.
