Aave secures emergency hearing to void ‘catastrophic’ restraining order

DL News • DeFi • Score: 6/10

Aave has filed an emergency motion to overturn a restraining order that blocks the transfer of roughly $71 million in frozen crypto tied to the Kelp DAO hack. The upcoming hearing will decide if the assets are released to a recovery fund or claimed by victims of a North Korean‑linked theft. The outcome could set a legal precedent for how decentralized finance protocols handle frozen assets and influence broader DeFi security practices.

Bitcoin tops $80,000 price as Clarity Act nears Senate floor with new Fed chair imminent

DL News • Bitcoin • Score: 7/10

Bitcoin surpassed $80,000, reflecting strong institutional demand and a $630 million inflow into U.S. spot Bitcoin ETFs on the first day of May. The price breakout underscores growing acceptance of crypto assets among large investors seeking exposure to a digital store‑of‑value. At the same time, Senate Banking Committee chair Tim Scott indicated that the bipartisan Clarity Act is moving toward markup and a floor vote, which could provide clearer regulatory guidance for digital assets. The legislative progress coincides with expectations that a new Federal Reserve chair will be confirmed, adding another macro‑policy variable for institutional portfolio considerations.

Clarity Act odds surge on stablecoin compromise, Coinbase support

DL News • Regulation • Score: 6/10

U.S. Senators have reached a compromise that would cap interest or yield on stablecoins, raising the projected likelihood of the Clarity Act’s enactment in 2026 to 64 percent. The proposed legislation would prohibit payments that function like traditional bank interest while still permitting reward mechanisms tied to transaction activity and DeFi liquidity provision. Coinbase chief executive Brian Armstrong has publicly indicated support for the forthcoming markup, signaling potential industry alignment that may influence institutional exposure to regulated stablecoin products.

Ethereum devs huddled in the Arctic Circle to fix the network. Here’s what to know

DL News • Ethereum • Score: 6/10

Ethereum core developers gathered in Longyearbyen, Svalbard for a week‑long interoperability session. The meeting resulted in a hard‑coded 200 million gas‑limit floor to increase transaction throughput. Developers also completed the repricing of EIP‑8037, which is intended to improve fee predictability and network capacity. In parallel, the Ethereum Foundation sold $23 million of ETH to Bitmine Immersion Technologies in an over‑the‑counter transaction to finance ongoing development work.

The new stack for global finance: Stablecoins edition - a16z crypto

a16z • Stablecoins • Score: 5/10

Stablecoins are increasingly being positioned as the foundational layer for a new on‑chain banking‑as‑a‑service stack. This stack allows developers to launch global financial products without obtaining traditional banking licenses. The shift reduces friction for cross‑border payments and opens new revenue streams for fintech firms. Established players such as Stripe and Mastercard are acquiring niche companies to capture strategic components of this emerging infrastructure.

‘Every hit earns crypto’? App that gives users Bitcoin for getting stoned deemed ‘dangerous’ and ‘unethical’

DL News • Payments • Score: 3/10

California-based startup Gudtrip has launched a mobile app that awards users Bitcoin for each inhalation from its cannabis vape pens. The platform gamifies consumption by increasing points for daily use and converting those points into cryptocurrency rewards. Health researchers and anti‑drug advocacy groups have criticized the scheme as dangerous because it may encourage repeated use and deepen dependence. The controversy highlights regulatory and ethical risks for investors considering exposure to crypto‑linked substance‑use products.

GameStop Doesn’t Have Enough Stock

Bloomberg • Other • Score: 2/10

GameStop, the publicly traded video‑game retailer, is currently facing an inventory shortfall that leaves it unable to fully satisfy customer demand. Bloomberg notes that the limited stock could weigh on the company’s sales and earnings in the near term. The situation is material for institutional investors because reduced revenue potential may affect profitability forecasts and valuation metrics.
