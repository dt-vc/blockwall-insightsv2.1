Aave secures emergency hearing to void ‘catastrophic’ restraining order

DL News • DeFi • Score: 6/10

Aave has filed an emergency motion to overturn a restraining order that currently blocks the transfer of roughly $71 million in frozen cryptocurrency linked to the Kelp DAO hack. The restraining order was described by Aave as catastrophic because it prevents the assets from being moved to a recovery fund. The upcoming hearing will decide whether the frozen crypto can be released to a recovery fund or claimed by victims of a theft attributed to North Korean actors. The outcome could set a legal precedent for how decentralized finance protocols handle large‑scale hacks and asset recovery.

Bitcoin tops $80,000 price as Clarity Act nears Senate floor with new Fed chair imminent

DL News • Bitcoin • Score: 7/10

Bitcoin surpassed $80,000, reflecting heightened institutional buying pressure. The price move was accompanied by $630 million flowing into U.S. spot Bitcoin exchange‑traded funds on the first day of May. At the same time, Senate Banking Committee chair Tim Scott indicated that the bipartisan Clarity Act is moving toward markup and a floor vote, which should reduce regulatory uncertainty for digital‑asset investors. Market participants are also watching the imminent appointment of a new Federal Reserve chair, a development that could influence monetary conditions and crypto valuations.

Clarity Act odds surge on stablecoin compromise, Coinbase support

DL News • Regulation • Score: 6/10

U.S. Senators have reached a bipartisan agreement to cap interest or yield on stablecoins, a move that raises the projected likelihood of the Clarity Act being enacted in 2026 to 64 percent. The proposed legislation would prohibit payments that function like traditional bank interest while still permitting reward mechanisms tied to transaction volume and decentralized finance liquidity provision. Coinbase chief executive Brian Armstrong has publicly indicated his company's support for the forthcoming markup of the bill. Institutional investors are watching the development closely because the regulatory framework will shape the risk‑return profile of stablecoin holdings and related DeFi strategies.

Ethereum devs huddled in the Arctic Circle to fix the network. Here’s what to know

DL News • Ethereum • Score: 6/10

Ethereum core developers gathered in Longyearbyen, Svalbard for a week‑long interoperability workshop aimed at addressing network performance. The meeting resulted in the adoption of a 200 million gas‑limit floor, which is intended to raise the baseline capacity for transaction processing. Participants also finalized the repricing of EIP‑8037, a change designed to improve fee economics and further support scalability. In parallel, the Ethereum Foundation sold $23 million of ETH to Bitmine Immersion Technologies in an over‑the‑counter transaction to finance continued development work.

The new stack for global finance: Stablecoins edition - a16z crypto

a16z • Stablecoins • Score: 5/10

Stablecoins are increasingly being positioned as the foundational layer for an on‑chain banking‑as‑a‑service stack that can deliver global financial products. This stack allows issuers to offer services without obtaining traditional banking licenses, reducing regulatory friction. Major payment and technology incumbents such as Stripe and Mastercard are actively acquiring specialized firms to capture components of this emerging infrastructure. Institutional investors should monitor the consolidation trend because control of the stablecoin plumbing could shape the future of cross‑border finance and open new revenue streams.

‘Every hit earns crypto’? App that gives users Bitcoin for getting stoned deemed ‘dangerous’ and ‘unethical’

DL News • Payments • Score: 3/10

California‑based startup Gudtrip has launched a mobile app that awards Bitcoin to users for each inhalation from its cannabis vape pens. The app uses a points system that increases rewards for daily use, effectively gamifying consumption. Health researchers have expressed concern that the incentive structure could encourage higher rates of use and potential addiction. Anti‑drug advocates have called the model unethical and a public‑health risk, raising regulatory scrutiny for investors.
