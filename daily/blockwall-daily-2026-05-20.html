ETH News: Vitalik Buterin outlines Ethereum's privacy measures. Here is what it actually means

CoinDesk • Ethereum • Score: 7/10

Vitalik Buterin has outlined three near‑term upgrades that will embed privacy directly into the Ethereum protocol. The upgrades include Account Abstraction with FOCIL, the introduction of keyed nonces, and the deployment of access‑layer tools such as the Kohaku toolkit. Together they are designed to make private transactions uncensorable and to conceal wallet query data from centralized node providers. This development responds to growing institutional demand for on‑chain privacy and could reduce reliance on external privacy solutions.

Federal Reserve proposes limited master accounts long pursued by crypto firms

CoinDesk • Regulation • Score: 6/10

The U.S. Federal Reserve has issued a revised proposal to establish limited master accounts that would enable crypto firms to settle transactions in U.S. dollars. These LMAs build on the earlier “skinny account” concept and are intended to give the central bank a clearer, risk‑controlled framework for overseeing crypto‑related payment services. By providing a regulated conduit for fiat settlement, the proposal aims to reduce systemic risk and improve the integration of digital‑asset businesses with the traditional financial system.

Crypto custody firm Copper is looking to sell the company for $500 million

CoinDesk • Enterprise • Score: 6/10

Copper, a crypto custody provider, is exploring a sale that could value the company at about $500 million with Wall Street bank Cantor Fitzgerald advising. The transaction is driven primarily by Copper’s ClearLoop settlement system, which is used by more than 1,000 counterparties and processes roughly $50 billion of monthly notional volume. Copper previously shut its broader enterprise custody business to concentrate on ClearLoop as the crypto IPO market remains subdued. Institutional investors may view the sale as an opportunity to acquire a platform that underpins significant settlement activity in the digital asset ecosystem.
