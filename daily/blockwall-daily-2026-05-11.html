Bitcoin (BTC) mining pools with 75% of hashrate back open standard for block construction

CoinDesk • Bitcoin • Score: 7/10

Seven of the largest Bitcoin mining pools, which together control roughly 75% of the network hashrate, have formally joined the Stratum V2 working group. Stratum V2 is an open‑source protocol that enables individual miners to construct their own block templates rather than relying on pool operators for transaction selection. By shifting block‑building authority to miners, the protocol aims to mitigate centralization risks associated with pool‑controlled transaction ordering. This development is significant for institutional investors because it enhances the decentralization and security of the Bitcoin network while preserving transparent transaction inclusion.

Wall Street giants are triggering a massive fee war that could crush crypto exchange margins

CoinDesk • Payments • Score: 6/10

Morgan Stanley has added cryptocurrency trading to its E*Trade platform and is charging a 0.50 percent commission. The fee is lower than the rates charged by established U.S. exchanges such as Coinbase, Robinhood and Charles Schwab, prompting a competitive pricing battle among major market participants. Analysts say the resulting fee war could erode profit margins for those exchanges while delivering lower transaction costs to retail traders, a development that institutional investors need to monitor for its impact on market liquidity and pricing dynamics.
