Ronin gaming sidechain gets ready to transition to Ethereum layer 2

CoinDesk • Layer2 • Score: 7/10

Ronin, the blockchain that powers the Axie Infinity game, will undergo a hard fork on May 12 to shift from its own sidechain to an Ethereum layer‑2 built on the OP Stack. The migration is intended to reduce token inflation, enhance security and improve scalability following a $625 million bridge exploit. The transition is expected to cause a brief network downtime of about ten hours, which investors should factor into operational planning.

Arbitrum DAO Granted Liability Reprieve in North Korea Asset Forfeiture Case on Bankless

Bankless • Regulation • Score: 7/10

A U.S. federal judge has ruled that the Arbitrum DAO is permitted to move approximately $70 million in frozen Ether, which was seized following the KelpDAO exploit, to a wallet managed by Aave LLC. The ruling removes a restraining order that previously blocked the transfer, allowing the DAO to initiate the asset relocation. However, the funds remain under the court’s jurisdiction and any final disposition will require additional judicial approval, underscoring the ongoing legal scrutiny of decentralized finance asset forfeiture cases.

Bitcoin (BTC) mining pools with 75% of hashrate back open standard for block construction

CoinDesk • Bitcoin • Score: 7/10

Seven of the largest Bitcoin mining pools, which together represent about three quarters of the network’s computing power, have become members of the Stratum V2 working group. By adopting the open‑source Stratum V2 protocol, miners can now construct their own block templates rather than relying on pool‑directed transaction selection. This shift reduces the influence of pool operators over which transactions are included, mitigating centralization risk in the Bitcoin mining ecosystem. Institutional investors should note that the change could enhance network resilience and align mining practices with broader decentralization goals.

Wall Street giants are triggering a massive fee war that could crush crypto exchange margins

CoinDesk • Payments • Score: 6/10

Morgan Stanley has introduced crypto trading on its E*Trade platform with a 0.50% transaction fee. The fee is lower than those charged by U.S. exchanges such as Coinbase, Robinhood and Schwab, prompting a competitive fee war among Wall Street firms. Analysts say the pressure on pricing could compress margins for the incumbent exchanges while offering retail traders more affordable access to digital assets.

Bankrupt Yield Fund
