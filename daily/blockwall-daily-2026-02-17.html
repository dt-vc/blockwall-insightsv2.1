Crypto's $300 billion stablecoin supply is increasingly used as 'everyday money,' global study finds

Naga Avan-Nomayo • Stablecoins • Score: 6/10

A global BVNK study surveying 4,600 crypto users across 15 countries reveals that the $300 billion stablecoin supply is shifting from trading to everyday uses like paychecks, purchases, cross-border payments, and savings, with 39% receiving income in stablecoins and 52% making merchant purchases. Institutional adoption is accelerating, as 13% of financial institutions and corporates already utilize stablecoins primarily for supplier payments and liquidity management, while 54% plan to adopt soon amid $33 trillion in 2025 transaction volume surpassing Visa. This trend matters for institutions due to efficiency gains in treasury operations, projected market growth to $1 trillion by late 2026, and regulatory clarity like the GENIUS Act, positioning early adopters for competitive advantages in global payments potentially comprising 25-50% stablecoin value by 2030.
