Crypto industry reels as April sees highest number of hacks ever

DL News • Security • Score: 9/10

April saw a record 29 crypto hacks, the highest monthly total ever reported. The attacks generated a combined loss of $579 million, with the largest hits coming from the Drift exchange and Kelp DAO. Analysis of the incidents shows that most were traced to code bugs and single points of failure within decentralized finance protocols. The surge underscores heightened security risk for institutional capital deployed in DeFi and may prompt tighter due‑diligence and risk‑mitigation measures.

Is Aave too Big to Fail?

Messari • DeFi • Score: 8/10

The rsETH exploit resulted in a $193 M loss for Aave, prompting a coordinated response from the broader DeFi community. DeFi United contributed more than $300 M, allowing Aave to fully reimburse affected depositors. Although Aave’s total value locked fell 45 %, it still controls about half of the top‑six lending protocols and analysts consider it undervalued with upcoming catalysts not yet reflected in price.

Hegseth recasts Bitcoin as national security asset amid Russia, China expansion

DL News • Bitcoin • Score: 6/10

U.S. Secretary of War Pete Hegseth informed Congress that the Pentagon is conducting classified Bitcoin initiatives, framing the digital asset as a component of national‑security strategy. A Navy admiral subsequently confirmed that the military maintains an operational Bitcoin node, indicating active involvement in the cryptocurrency’s infrastructure. The statements underscore heightened geopolitical competition, as Russian and Chinese mining expansions are prompting U.S. defense agencies to explore crypto capabilities for strategic resilience.

Paradigm Files Comment Letter on the OCC’s GENIUS Rulemaking

Paradigm • Regulation • Score: 5/10

Paradigm submitted a comment letter to the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency objecting to the agency’s proposed rule under the GENIUS Act, which the firm says expands the yield prohibition for payment stablecoins beyond the intended scope. In the letter Paradigm urges the OCC to adopt safe harbor provisions, reduce reporting burdens, and clarify rules for white labeling and cross chain transfers to preserve stablecoin innovation. Institutional investors are watching the outcome because regulatory certainty will shape the risk profile and deployment of stablecoin‑based payment solutions.

Tokenized Treasury securities are seeing greater use as collateral on exchanges, reflecting a shift toward more regulated assets.

The transaction is part of SBI’s broader strategy to unify regulated trading platforms in Japan. The move aligns with upcoming regulatory changes that will bring crypto assets under the Financial Instruments and Exchange Act, increasing oversight of exchanges.
