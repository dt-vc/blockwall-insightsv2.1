LayerZero says it ‘made a mistake’ in $292 Million Kelp exploit

CoinDesk • Security • Score: 9/10

LayerZero has acknowledged a critical mistake that enabled a $292 million exploit of the Kelp protocol. The breach occurred because LayerZero’s verifier was permitted to secure high‑value transfers while operating in a vulnerable configuration. The company has publicly stated that it “owns” the error that led to the loss. The incident highlights heightened security risks for cross‑chain messaging infrastructure that underpins many decentralized finance applications.

Bitcoin news: Soon, traders will be able to bet on BTC volatility, not just price, on CME

CME Group has announced plans to launch Bitcoin volatility futures. The contracts are slated for June 1, subject to regulatory approval. This addition broadens CME’s crypto derivatives suite, giving institutional investors a new tool for managing exposure to Bitcoin volatility.

Court Lets Arbitrum DAO Transfer $71M in ETH Tied to North Korea Hack to Aave

Cointelegraph • DeFi • Score: 7/10

A New York court has approved the Arbitrum DAO’s request to move approximately $71 million in ETH to the Aave protocol. The transfer is part of the ongoing recovery effort related to the Kelp DAO exploit that authorities have linked to North Korean hackers. By clearing the legal obstacle, the ruling could facilitate future DeFi restitution cases and may support the stability of the rsETH collateral that underpins the Arbitrum network.

Judge clears path for Aave to move $71 million in ETH linked to North Korea hack

CoinDesk • Regulation • Score: 7/10

U.S. District Judge Margaret Garnett ruled that $71 million worth of ETH, which had been frozen after a North Korean‑linked hack on the Arbitrum network, may be transferred to the Aave lending protocol. The ruling removes a legal barrier that had prevented Aave from integrating the seized assets into its platform. Institutional investors view the decision as a precedent that could shape how future cryptocurrency seizure cases are handled and influence compliance and asset‑recovery strategies.

Strategy CEO Outlines Criteria for Bitcoin Sales

Cointelegraph • Bitcoin • Score: 6/10

Strategy, a publicly traded investment vehicle, holds more than 818,000 Bitcoin, representing roughly four percent of the total supply. The firm has said it will only dispose of Bitcoin when proceeds are needed to pay dividends on its Series A Perpetual Stretch Preferred Stock and to achieve tax efficiency. CEO Phong Le says those sales are intended to be accretive to shareholders. He also expects the transactions will not have a material impact on Bitcoin’s market price, a point that may reassure institutional investors monitoring large‑holder activity.

Crypto industry cheers Senate Clarity Act markup date as market structure push resumes

CoinDesk • Regulation • Score: 6/10

The U.S. Senate announced a markup date for the Crypto Clarity Act, signaling the next legislative step for digital‑asset regulation. The proposal seeks to clarify federal jurisdiction over crypto markets, establish consumer and developer safeguards, and outline reward mechanisms for stable‑coin issuers. By providing a clearer market structure, the bill aims to reduce regulatory uncertainty for institutional investors and could influence capital allocation decisions in the crypto sector.

Trump Media’s Q1 loss widens to $406 million on bitcoin, CRO markdowns

CoinDesk • Enterprise • Score: 5/10

Trump Media & Technology Group reported a first‑quarter net loss of $406 million. The loss was driven primarily by a $244 million unrealized decline in its Bitcoin holdings and a $108.2 million write‑down on its Crypto.com CRO token investment. The outcome highlights the heightened financial risk that corporate exposure to volatile crypto assets can pose for investors.

Crypto wallets are being rebuilt for AI agents, Trust Wallet and Mesh executives say at Consensus Miami

CoinDesk • Infrastructure • Score: 5/10

At the Consensus Miami conference, Trust Wallet CEO Felix Fan and Mesh CTO Arjun Mukherjee announced that crypto wallets are being redesigned to work with AI agents. The new architecture will allow AI agents to have a direct role in managing and interacting with digital assets. This development signals a shift toward more automated and intelligent wallet experiences for users. Institutional investors should watch the evolution as it could impact how digital assets are accessed, secured, and integrated into broader AI‑driven financial workflows.

How DeFi is changing the financial landscape for Latin Americans

CoinDesk • DeFi • Score: 5/10

DeFi is moving from a niche crypto experiment to a mainstream financial tool in Latin America. Global protocols such as Aave are forming partnerships with local fintech firms that deliver user‑friendly interfaces, stablecoins and fiat on‑ramps. These collaborations reduce entry barriers for everyday users and create new opportunities for institutional investors seeking exposure to a rapidly expanding decentralized finance market in the region.

Emerging-market users are treating crypto exchanges like banking apps, Binance says

CoinDesk • Payments • Score: 5/10

Binance reports that users in emerging markets are using its crypto exchange in the same way they would a traditional banking app, conducting everyday financial transactions on the platform. The company cites the high prevalence of unbanked adults, individuals who lack access to credit, and savers who earn no interest as drivers of this behavior. For institutional investors, the trend highlights a growing demand for crypto‑based financial services among underserved populations and a potential avenue for expanding market share.
