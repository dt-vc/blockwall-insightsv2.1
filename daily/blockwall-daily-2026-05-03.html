Linux Copy Fail: ‘A Trivially Exploitable Bug’

Cointelegraph • Security • Score: 5/10

A logic bug in Linux that is trivially exploitable was disclosed after a private report and patched in early April. The vulnerability affects major Linux distributions released over the past nine years and can be leveraged by a small Python script to obtain root on any platform, raising concerns for crypto exchanges and blockchain nodes that rely on Linux. The newly launched Project Glasswing aims to harness AI for defensive security against such threats.

NY Forces Uphold to Pay $5M Over Fraudulent Crypto Product

Cointelegraph • Regulation • Score: 5/10

New York Attorney General Letitia James secured a settlement of more than $5 million from the crypto platform Uphold for promoting the fraudulent CredEarn product. CredEarn was advertised as a safe savings vehicle but was actually funded by risky micro‑loans to low‑income gamers in China and provided no insurance coverage. Uphold denies intentional wrongdoing, will compensate victims, and acknowledged that it operated without the broker registration required under New York law, highlighting regulatory compliance risks for crypto service providers.
