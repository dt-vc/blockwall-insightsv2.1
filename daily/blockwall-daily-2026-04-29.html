Meta (META) starts stablecoin payout to creators in Circle's USDC on Polygon, Solana via Stripe

CoinDesk • Payments • Score: 7/10

Meta has launched a payout system that lets creators receive Circle’s USDC stablecoin on the Polygon and Solana blockchains. The payments are processed through Stripe and the initial rollout is limited to creators in Colombia and the Philippines. Both Meta and Stripe will provide tax reporting for these transactions, underscoring Meta’s renewed focus on stablecoin‑based payment solutions for digital creators.

A federal judge has rejected Sam Bankman‑Fried’s request for a new trial, describing the purported new evidence as baseless. The ruling upholds the conviction that resulted from his 2023 fraud trial. KuCoin EU Hires AML Chief After Austria MiCA Business Ban

Cointelegraph • Regulation • Score: 6/10

KuCoin settled a $300 million AML case with U.S. authorities and paid a $500,000 CFTC civil penalty for operating an unregistered offshore commodities exchange. After an Austrian regulator prohibited its operations, the exchange created a KuCoin EU unit and appointed an AML chief to satisfy the European Union’s Markets in Crypto‑Assets (MiCA) framework. The move is intended to restore the platform’s ability to serve European institutional investors and demonstrate compliance with emerging crypto regulations.

Stable Sea Adds WisdomTree Tokenized Treasury Fund for Corporate Cash

Cointelegraph • Tokenization • Score: 6/10

Stable Sea has incorporated the WisdomTree tokenized Treasury fund into its corporate cash solutions. WisdomTree’s tokenized Treasury fund (WTGXX) received SEC approval for continuous trading and currently holds $857 million in assets. Major institutions including Franklin Templeton, Binance, Standard Chartered, BlackRock and Northern Trust are using the tokenized money‑market fund shares as collateral. The adoption signals growing institutional use of tokenized assets in core market infrastructure.

Bitcoin Drops Under $75K After Fed Decides To Hold Rates: Will Bulls Buy?

They warned that these factors could generate short term downside pressure, prompting institutional investors to monitor risk exposure closely.

DeFi lender Sky hits $124m revenue record. Yet token holders aren’t impressed

DL News • DeFi • Score: 5/10

Sky, a decentralized finance lender with $13 billion in assets under management, posted a record gross revenue of $124 million in Q1 2026. Net revenue for the quarter was $61 million, driven by strong institutional demand for its USDS stablecoin. The earnings beat expectations but the Sky governance token fell about 2.4% after the results were released. The token decline indicates that investors remain cautious about the platform’s valuation despite the revenue surge.
