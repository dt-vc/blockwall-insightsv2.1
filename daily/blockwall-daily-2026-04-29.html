DeFi absorbs $292 million shock as AAVE-led rescue steadies markets: Standard Chartered

CoinDesk • DeFi • Score: 9/10

A $292 million exploit on KelpDAO spread to AAVE and created a sharp liquidity crunch across DeFi. In response, AAVE coordinated a $300 million rescue that was supported by a coalition of DeFi firms. The injection stabilized deposits and yields and demonstrated the sector’s resilience. Standard Chartered said the episode underlines confidence in a projected $2 trillion tokenised real‑world asset market by 2028.

Meta (META) starts stablecoin payout to creators in Circle's USDC on Polygon, Solana via Stripe

CoinDesk • Payments • Score: 7/10

Meta has begun paying creators in Circle’s USDC stablecoin on the Polygon and Solana blockchains, with the transaction flow powered by Stripe. The initial rollout is limited to creators in Colombia and the Philippines and includes tax reporting from both Meta and Stripe. The feature underscores Meta’s renewed push into stablecoin‑based payments and could broaden crypto adoption in digital content monetization.

Judge dismisses SBF ‘facts,’ new trial request in scathing order

DL News • Regulation • Score: 6/10

A federal judge rejected Sam Bankman‑Fried’s petition for a new trial, describing the purported new evidence as baseless. The ruling upholds the conviction that resulted from his 2023 fraud trial. The decision reduces the likelihood of a pardon or successful appeal, a development that institutional investors should monitor given the broader implications for crypto market stability and regulatory scrutiny.

Robinhood (HOOD), Coinbase (COIN) plunge in crypto stock rout, outpacing bitcoin (BTC) decline

CoinDesk • Enterprise • Score: 6/10

Robinhood’s shares fell roughly 14% after the company reported a 47% decline in crypto‑related revenue. Investors are also watching the upcoming Federal Reserve policy decision for its potential impact on risk assets.

Wall Street is coming to Consensus Miami — and it’s not just to watch

CoinDesk • Enterprise • Score: 6/10

Wall Street firms including Morgan Stanley and JPMorgan are sponsoring and speaking at Consensus Miami, underscoring a growing alignment between traditional finance and digital assets. The event will bring together high‑profile policymakers and crypto industry leaders to discuss topics such as stablecoins, tokenization, and the emerging concept of agentic commerce. Institutional investors can view the conference as a signal that major financial institutions are actively engaging with the crypto ecosystem and shaping its regulatory and commercial frameworks.

Bitcoin Drops Range Highs As Traders Cut Risk Ahead Of FOMC

Cointelegraph • Bitcoin • Score: 5/10

Bitcoin fell from its recent high of $79,500 as market participants reduced exposure ahead of the Federal Open Market Committee meeting. The pullback reflects a broader risk‑off sentiment among traders who anticipate potential volatility around monetary‑policy decisions. Historical patterns indicate that Bitcoin often experiences corrective moves after FOMC announcements. Recent liquidations have already reached billions of dollars, underscoring the scale of capital that can be affected by policy‑driven market shifts.

DeFi lender Sky hits $124m revenue record. Yet token holders aren’t impressed

DL News • DeFi • Score: 5/10

Sky, a DeFi lender with $13 billion in total value locked, posted a record first‑quarter gross revenue of $124 million and net revenue of $61 million. The earnings surge was driven by strong institutional demand for its USDS stablecoin. Nevertheless, the Sky governance token fell about 2.4% after the results were released, reflecting market disappointment.

Billionaire trader Paul Tudor Jones made bank in gold. Now he calls Bitcoin the ‘best inflation hedge’

DL News • Bitcoin • Score: 5/10

Paul Tudor Jones, a billionaire hedge‑fund manager who previously profited from gold, has publicly declared Bitcoin the best inflation hedge. He argues that Bitcoin’s fixed supply gives it an advantage over gold, whose supply can increase. His endorsement aligns him with other Wall Street leaders such as Larry Fink, though he also cautions investors about the cryptocurrency’s cyber‑risk exposure.
