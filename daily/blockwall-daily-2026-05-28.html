U.S. CFTC opens crypto 'perp' door with first approval at regulated firm

CoinDesk • Regulation • Score: 9/10

The U.S. Commodity Futures Trading Commission has issued its first approval for bitcoin perpetual futures on a regulated exchange. The approval authorizes the exchange Kalshi to list the contract identified as BTCPERP. It also permits a Coinbase affiliate to direct U.S. customers to global options and perpetual contracts. The ruling creates a regulatory framework for crypto derivatives in the United States and is viewed as a significant step toward broader market adoption.

Bitcoin drops below $73,000 as U.S. strikes on Iran spark $1 billion liquidations

CoinDesk • Bitcoin • Score: 8/10

Bitcoin slipped below the $73,000 threshold following U.S. airstrikes on Iran, triggering a broad sell‑off across the cryptocurrency market. The price decline generated nearly $1 billion in leveraged liquidations, highlighting the sensitivity of margin‑based strategies to sudden risk events. Long positions were the primary source of the wipe‑out, with Bitcoin and Ether together accounting for the majority of the losses. The episode underscores the importance for institutional investors of monitoring geopolitical developments and managing exposure to leveraged crypto assets.

Samsung is buying a $408 million stake in South Korea’s biggest crypto exchange

The transaction lowers Kakao’s ownership after Kakao disposed of roughly $1.5 billion of equity in the exchange within a month. This eliminates the traditional weekend market closure that produced the “CME gap.” The continuous trading window gives institutional investors the ability to hedge positions at any time while the exchange still observes a short maintenance period on Saturday.

Wall Street’s trillion-dollar dilemma: Why AI-powered hackers are keeping big banks off the blockchain

CoinDesk • Security • Score: 7/10

CertiK’s chief executive has warned that AI driven attacks made April the worst month for decentralized finance security in four years. The attacks were recorded on 27 of the 30 days in that month. High profile incidents include a $1.46 billion breach of Bybit and roughly $600 million stolen from Drift Protocol and Kelp Dao. These losses are prompting large banks and other Wall Street firms to pause or limit the migration of trillions of dollars onto blockchain platforms.

CoinDesk • Bitcoin • Score: 7/10

BlackRock's iShares Bitcoin Trust experienced a net outflow of $528 million, marking the second‑largest single‑day withdrawal since the fund’s inception. The broader U.S. spot Bitcoin ETF market also saw outflows totaling $733 million on the same day. The withdrawals occurred as Bitcoin fell below $73,000 amid heightened Middle‑East tensions, prompting forced sales of the fund’s underlying Bitcoin and raising liquidity concerns for institutional investors.

UniCredit warns Europe may struggle to contain crypto-bank crisis under MiCA rules

CoinDesk • Regulation • Score: 6/10

UniCredit, a leading European bank, has warned that the EU’s deposit‑insurance framework may not be robust enough to absorb a crypto‑bank shock under the new MiCA rules. The bank points to a gap between the EU’s partial guarantees and the broader protection offered in the United States. Without full‑coverage deposit guarantees, large stablecoin reserve accounts could be exposed to significant losses if a crisis hits the crypto‑banking sector. Institutional investors therefore need to reassess the resilience of their stablecoin holdings and related liquidity strategies in Europe.

This integration enables token holders to borrow against the Treasury‑backed token on Euler’s decentralized finance lending platform. The development bridges traditional fixed‑income securities with DeFi, offering institutional investors a new way to leverage low‑risk government debt in crypto‑based liquidity strategies.

Grayscale, one of the largest crypto asset managers, has postponed its planned U.S. IPO until at least Q4 2026 because of weak market conditions and low investor demand. The postponement signals a broader cooling of the crypto IPO market. Other crypto firms such as Payward, ConsenSys and Ledger are also delaying their public listings, underscoring the sector’s current challenges for institutional investors.
