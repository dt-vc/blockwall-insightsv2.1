North Korea behind $286m Drift Protocol hack, investigators say

DL News • Security • Score: 9/10

Investigators attribute the $286 million theft from Drift Protocol to a North Korean‑linked hacking group. The attack on the Solana‑based non‑custodial platform used social engineering of multisignature signers and private‑key compromise, mirroring tactics seen in the Bybit hack. The breach has heightened institutional concerns about DeFi security and drawn criticism of stablecoin issuer Circle for a slow response.

Weekly Top Stories: Drift drained for $285m

Circle announces plans to debut wrapped Bitcoin for institutions. Here’s what it means

DL News • Tokenization • Score: 6/10

Circle has introduced cirBTC, a wrapped token that is fully backed by Bitcoin on a 1:1 basis and is targeted at institutional investors. The token will initially be issued on the Ethereum and Arc blockchains and Circle has outlined a multichain roadmap to extend its availability. By providing a regulated Bitcoin‑linked asset, Circle aims to meet growing institutional demand for Bitcoin liquidity in decentralized finance, though the design also raises questions about centralization of the token’s custody.

Why Coinbase’s team-up with Amazon, Google and Stripe can turbo-charge AI agents

DL News • Payments • Score: 6/10

Coinbase has joined the newly created x402 Foundation, which is governed by the Linux Foundation and includes tech giants Amazon, Google and Microsoft/Stripe. The foundation is developing the x402 protocol as a universal standard for embedding stablecoin payments directly into web interactions. This protocol is designed to allow AI agents to execute autonomous transactions using stablecoins. Venture capitalists view the initiative as a catalyst for AI driven payments and project a $5 trillion market by 2030.

Ethereum Foundation stakes $93 million of ether in a day, reaching its 70,000 ETH target

CoinDesk • Ethereum • Score: 6/10

The Ethereum Foundation, the nonprofit organization that oversees the Ethereum protocol, has increased its ETH stake to support network security and generate revenue. A single deposit of $93 million worth of ether raised its total staked holdings to roughly $143 million, bringing it close to its target of 70,000 ETH. By shifting from selling ETH to fund operations toward earning an estimated $3.9 to $5.4 million per year in staking yield, the foundation is creating a sustainable funding source for development and ecosystem initiatives.

Q1 was bad for crypto, but industry say things are looking up

DL News • Bitcoin • Score: 5/10

The crypto market contracted by more than 20% in the first quarter, with Bitcoin and Ethereum exchange‑traded funds experiencing a combined $1.2 billion of outflows. Despite the downturn, institutional investors are starting to redeploy capital into the sector. Recent inflows into Bitcoin ETFs are being interpreted as an early sign of a market rebound. The trajectory of the recovery will depend on broader macroeconomic variables such as geopolitical tensions and Federal Reserve policy.

Dunamu x Naver Financial: Merger Delayed

Tiger Research • Regulation • Score: 6/10

The Korea Fair Trade Commission has extended its review of the planned share exchange between Dunamu, the operator of Upbit, and Naver Financial, pushing the expected closing from June to September. The Financial Supervisory Service also issued a correction order on Dunamu’s disclosure related to the transaction. The merger, valued at roughly KRW 20 trillion (about $13.7 billion), is designed to integrate a cryptocurrency exchange, payments platform and artificial‑intelligence capabilities. Regulators and investors see the combined entity as a potential catalyst for the development of stablecoins and real‑world‑asset tokenization in South Korea.
