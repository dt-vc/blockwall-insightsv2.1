Bitcoin enters a reset phase: Whales sell as BTC leverage gets flushed

Ishika Kumari • Bitcoin • Score: 6/10

Bitcoin is entering a reset phase as large whales, including the Hyperunit wallet linked to Garrett Jin, sell substantial holdings, with over $340 million in BTC sent to Binance and positions dropping from a peak of $11.5 billion to $2.2 billion. This deleveraging follows the unraveling of aggressive leveraged bets on BTC, ETH, and Solana, which incurred heavy losses and exposed risks amid weak market signals and ETF outflows. For institutional investors, the selling signals a shift toward caution and capital preservation, potentially stabilizing prices after profit-taking from coins acquired near $50,000 while derivatives markets see rising perp exposure. This reset may create opportunities for selective accumulation, as some whales quietly add BTC during dips near $71,000-$73,000 despite broader spot weakness.
