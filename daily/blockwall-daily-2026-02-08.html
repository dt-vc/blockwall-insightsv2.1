Tether helped Turkish investigators by freezing half a billion dollars in crypto: report

Mathew Di Salvo • Stablecoins • Score: 7/10

Tether froze approximately $544 million in USDT wallets at the request of Turkish authorities investigating Veysel Sahin, who is accused of operating illegal online betting platforms and laundering proceeds through cryptocurrency. This action, supported by Turkey's Financial Crimes Investigation Board (MASAK) and Istanbul prosecutors, immobilized funds linked to a major gambling syndicate, marking one of the largest stablecoin freezes on record. For institutional investors, it underscores Tether's capacity to blacklist addresses via smart contract controls, demonstrating robust compliance with over 1,800 law enforcement cases across 62 countries and $3.4 billion in prior freezes. Such cooperation highlights stablecoins' role in anti-money laundering efforts, potentially reducing counterparty risk amid rising global regulatory scrutiny.
