Digital Asset Holdings Targets $300M in Latest Capital Raise

Cointelegraph • Tokenization • Score: 9/10

Digital Asset Holdings has set a target of $300 million for its latest capital raise, reflecting heightened investor confidence in tokenized finance solutions. The fundraising effort coincides with broader enterprise adoption of blockchain‑based assets, as the Depository Trust & Clearing Corporation (DTCC) pilots tokenized securities. Moody’s has begun publishing credit ratings directly on‑chain and the Japan Securities Clearing Corporation (JSCC) is testing on‑chain issuance of government bonds. Swiss bank Amina’s support for the Canton Coin further signals institutional interest in digital‑asset ecosystems.

Policy at Consensus Miami: State of Crypto

CoinDesk • Regulation • Score: 7/10

At the Consensus Miami conference, White House adviser Patrick Witt indicated that the Clarity Act, a comprehensive U.S. crypto regulatory framework, could be enacted as early as July 4. The Clarity Act is designed to provide a comprehensive regulatory framework for digital assets. Institutional investors see the prospect of near‑term legislation as a sign that the U.S. government is increasing its focus on legal certainty for the crypto sector.

Ethereum Down 35% Vs. Bitcoin in a Year: Will ETH Price Decline More in 2026?

Cointelegraph • Ethereum • Score: 4/10

Ethereum has lost more than 35% of its value relative to Bitcoin over the last twelve months. Technical analysts project that the bearish trend may persist, with a possible additional 40% decline in ETH price by 2026. Data from Binance shows growing Ether reserves while Bitcoin reserves are falling, a pattern that signals increasing sell‑side pressure on Ethereum. Institutional investors should view the combined price weakness and reserve dynamics as a heightened risk factor when assessing exposure to ETH in diversified crypto portfolios.

The NFT market was 'oversold' and prices fell too far, says Yuga Labs’ new CEO

CoinDesk • NFT_Gaming • Score: 4/10

The newly appointed chief executive of Yuga Labs has described the overall NFT market as oversold. He says recent price declines are steeper than justified by fundamentals. This view signals a potential upside for the sector as valuations may correct. Institutional investors should watch for a possible rebound in high‑profile NFTs such as the Bored Ape Yacht Club collections.

Agentic commerce will run on crypto rails, PayPal and Google reps tell Consensus Miami

CoinDesk • Payments • Score: 4/10

Senior representatives from PayPal and Google Cloud addressed the Consensus Miami conference about the future of “agentic commerce.” They said that scaling this model will depend on open payment protocols that can interoperate across platforms. They also highlighted the need for machine‑readable merchant catalogs and multi‑party crypto custody solutions to support automated transactions. The remarks indicate a strategic move to embed crypto infrastructure into mainstream e‑commerce payment systems, which could be material for institutional investors evaluating exposure to digital‑asset payment networks.
