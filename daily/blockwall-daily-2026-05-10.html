Policy at Consensus Miami: State of Crypto

CoinDesk • Regulation • Score: 7/10

At the Consensus Miami conference, White House adviser Patrick Witt indicated that the U.S. government is prepared to move forward with the Clarity Act, a comprehensive regulatory framework for crypto assets. Witt suggested that the legislation could be signed into law as early as July 4. The announcement highlighted a growing governmental emphasis on delivering legal certainty for digital assets. Institutional investors are likely to view the prospect of a clear federal framework as a catalyst for broader market participation and risk mitigation.

The NFT market was 'oversold' and prices fell too far, says Yuga Labs’ new CEO

CoinDesk • NFT_Gaming • Score: 4/10

Yuga Labs' newly appointed chief executive has stated that the NFT market is currently oversold. He believes that recent price declines are more severe than warranted, implying that valuations may be undervalued. The assessment points to a potential upside and a rebound opportunity for flagship collections such as Bored Ape Yacht Club, which could be relevant for institutional allocation strategies.

Agentic commerce will run on crypto rails, PayPal and Google reps tell Consensus Miami

CoinDesk • Payments • Score: 4/10

At the Consensus Miami conference senior executives from PayPal and Google Cloud said that the next phase of e‑commerce, described as “agentic commerce,” will run on crypto rails. They explained that scaling this model will depend on open payment protocols, machine‑readable merchant catalogs and multi‑party crypto custody solutions. The remarks indicate that major technology and payments firms are moving to embed cryptocurrency infrastructure in mainstream online transactions, a development that could reshape how institutional investors allocate capital to digital‑payment ecosystems.
