DeFi needs to ‘mature’ if it wants to be the future of finance, hacked Drift Protocol insider says

DL News • DeFi • Score: 9/10

Hackers drained $286 million from the Solana‑based Drift Protocol on April 1. A former Drift Labs insider said the attack shows that decentralized finance must mature and improve security to win mainstream trust. The loss follows a series of high‑value DeFi exploits that have shaken confidence in the sector. Institutional investors are watching these events closely because persistent security gaps could affect the viability of allocating capital to DeFi platforms.

North Korean Cyber Spies Are No Longer Just Remote Threats

Cointelegraph • Security • Score: 9/10

North Korean state‑backed hackers carried out a $285 million exploit on the Drift decentralized exchange, cutting its total value locked in minutes. The same group has been linked to the $1.4 billion loss suffered by the centralized exchange Bybit. Unlike prior remote‑only operations, the attackers attended crypto conferences in person, used social engineering to gain the confidence of multisig signers and then manipulated those signatures. This shift to on‑site infiltration creates a more aggressive threat vector for the DeFi ecosystem and raises security concerns for institutional investors allocating capital to decentralized protocols.

BlackRock and Morgan Stanley square off in Bitcoin ETF ‘fee war’

DL News • Bitcoin • Score: 7/10

Morgan Stanley has introduced a Bitcoin exchange‑traded fund that charges a 14‑basis‑point management fee, positioning it below the fee structures of competitors such as BlackRock. The product is supported by the bank’s network of roughly 16,000 financial advisers who collectively oversee about $7 trillion in assets. By offering a lower‑cost entry point, the ETF is expected to encourage broader institutional participation in Bitcoin investment vehicles.

Roman Storm defends Tornado Cash operation as judge weighs acquittal

DL News • Regulation • Score: 6/10

Roman Storm, who is linked to Tornado Cash, is arguing before a federal judge that he should be cleared of criminal charges tied to his role in operating the privacy‑enhancing mixer. The case centers on allegations that the mixer facilitated illicit transactions, but Storm argues that the technology provides legitimate privacy for lawful users. The judge’s ruling could set a precedent for how U.S. authorities treat crypto mixers and other privacy‑focused DeFi protocols, affecting compliance strategies and risk assessments for institutional investors.

Trump admin tables new crypto 401(k) plans. President's 'Wall Street buddies' are the winners, critics say

DL News • Regulation • Score: 6/10

The Trump administration has delayed a proposal that would allow 401(k) fiduciaries to add crypto and other alternative assets to retirement plans. The U.S. Department of Labor had earlier suggested a rule change that would permit inclusion of major tokens such as Bitcoin and Ethereum. Industry insiders view the change as a step toward broader mainstream adoption of digital assets. Critics including Senator Elizabeth Warren argue that the move could expose retirees to heightened risk and that the president’s Wall Street allies stand to benefit.
