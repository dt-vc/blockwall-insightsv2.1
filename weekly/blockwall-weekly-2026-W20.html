🔥 Top Stories of the Week

Ethereum news (ETH): The $293 million KelpDAO hack shows why DeFi is finally being forced to grow up

CoinDesk • Security • 2026-05-16 • Score: 10/10 Bearish

A $293 million exploit of KelpDAO, linked to a vulnerability in LayerZero’s bridge, highlighted that DeFi’s biggest risks now stem from infrastructure, governance and operational security rather than smart‑contract bugs. Industry leaders such as Lido and Spark say the incident will accelerate a shift toward low‑risk, “boring” DeFi as institutional capital seeks reliability.

Why It Matters
The KelpDAO hack spotlights cross‑chain bridge risk, likely compressing valuations for high‑risk DeFi protocols and driving capital toward audited, low‑risk infrastructure while prompting tighter regulatory scrutiny on bridge security. STORY 2: SpaceX’s massive IPO puts a sizable Bitcoin treasury on a public balance sheet, legitimizing crypto as a corporate asset and opening a new liquidity source that could attract more institutional capital to the broader crypto ecosystem. STORY 3: Saudi Arabia’s $12.5 billion tokenization drive creates a sovereign‑backed stablecoin and RWA pipeline, establishing a regulatory blueprint that could unlock trillions of on‑chain assets and position early‑stage tokenization platforms for rapid scaling. STORY 4: Hana Bank’s $670 million stake in Dunamu and joint stablecoin and tokenized‑security projects validate the Korean market, accelerate infrastructure development and create co‑investment opportunities for Web3 startups seeking regulated entry points. STORY 5: IREN’s $3 billion financing to build AI‑focused data centers merges mining scale with hyperscale compute, potentially lowering cloud costs for blockchain workloads and attracting cross‑industry investors to high‑performance Web3 infrastructure. STORY 6: Bullish’s acquisition of Equiniti to enable on‑chain equity issuance brings deep liquidity and regulatory expertise to tokenized securities, raising the competitive bar for early‑stage tokenization platforms. STORY 7: Cerebras’s blockbuster IPO underscores investor preference for AI hardware over crypto infrastructure, tightening funding for blockchain compute projects and intensifying talent competition between AI and Web3 firms. STORY 8: Elliptic’s $120 million raise to expand AI‑driven compliance tools strengthens the regulatory stack essential for institutional crypto adoption, creating a defensible moat and a likely acquisition target for larger fintech players. STORY 9: Ripple’s $200 million facility from Neuberger Berman boosts liquidity for its brokerage services, deepening network effects for XRP and positioning the platform as a preferred venue for institutional margin financing. STORY 10: Circle’s $222 million raise for the Arc layer‑1 delivers an institution‑focused, compliant blockchain for stablecoins and tokenized assets, giving Circle a strategic infrastructure advantage and locking in high‑value stablecoin flows.

📡 Notable Signals & Patterns

Institutional Shift Toward “Boring” Low‑Risk DeFi The $293 million KelpDAO hack and the $10.8 million Thorchain exploit have sharpened investors’ focus on infrastructure, governance and operational security rather than pure smart‑contract risk. Lido and Spark warned that institutional capital will now favor protocols with audited bridges, modest yields and transparent governance. Expect a migration of new capital into “boring” DeFi products such as custodial lending, on‑chain money‑market funds and audited bridge solutions. 2. Tokenization Liquidity Networks Gaining Critical Mass BlackRock and Janus Henderson secured a $1 billion daily stablecoin liquidity line from Basin, while Animoca‑backed NUVA is onboarding $19 billion of real‑world assets onto Ethereum. JPMorgan’s SEC filing for a tokenized Treasury money‑market fund adds another major custodian to the mix. The emerging pattern points to a layered liquidity stack that will enable instant redemptions and 24/7 trading of tokenized securities, accelerating institutional adoption of on‑chain asset classes. 3. Circle’s Expansion Beyond USDC with the Arc Blockchain Circle raised $222 million in a presale that values its new Arc layer‑1 at $3 billion, positioning the network as an institution‑focused rail for stablecoins and tokenized assets. The funding round attracted a16z crypto, BlackRock and ARK Invest, signaling confidence that Circle can compete in the broader blockchain infrastructure space. Arc is likely to become a preferred settlement layer for regulated stablecoin issuers and tokenized fund managers seeking compliance‑ready throughput. 4. U.S. Legislative Momentum Provides Stablecoin Clarity The Senate Banking Committee advanced the Digital Asset Market Clarity Act to the full Senate floor with a bipartisan 15‑9 vote, creating a clear regulatory framework for dollar‑stablecoins and tokenized securities. The bill’s passage will reduce legal uncertainty for issuers and could prompt European regulators to align MiCA provisions with U.S. standards. Anticipate a surge in stablecoin issuance and cross‑border tokenized fund structures as firms position for a harmonized global regime. 5. Traditional Finance Deepening Direct Crypto Exposure Hana Bank’s $670 million purchase of a 6.55 % stake in Dunamu, KDDI’s $65 million acquisition of a 14.9 % share in Coincheck, and SpaceX’s planned $75 billion IPO that will bring a sizable Bitcoin treasury to public markets illustrate a rapid convergence of legacy finance and crypto. These moves provide established distribution channels for stablecoins, tokenized assets and on‑chain payments, and they signal that large banks and telecoms see crypto as a core component of future financial services. Expect accelerated rollout of won‑pegged stablecoins, blockchain remittance products and tokenized securities in Asia and beyond.
