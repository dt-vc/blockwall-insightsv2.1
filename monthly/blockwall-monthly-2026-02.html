📊 Month in Review

The defining story of February was not about crypto at all. While our sector grappled with regulatory headwinds around Binance's compliance failures and the usual compliance theater from Washington, the real capital migration happened elsewhere. ElevenLabs, a voice AI company founded just four years ago, raised $500 million at an $11 billion valuation from Sequoia Capital, tripling its worth in a single year. This moment crystallizes a brutal truth for European venture capital: the best founders are solving problems that transcend blockchain entirely, and the smartest capital is following them there. For Blockwall, this serves as a clarifying signal about where genuine innovation capital flows when regulatory uncertainty clouds one sector. The question for February is not what happened in crypto, but what it means that the most compelling venture story of the month had nothing to do with us.

The capital flows tell a story of bifurcation. ElevenLabs attracted $500 million from Sequoia, with Andreessen Horowitz quadrupling its position and ICONIQ tripling theirs, signaling conviction in AI infrastructure at scale. Meanwhile, in our sector, we saw the usual pattern: regulatory pressure creating defensive positioning rather than offensive expansion. Morgan Stanley's filing for a national trust bank charter to custody digital assets represents genuine institutional progress, yet it arrived alongside multiple Senate investigations into Binance's sanctions compliance and allegations of $1.7 billion in illicit flows. The contrast is instructive. Traditional finance is building custody infrastructure methodically, while crypto platforms face existential compliance questions. For founders in our portfolio, this environment demands clarity: the winners will be those building compliant infrastructure that institutions can actually use, not those betting on regulatory arbitrage.

The infrastructure layer remains where Blockwall's conviction lies. CME Group's announcement of 24/7 crypto futures and options trading launching May 29 represents genuine progress toward market maturity, as does STS Digital's $30 million strategic round to scale institutional derivatives platforms. These developments matter because they address real friction points for institutional adoption. Yet they also highlight what our portfolio companies must deliver: seamless integration with traditional finance rails. Our investments in FINEXITY, Unstoppable Finance, and bloXmove are positioned precisely here, at the intersection of tokenization and institutional infrastructure. The builders who understand that crypto's future depends on interoperability with traditional systems, not replacement of them, will capture disproportionate value in the next cycle.

Looking ahead to Q2, expect regulatory pressure on crypto platforms to intensify while institutional infrastructure accelerates. The Binance investigations will likely produce enforcement actions that reshape compliance standards across the industry. Simultaneously, Morgan Stanley's move signals that traditional finance is moving from skepticism to integration. For Blockwall, this creates a clear thesis: fund the infrastructure that bridges these worlds. The ElevenLabs moment reminds us that capital follows genuine innovation, and genuine innovation in payments and DeFi infrastructure now requires regulatory sophistication as much as technical brilliance. Founders should prepare for a market that rewards compliance-first builders and punishes those treating regulation as an afterthought. The next twelve months will separate the serious infrastructure plays from the speculation.

🎯 Key Themes of February

Institutional Capital Blurs Crypto AI Boundaries
ElevenLabs raised $500 million in Series D funding at an $11 billion valuation led by Sequoia while Paradigm launched a $1.5 billion fund targeting AI robotics alongside crypto investments and Rowspace secured $50 million from Sequoia for AI-driven financial data tools. Bitwise's acquisition of $2.2 billion staking firm Chorus One further entrenched traditional asset managers in Web3 yield generation. We believe this convergence signals crypto VCs pivoting to AI as the new high-conviction bet with Web3 infrastructure providing the scalable backbone for agentic economies. Over the next 3-6 months expect accelerated fund deployments into hybrid AI-blockchain protocols especially in Europe where MiCA clarity accelerates tokenized AI compute markets. Builders should prioritize interoperable L1s like Solana for low-latency AI agents while investors allocate 20-30% of portfolios to these frontier funds to capture outsized returns from tokenization of AI models.
Regulatory Scrutiny Targets Exchanges Sparking Custody Race
Eleven US senators urged probes into Binance over $1 billion in alleged Iran flows and $1.7 billion tied to terrorist groups while a senator investigated firings of compliance investigators. Morgan Stanley filed for a national trust bank charter to custody digital assets and offer staking directly countering centralized exchange risks. US DOJ seized $580 million from Chinese criminals underscoring enforcement momentum. This theme marks an inflection where US regulation forces a shift from opaque CEX dominance to regulated custody solutions vital for Web3's institutional layer. In the next 3-6 months MiCA-compliant European platforms will gain primacy as safe havens pulling liquidity from Binance amid escalating probes. Builders must embed KYC-native smart contracts in DeFi protocols and investors should overweight TradFi entrants like Morgan Stanley for compliant yield plays bridging to tokenized RWAs.
TradFi Infrastructure Powers 24/7 Crypto Derivatives Boom
STS Digital raised $30 million from Kraken and Fidelity to scale institutional crypto derivatives while CME Group launched 24/7 futures and options trading on May 29 amid record volumes. Stripe warned blockchains must hit 1 billion TPS for AI agent commerce and a Pantera-backed Solana firm built APAC staking networks linking Seoul Tokyo Singapore and Hong Kong. These moves solidify high-throughput infrastructure as Web3's killer app for global finance. Strategically this unlocks tokenized derivatives markets tying DeFi to real-world volatility hedging with European MiCA frameworks enabling cross-border clearing. Expect 3-6 month surges in L2 adoption for derivatives as AI agents drive micropayments via protocols like x402. Investors target infrastructure tokens like SOL while builders focus on scalable rollups to tokenize TradFi products.
AI Revolutionizes Web3 Security and Auditing Protocols
AI tools averted a critical XRP Ledger flaw patched emergently uncovered a high-severity Ethereum bug affecting 40% of validators and powered audits exposing Solana platform insider trading. South Korea's tax office leaked a seed phrase losing $4.8 million in tokens highlighting custody gaps AI can bridge. This represents a strategic pivot where AI becomes Web3's defensive moat enabling trustless scaling for DeFi and tokenization. We believe AI-native security will reduce exploit losses by 50% in coming quarters signaling maturity for institutional onramps. Over 3-6 months Ethereum's Hegota fork with smart accounts will integrate AI auditing as standard tying into MiCA's risk disclosure rules. Builders embed AI oracles in protocols like Aave which hit $1 trillion lending volume and investors back security DAOs for asymmetric upside in a hack-prone ecosystem.
Bitcoin ETF Volatility Reveals Maturing Accumulation Cycle
Spot Bitcoin ETFs saw $1 billion inflows in three days led by BlackRock alongside Abu Dhabi funds buying $1 billion dips yet posted $3.8 billion in five-week outflows amid $771 million ETH dumps and $220 million trader losses. BTC futures open interest shed 45% from peaks indicating orderly deleveraging not capitulation. This pattern confirms ETFs as stabilizing forces channeling sovereign wealth into Web3 despite volatility underscoring Bitcoin's role as digital gold. Strategically it accelerates tokenization narratives with European ETPs expanding to DeFi yields under MiCA. In 3-6 months renewed inflows will coincide with rate cuts boosting L1 valuations. Investors position in ETF-adjacent staking like BlackRock's ETH buys while builders tokenize ETF-like products on-chain for retail access.
